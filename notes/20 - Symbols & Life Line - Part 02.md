# 2. Signs & Symbols (continued)

## 2.1 Triangle

- Seen **mostly on the Life Line and the luck line (fate line / bhāgya rekhā)**.
- A **very good sign — the bigger the triangle, the better.** Called a **rare sign**; the teacher had seen it in only one or two hands. Example: **Arvind ji's hand** (seen the day before) had a very clear triangle.
- **Where:** anywhere on the Life Line — inside or outside. (Placement to be taught with the Life Line.)
- **Meaning — at the age/period where it appears:**
  1. **Progress** in that time period of life (example: if the period works out to be between 30 and 40, life will progress and be better than before).
  2. **Property**, a **fixed asset**, or a **major investment** you have made.

> [!INFO] Second triangle (formed by lines in the palm)
> - Formed by the **Life Line, Fate Line, Mind Line, and the Mercury line** together (a triangle in the middle of the palm — the area of **Rāhu**).
> - Also called very good; **good money-wise**.
> - **Conditions:** the cleaner the better. If an **island** or **many lines** fall inside it, it is **not called as good**.
> - **Colour condition:** in the middle (Rāhu area) there should not be much **blue or black** colour; **yellow or light blue** is also **not called very good**.

## 2.2 Trident (triśūla)

- Seen **at the end of a line** — so you must know which line it is and where its end is.
  - On the **fate line** the end is **not** a trident: there, a bunch/cluster is seen in most hands.
- **Shape:** a straight line with this form formed separately on top of it (three prongs).
> [!WARNING] What is not a trident
> If a line (e.g., the Sun line) goes along and **a line just turns off from it or a branch emerges from it**, that is **not a trident** — it is only the line turned/its branch. A true trident is a separate formation (it may look in several ways; all those are taken as trident).
- **Effect:** forms at the end of a line and **increases that line's quality** — e.g., on the **Sun line**, the results from the Sun line come **increased**. A **very good but rare** sign; teacher: not in her own hand.

If it is **right below the Sun finger**, then it is accepted.

## 2.3 Net / grille (jālī)

- A lattice of crossing lines.
- **Where found:** **most on the Venus Mount** ("filled with a net, in everyone's Venus"); also seen on the Mercury mount.
- **Conditions to count it as a jālī:**
  1. **Vertical and horizontal lines must look alike** — clear, equally visible. If the vertical lines are dark and the horizontal ones faint, it is **not counted** (it will not give so bad a result).
  2. **At least three** standing (vertical) lines **and three** sleeping (horizontal) lines.
- **Effect:** a jālī is **always bad** — increases trouble and is called a cause of **misfortune (durbhāgya)**.
  - Since **Venus shows married life and family life**: trouble in **married life, family life, child birth**, and **a great deal of anxiety** in the person.
  - **General rule:** the more cuts, crosses, nets, lines on Venus → the more trouble there.

- **Meaning:** **power, name, fame, good spiritual growth**; it **makes you a researcher**. The first quality of a fish: it does **not** give money. You may go **very far in the spiritual field** and become a good researcher.
- Not an island: it looks **different** and is generally seen on the Ketu area.

# 4. Life Line – Continued

## 4.1 Ambition line

- **Definition:** a line rising **from the Life Line toward the Jupiter Mount**; it can **rise from anywhere** on the Life Line (no fixed point). Called the **Ambition line**.
- Lines keep forming (especially in the **active hand**), so it need not have been there since childhood.
- **If present from childhood:** from childhood the person has some **desire/ambition** they want to fulfil, a big way of thinking.
  - **Analogy (Teacher):** in classes 6th–8th some children have no aim ("whatever parents say"), while others think big from childhood — "I want to do something big in life".
- The desire is **good** — it goes toward Jupiter. **Family support** may also be obtained: the **Life Line shows the father/father figure** and the **Venus Mount represents family**.
- Teacher: it should be in students' hands "for sure" — it is in hers.
- A student asked if it can be called **goal-oriented** — **Teacher: yes, absolutely.**

## 4.4 Supportive line vs parasite line (restated, with additions)

| | Supportive line | Parasite line |
|---|---|---|
| Position | Runs **inside**, at a **little distance** from the Life Line — **not stuck to it** | **Very fine line right next to the Life Line**, very thin, **light colour**, running alongside |
| Meaning | Trouble came (e.g., a break in the Life Line), **but someone supported you** during that time period | "Parasite" = one who **hollows you from inside**: a person/situation that **keeps troubling you internally** |
| Duration | As far as the line runs | **Responsibility stays with you as far as the parasite line runs** — "until death" if it runs to the end; you feel a **heavy burden** |

- **Examples of a parasite situation (Teacher):** having to give money to someone **every month** as help; a **divorced sister/daughter living permanently at home** — you cannot leave her but must look after her.
- **Inward vs outward parasite line:**
  - **Inside (inward)** → puts a lot of **pressure on the mind**; usually someone from **home/family**.
  - **Outside (from the outer side)** → **outer circumstances**: **career**, **neighbours** (e.g., having taken a house and quarrels daily), or a **house loan** paid with great difficulty.
- **Combination with property:** is a house being built? That must also show: **a triangle or a square** shows property; then if a **parasite line also runs inside**, property is made but with a burden (e.g., loan). Every hand differs — "no two hands are same".

## 4.5 Island (additional points)

- **Where:** on **lines only, not on mounts** (confirmed again to Harishchandra ji).
- Can be very large or so small it can't be recognised. **Islands are seen most on the Heart Line and Mind Line**, because the effect of trouble is greatest on **heart and mind**; sometimes you will not know it is an island but you will see the **patch**.
- **Very tiny island:** do not mistake for a **triangle**. Some are fine lines with a slight gap in the middle "as if see-through" — you cannot recognise those with the hand.
- **Rule: a small island → trouble for a short time; a big island → trouble for a long time.**
- **How the person takes it depends on the Mind Line** (taught later):
  - A **straight, good, light (not very dark) Mind Line** → the person can take the island **positively**. The island still troubles, but the trouble becomes part of life — "this is what living means". *Analogies (Teacher):* a home with only a fan believes "only a fan can give air"; a person thinking "no job above ₹20,000, so life must be lived on ₹20,000".
  - A **wavy Mind Line** (up and down) → can show **depression**; so does a Mind Line that **dips down** at a place ("going into a depressive state"); the **Heart Line** may also look wavy. Any trouble's impact falls on heart and mind.
- **Hand type also counts:** a **watery hand** with a wavy Mind Line and an island is already **emotional** and gets upset quickly; an **earthy hand** is **practical** and is less affected however much trouble comes. "**Do not forget everything taught earlier.**"

## 4.6 Triangle on the Life Line – detailed

- **Where:** anywhere — outside or inside; **big or small**.
- **Example:** Arvind ji's hand has a very big triangle on the outer side, touching the Fate Line *(he said it touches the Mind Line and lies between the Mind, Fate and Life Lines; **Teacher: no — it touches the Fate Line; if it touched the Mind Line it would be higher up**; to be checked by placing a hand)*.
- **Reading as asset:** a triangle = a **self-made asset** — property, a major investment. Whether **outside or inside** the Life Line, it is an **asset**.
  - **The bigger the triangle, the bigger the asset** — and also **the longer it takes to build** the property (e.g., the loan to be repaid, or time to move in/start work there). Keep **deśa-kāla-paristhiti** in mind.
- **Inner-side triangle** → becomes a **liability**: needs maintaining; **more stress than the outer** one. *Examples:* a loan taken for a property; a property **far from home** that must be visited and looked after; a **boundary wall** and cleaning cost a lot.
- **Triangle joined to the Fate Line (third condition):**
  - e.g., a line leaves the Fate Line and joins the Life Line, forming a triangle.
  - Several ways to read; depends on **the client's question**. It can be taken as a triangle, **and** the Life Line and Fate Line have **different origins**, so **two different ages** result: for **career**, use the **Fate Line's age**; for **one's own age**, use the Life Line (to be taught with the Heart Line).
  - A line going **outward** at that age → something **from outside** (not necessarily abroad; **shifting out of where you lived**, or e.g. **a second shop**, **online business**) → **expansion in career**, because it is joined to the Fate Line. We see career mostly **after 15–20 years of age**.
  - **Family support:** wherever the Fate Line and Life Line **touch** or are **connected** (even if one runs at a distance but connects by a line) → **your work progresses with family support** — a job placed by an acquaintance, a **father's support**, starting work on **ancestral land**; "a thousand ways". *Teacher's own example:* her Fate and Life Lines join, and her father has always supported her in her job.

## 4.7 Square on the Life Line

- Can form **outside, inside**, or other positions: it is a **protective shield** (surakṣā kavaca): in that time, however big the trouble, you **get out of it**. Also **property** indicated.
- **Inner-side square** → some consider it **bandhana yoga** ("jail yoga") — like **bandhana yoga in astrology** (the teacher also mentioned a numerology "9454" combination called bandhana yoga — *unclear*) **[unclear in transcript]**.
  - **Not called bad**, because it forms through a square. It means you are **tied/bound by some work or circumstance**.
  - *Examples:* a person with a 9-to-6 job who cannot easily take leave when children/wife want a week's trip — he **cannot compromise as much as the rest of the family**; the **Corona period** was a bondage for everyone (good for some, bad for others).
  - Whether it is truly "jail yoga" is checked from the **Sun line and Mercury line** too (to be taught). Meaning: you are **stuck in a situation and cannot get out**.
  - **Where else to look:** if due to **career** → a negative sign on the **career line**; due to **health** → possibly the **Mercury line**; **mental stress** → **Heart Line or Mind Line**.

## 4.8 Cross on the Life Line

- **Not** a cross: a line's own **path** (e.g., the Mercury line may start anywhere — mid-palm, or through the Venus Mount). **Teacher:** "like the course of a river — you don't call it bad." A cross is **seen separately on a line**, with its own crossing on top.
- **Meaning:** wherever a cross comes — a **trouble/disaster in life** at that time. **What follows depends on the condition of the Life Line afterwards**: good later line → good life; otherwise bad.

## 4.9 Chains at the start; dark lines

- **Chains** are **seen where the Life Line starts** (mostly in everyone). Two things: a **cluster of lines**, or **chains**.
  - **Not called islands** because many chains run together.
  - **Meaning:** **health issues**; and at **ages 10, 12, 15** everyone has a little struggle — the **burden of studies**, children kept away from family — **hostel**.
- **Line going outward from this start (as in a hand seen last class — Harishchandra ji or someone):** *outward = expansion* → expansion for **study at a small age**: e.g., tuition when other children in the family did not; **online** study; **hostel**.
- **If chains appear repeatedly beyond the start**, **health issues will continue throughout life.**
- **Bleeding line:** a **very dark** line. Another way to see health. In the hand shown, the Life Line is a bleeding line. A bleeding line gives **trouble in life**: **not a smooth life**. The teacher would **not** say "problem with food, clothes, shelter", but **no comfortable life** — "every day your life will be like a challenge".

# 5. Age Calculation on the Life Line (first lesson)

> [!NOTE] Teaching method
> The teacher said she needed software (Canva/whiteboard) to demonstrate but **the full hand picture was not available**; she also showed the **traditional paper method**. **She will teach it again tomorrow with practice.**

> [!IMPORTANT] Steps (Life Line)
> 1. Draw/take the Life Line **from its starting point to where it ends** (it may also turn inward). *Traditional method:* place a **thin strip of paper** along the line; mark the full length on the paper.
> 2. **Halve** the length: **fold the paper in half**, then lay it back and mark where the half falls on the line.
> 3. From that half-point, draw a **line to the Mercury line side (toward the Mercury finger)** *(teacher: "from Mercury line I'll bring a line to here")* **[exact direction unclear in transcript]**.
> 4. **Start → half-point = age 30.** This is **fixed**: **age 30 for the Life Line and also for the Fate Line.** *"Whatever the person's actual age, I take 30 up to here."*
> 5. **After 30**, take the **average life span** of the country/continent (**deśa-kāla-paristhiti**): **here, 75** (not more than 75–80). So the second half runs **30 → 75**.
> 6. **Halve again:** 30 to 75 = **45 years**; half = **22.5**. The mid-point = **30 + 22.5 = 52.5** years.
> 7. **Keep halving:** halve **30 → 15** (start to the first half = 0–15; "this shows 15 to 30" for the next part). *i.e.*, the start-to-half is divided into 0–15 and 15–30.
> - Teacher: "this is only the beginning; with practice you will get an estimate."

> - **Outcome:** **Arvind ji: yes, made it after 35.** (Later: "30, 35 years later the property was bought **for rent**".) Teacher: the event joins to the triangle.
