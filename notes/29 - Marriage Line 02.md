## 1. Names, location and recap of the Marriage Line

> [!INFO] Names of the line
> - **vivāha rekhā (Marriage Line)**
> - **Line of Union** (the teacher said the student will meet this name in books; she forgot to mention it last class)
> - **śīla-guṇa rekhā** (character-quality line)
> - Union Line

> [!INFO] Location
> Lines found **between the heart line and the saṃdhi rekhā (joint crease) of the last phalange of the Mercury finger**, on the budha parvata (Mercury Mount). A short line seen there is the marriage line.

- In the previous class roughly **15–20 combinations** were covered. A few remaining ones are done today.
- The age scale used for this line: **0 to 50** (the teacher recalled this later in the class when discussing lines above and below the marriage line).

## 2. Remaining marriage-line combinations (in order taught)

### 2.1 Marriage line very close to the heart line
- When the age is calculated for a marriage line running very close to the heart line, the heart line position comes to **25 years**; the teacher then took **half of 25 ≈ 13 years**. [unclear in transcript — how the half-of-25 figure was derived]
- So this is a **very young age**: either a relationship or an attraction toward someone at a young age, **or** an early marriage (where marriages in young age happen).
- *Speaker emphasized:* judge by **deśa-kāla-paristhiti** (place, time, circumstance).
- If such a line is seen around **15–20 years** (or before), **check the life line at this age**.
- A **star on the Mars Mount** (especially in a **female** hand) can indicate a **teenage relationship**.
- Add-ons that stack into more combinations: an **influence line** coming from there; or, even without an influence line, a **star + a small line + disturbance in the lines** nearby → relationship in teen age.

### 2.2 Marriage line turning down and ending on the Venus Mount → divorce
> [!IMPORTANT] Do not confuse with the previous class
> - **Previous class:** a line from the Venus Mount came and **cut** the marriage line (crossed it).
> - **This class:** the **marriage line itself bends down and stops on the Venus Mount.**

- This is a **divorce combination**.
- If seen in **both hands**, one can say the divorce was **pre-decided** — the person's intention/mindset from the beginning was to take a divorce. (Teacher: sometimes a situation arises suddenly; sometimes the person had decided from the start.)
- **Shape tells the nature of the problem:**

| Shape of the marriage line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Runs straight, then **suddenly drops down** | **Sudden problem** |
| Curves down **gradually** (slowly, slowly bending) | Problem that is **continuing and building gradually** |

### 2.3 Vertical lines on the marriage line
- Vertical lines that **cut** the marriage line (cross it) → **trouble**.
- Vertical lines going **upward** from it → **children lines**. (The teacher will discuss these properly in the **children chapter**.)
- Reading given:
  - **Darker** line → **boy**; **lighter** line → **girl** (birth of a girl).
  - **More than one** line → can show **more than one miscarriage / abortion**.
  - Further indications of this will also be found on the **family ring** and around the **thumb**; mentioned here since the point came up.
- **Marks on the child lines:** a **cut/cross** or **island** on them → trouble in the **child's health** — a health issue from birth, or a **stillborn child**.

### 2.4 Many lines around the marriage line (light and dark)
- Check whether the extra lines are **below**, **above**, or on **both sides** of the marriage line. (Age scale used: 0–50.)

| Position of extra lines | Reading |
|---|---|
| **Below** | Relationships **before marriage** |
| **Above** | Enjoyment/relationships continue **after marriage** |
| **Both sides** | Both **before and after** marriage |

> [!EXAMPLE] Anecdote used by the teacher
> **Who:** A man (unnamed) she once met. **Hand:** not specified. **Observed:** lines of this kind around the marriage line. **Interpretation:** he said he had always "enjoyed life greatly" (without direct words); his hands showed such lines, so one can understand in what sense he enjoyed life. **Reasoning:** lines around the marriage line = relationships. **Outcome:** his own statement agreed with the reading.

- If you don't want to read it harshly, you can say the person's **inclination (jhukāv)** may be like that — comparable to a chart where someone has the **Moon in the 7th house**: inclination in that direction.

### 2.5 Island on the marriage line
- Island at the **start** → problem **before marriage**: trouble during engagement, sudden problems, marriage almost happening but breaking; difficulties from family or anyone else.
- Island in the **middle** → problem in the **middle age of married life**.
- Island at the **end** → problem in the **last** phase.
- (Rule of thumb: island at start = problem at start; middle = middle; last = last.)

### 2.6 Marriage line cutting the Sun line → intercaste marriage
- The teacher said there are other intercaste combinations too; this one: the **marriage line cuts across the Sun line**.
- Reading: **intercaste marriage** and a **loss of mān-sammān (honour/respect)** for the person with this hand.
- *Reasoning:* the marriage line is cutting the Sun line (the line of honour), hence reduced respect and intercaste marriage.

### 2.7 Mole (til) on the marriage line or the Mercury Mount
- Already taught: a mole does not indicate a good event.
- A mole **on the marriage line** can form a **widowhood yoga** (transcript word "window"; read as widow). [unclear in transcript]
- A mole on the marriage line **or on the Mercury Mount** is **not** considered good.

### 2.8 Marriage line extending to the Mind Line → different religion
- Previous class: the line sloping toward the heart line was covered.
- Today: the marriage line **crosses the heart line and goes all the way to the Mind (Head) Line** — touches/joins it, **or a branch from it reaches the head line**.
- Reading: **partners of different religions** (marriage between different religions).
- Also a **divorce combination**. Trouble comes **after a child is born**.
- *Reasoning:* upbringing of the child — the wife says "we'll raise the child by my religion", the husband says "by mine"; disputes about raising the child lead to divorce.

### 2.9 Cross, star, and island marks on drooping/straight lines
- **Cross on a drooping marriage line** → not good.
- **Cross at the end of a straight marriage line** → not good (taught earlier).
- **Star** → also not good.
- All of these spoil the line.

### 2.10 Second line starting above the marriage line → second marriage
- Marriage line goes along, ends, and **another line starts above it** and goes upward → combination of a **second marriage**.
- *Reasoning:* if it started from below, it would be the first marriage. The first marriage exists, but disturbances begin and the person makes up their mind for a second marriage; hence the second line appears above.
- Seen only in some hands; observe.

### 2.11 Fork at the end of the marriage line
- Fork with two branches at the end, together with a line from the Venus Mount (life line side) cutting across it.
- Reading: people around — **family/household circumstances** — become a cause of trouble in married life.
- A fork by itself is **not bad**; the line **cutting** it is **not good**.

### 2.12 Square and gaps among the lines around the marriage line
> [!EXAMPLE] Hand shown in class
> **Who:** A hand put forward during the class (a student said *Dave ji* was about to give someone's hand; the teacher asked "which hand?" — **right**). **Hand:** right. **Observed:** lines around the marriage line, a **gap** in the middle, lines going up and down, and a **square** formed. **Interpretation:** the up/down lines and the gap show **disturbance**; but the square gives **protection** — the worsening married life **recovered/steadied**. **Reasoning:** square = protection sign. **Outcome:** not complete recovery — the bad middle phase has been **removed/passed**.
> In the same moment a student remarked that the hand has a **government-job line**; the teacher acknowledged without comment.

### 2.13 Downward branches from the marriage line

| Downward branch | Reading |
|---|---|
| **Wide distance** between the branch and the main line | **Can cause divorce** |
| Branch very close, only a **faint small** one | Situation will be bad, but **divorce is not necessary** |

*Reasoning:* the distance between the two is small in the second case.

## 3. Yogas for a good married life

> [!TIP] Teacher's method
> Books list yogas. Just as yogas form in jyotiṣa, **yogas can be formed in the hand**. The teacher asked the class to build the yoga themselves: *"What should we look at, in any hand, for a good married life?"*

**Student answers and the teacher's elaboration**

1. **Venus (śukra) good** — student's first answer.
2. **Jupiter (guru) Mount** — teacher prompted ("look at Guru").
   - *Taught at the start of the course:* a **cross on Jupiter**, especially in a **female hand**, increases the chances of **recovery** in married life **even if the marriage line looks very bad**.
3. Order stated by the teacher: **first Jupiter good, then Venus good.**
4. **Venus:** see it **more in the male hand**.
   - *Reasoning:* in a female, Venus shows family circumstances too; a female may "get through" it once. But when **child-birth** time comes, see the **male hand's Venus condition**.
5. **Quality of the hand.**
6. **Fingers:** good, beautiful, **long**; especially the **Jupiter finger**. *The more beautiful the fingers, especially Jupiter's, the better the married life.*
7. **Fate line in the male hand** (raised by a student): career is necessary for marriage; without career there are disputes.
   - Teacher agreed: many hands have a good marriage line, everything fine, but **no career** → marriages are **not happening**. **Always see the fate line.**

### 3.1 Satī Yoga
> [!INFO] Satī yoga (as described)
> Conditions together:
> - **Jupiter Mount good**
> - **Fingers long and fine/beautiful** (teacher corrected herself: not "thin" — **long**)
> - **Soft hand**, like a **female hand**
> - **No crooked fingers**, especially the **Jupiter finger**
> - **Venus Mount in good condition**

## 4. Love marriage vs arranged marriage

> [!NOTE] Teacher's framing
> Teacher asked Anurag to give a combination for love or arranged; she then gave several. "If it is not love, it will be arranged." Giving any one combination is acceptable.

### 4.1 Hand type (quality of hand)
- **Fiery hand** → more chances of love marriage.
- **Watery hand** → more chances of love marriage.
- Earthy and airy hands **can also** have love marriages; it is not that they never do. But in a study, **fire and water** show higher chances.
- Nowadays most young people have **fiery or watery** hands; earthy and airy are fewer.

### 4.2 Heart Line
| Heart line | Reading |
|---|---|
| Ends (straight or curved) **between the two points shown, around the Saturn Mount** | **Fewer chances** of love marriage — such a person has no inclination |
| **Ring of Saturn** (line going around Saturn Mount; the teacher asked a student to write the name) | Person is **very serious**, very **practical**, not attentive to love; also called **"no marriage"** sign. Won't quickly agree to marry → more arranged or none |
| Goes **toward the Jupiter Mount**, **straight** | Some practicality; love marriage chances **not absent** — not blind in love |
| Goes to Jupiter, **very slightly curved**, ending right on it | Becomes "blind in love"; **emotional**. Still weighs long-term suitability — somewhat calculating. [partly unclear in transcript] |

- Always also check the **condition** of the heart line and of the other lines.
- **Influence line** coming onto the **fate line at any age** → also an indication of love marriage.

> [!EXAMPLE] Student's own hand (Ring of Saturn question)
> **Who:** Anurag. **Hand:** left. **Observed:** a line that dips slightly downward there. **Interpretation given:** it may be the **saṃdhi rekhā** (joint crease) itself curving down — that is a different line; a Ring of Saturn is seen as a separate line formed in the other area. **Outcome:** not concluded as Ring of Saturn.

### 4.3 Jupiter Mount marks
- **Cross on Jupiter**: seen generally; it is **not an indication of love marriage**, but **if a love marriage happens, it will be a good one**; if one is in love, chances of **converting to marriage** are higher.
- **Svastika** (the teacher: "satiya", swastika) on Jupiter → also favourable in the same way. [sentence trailed off in transcript]

### 4.4 Mars Mount (especially female hand)
- See **lower Mars** (not upper Mars) in a female hand.
- For a **rebellious** hand — left home for love, ran away — look at the **outer Mars**. [terminology: "lower Mars" and "outer Mars" both used; see note below]

> [!WARNING] Terminology note
> Teacher said "lower Mars, not upper Mars" for the general reading, then "outer Mars" for the rebellious case. Not clarified whether these are the same mount region.

### 4.5 Other checks for a rebellious/love-marriage hand
- **Sun finger** — the person who does not listen to anyone. [a phrase about "country" is unclear in transcript]
- **Thumb** — see **will power**.
- **Mind line and life line gap:**
  - **Big gap** between the life line and mind line → **open-minded**.
  - Many hands have a gap but **a line joining** it — **ignore** that (leave it).
  - Check together with the **thumb quality**, **Sun finger**, and **Mars Mount**.
- A **star** (at the location indicated) → inclination in **early age** toward boyfriend/girlfriend, relationship, living-in.

*Teacher closed this: "Mind line is done; mostly this much will come."*

## 5. Disturbed married life

- Check **Venus Mount for moles** — a mole must not be there.

> [!EXAMPLE] A male acquaintance of the teacher
> **Who:** A man (unnamed), over **30**, **not yet married**. **Hand:** not specified. **Observed:** about **two or three moles** — one probably on the **Sun finger**, one on **Jupiter**, one somewhere on **Venus**. **Interpretation:** results from all three are not good; **mole on Venus is very bad anyway**; marriage is **late** (in his surroundings marriage usually happens at 27–28). **Reasoning:** moles give bad results.

- **Venus Mount:** many lines or a **jālī (grille)** should not be there.
  - *Definition:* a grille = **at least 3 vertical and 3 horizontal lines**, and all must be of the **same darkness**. If one is light and one dark, it is not a grille. (Otherwise every Venus Mount is full of lines.)
  - Meaning: **anxiety**; Venus covers relationships and family, so **up-and-down** movement in life over these matters.
- **Thumb:**
  - **First phalange** = will power.
  - **Second phalange**: visible in some, not in others; **the more lines there**, the **more trouble in married life**.
- **Family ring:** the line at the bottom of the Venus Mount joining near the thumb (last line meeting at the end of the Venus Mount).
  - It must have a **chain**. Chain can be **one, two, or more**.
  - **No chain** → **not good** for married life; **chain present** → fine; chain shows **emotional connection**.
  - To be studied again for child birth.
- **Jupiter Mount:**
  - **Grille** → bad; **cross** → fine here; any **cut mark** → bad for married life.
  - Check for a **mole** or any **dark mark**.
- **Jupiter finger crooked** → **direct trouble in married life**; in the chart too, **Jupiter appears afflicted**.
- *Speaker emphasized repeatedly:* Wherever **blackness/darkness** shows up in the hand, **that area is bad** — in any area; also a **change of colour** (e.g. whole hand pink and one place yellowish). The teacher said these tiny details must be watched, because there is **no chart here** to "see 6, 8, 12 quickly".

> [!CAUTION] Mole rule — repeatedly stated
> A mole is bad in **all cases** (male or female) and **always gives bad results**. The teacher said this repeatedly in the Q&A.

## 9. Summary

> [!SUMMARY] What this class established
> - **Marriage line:** position, names; age scale 0–50; very close to heart line = very young age; down-turn to Venus = divorce (both hands = decided); sudden vs gradual bend; vertical lines = trouble or children (dark = boy, light = girl); marks on children lines = child's health; extra lines below/above = relationships before/after marriage; island position = problem timing; cutting Sun line = intercaste and loss of respect; mole = bad; extension to Mind Line = different religion/divorce after child; second line above = second marriage; square = protection; far downward branch = divorce possible, near one = not necessarily.
> - **Good married life yoga:** Jupiter good → Venus good (male hand mainly) → hand quality → long beautiful fingers (Jupiter) → fate line in male hand; **Satī yoga** = good Jupiter Mount + long fine fingers + soft hand + straight Jupiter finger + good Venus Mount.
> - **Love marriage:** fiery/watery hand; heart line ending under Saturn or Ring of Saturn reduces it; heart line to Jupiter increases; influence line to fate line; Jupiter cross = good outcome if it happens; lower/outer Mars; Sun finger; thumb; gap between life and mind lines.
> - **Disturbed married life:** moles (Venus, Jupiter, Sun finger), Venus grille, lines on thumb's second phalange, no chain in family ring, Jupiter grille/cuts/moles, crooked Jupiter finger, dark/discoloured areas.
