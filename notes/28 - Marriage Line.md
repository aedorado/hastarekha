# 1. Introduction to the Marriage Line

> [!INFO] vivāha rekhā (Marriage Line)
> - Located on the **Mercury mount**.
> - It **enters from the outside towards the inside** of the hand (comes from the outer edge inward). It will look very small.

# 2. Student Questions

## 2.1 Is the finger or the mount more influential? 

- **Q :** Does the finger have more effect or the mount? If the mount, roughly what percentage?
- **Teacher:**
  - Ideally the **whole hand** must be seen; the whole hand has the greatest influence.
  - Between the two, the **mount's influence can be taken as slightly more than the finger's**. No percentage was given.
  - Reasoning: it is believed in palmistry that the energy of the **navagrahas (nine planets)** is received through the fingertips and then **collects in the mounts**. Energy arrives and stops in the mounts.
  - Therefore the **mount's condition should be better than the finger's**.

## 2.2 Finger tilts: Jupiter ↔ Saturn 

- **Q:** If the Saturn finger tilts towards Jupiter, or the Jupiter finger tilts towards Saturn, does the prediction change? **A:** Yes, definitely.

> [!TIP] Reading principle
> Read a tilt like the **yuti (conjunction) of planets**: whichever planet's finger moves towards the other, read the two planets' significations together.

| Tilt | Teacher's reading | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **Jupiter finger tilted towards Saturn** | Research enters the person's knowledge; wants to take knowledge slowly, no hurry; will not break rules and regulations; will not force or impose own knowledge on others | Saturn = slow, delay, understanding something in depth; also the kāraka of the **aṣṭama** (eighth) |
| **Saturn finger tilted towards Jupiter** | Uses intelligence/discretion (buddhi-viveka) and knowledge in own work (karma); karma may be like Jupiter's. Saturn gives negativity, but with the tilt, **positivity enters**: even if the person is troubled or the negative impact is large, they find some positive aspect in it | Saturn understood first and directly as **karma**; the karma is moving towards Jupiter |

- **Clarification asked :** "Can we not say negativity will increase?" — **Teacher:** Saturn is negativity; if it is towards Jupiter, the negativity **is not increasing**; the person draws something positive out of it. If a lot has gone wrong for a person, she would still say "fine, whatever happened, it was good" — what happened happened, but how it is taken is positive. This is what a Saturn finger tilted towards Jupiter shows. 

## 2.3 Mount tilting towards a finger; the apex 

- **Q:** A mount shifting towards another finger — what is the prediction?

> [!INFO] Apex (as explained)
> - The apex can **move**: towards the thumb side (slightly outside the mount), towards Saturn, or downward.
> - The apex shows **which planet's predominance** is in the person (e.g. "this person looks Jupiter-predominant, has Jupiter qualities"; another seems to have Mercury-like qualities). Compared with astrology: the way we say "influence on the lagna" or "on the Moon".
> - **Apex exactly in the centre** → that planet's predominance is greater in the person.
> - **All apexes in the centre** → equal influence of all planets, like **ṣaḍbala** where all planets are strong.
> - If an apex has moved off centre, that planet's influence is not as much; **do not read it as predominance**, read it like other predictions.

**Shifted mount (e.g. Jupiter mount towards Saturn):**
- Shifting from its own place is not very good, in itself. *Analogy:* you had a place in the house; when you move away from it you have left your comfortable place, so there will be some difficulty.
- Then apply the **same rule as for fingers**, according to the question asked:
  - **Education question:** Jupiter towards Saturn is good; the person uses knowledge, intellect, discretion in their karma.
  - **Health question:** read as trouble related to Jupiter, and **add other things**. *Example:* for a stomach problem also see the **Rahu mount** (hand center), the **Moon mount**, the **Mercury line**, and whether there is a **negative sign on Jupiter**. Combine all of these; **never predict health from this alone**.
- **Sun towards Mercury:** Sun shows name, fame, creativity; creativity joined with Mercury means the person **commercialised creativity**, started earning from it, started a business.

## 2.4 Main points for predicting nature

> [!TIP] Order of reading nature (teacher's answer)
> 1. **Hand type** first — fiery, earthy, watery, airy. Roughly **60%** of the nature prediction can be done from hand type.
> 2. **Thumb**, especially the **top phalange** — shows will power and decision-making power.
> 3. **Mercury** — communication.
> 4. Then mix in the other details, and the more mounts you add, the better.

> [!EXAMPLE] Combination example given
> - **Who:** hypothetical person. **Hand:** not specified.
> - **Observed:** fiery hand; Jupiter finger tilted towards Saturn; strong first (top) phalange of thumb.
> - **Interpretation:** all fiery-hand qualities apply, but will not break rules and regulations; impatient, but not to the extent of going outside social limits. With a strong will-power phalange, firm in decisions.
> - **Reasoning:** hand type gives the base (60%); finger tilt modifies it; thumb phalange adds decision strength.

## 2.5 Sun finger tilting towards Saturn finger; career question

- **Q:** A question can come in terms of Sun or in terms of Saturn; how to handle both?
- **Teacher:**
  - **Question in terms of Saturn** (career, karma): Sun finger tilting is **very good for career**.
  - **Question in terms of Sun:** some **negativity** — name and fame get slightly delayed, a slight **defame yoga**, and some health problems. All three.
  - **Reverse** (Saturn tilting towards Sun): for a karma question it is very good; could be government-related karma, name and fame coming from one's work.
  - Answer **according to the question asked**.

> [!WARNING] Crooked Saturn finger (for career)
> - Always check first that the **Saturn finger is not crooked (tēḍhī-mēḍhī)**. If it is, it gives trouble in career, or the career may be of a kind where **cleverness** has to be used.
> - **Do not confuse with knotty hand** (knotty joints, which gives a philosophical mindset). The crooked finger is a separate thing.
> - Always see the **Saturn finger, Saturn mount and the whole straight line** (the line running up to Saturn) for career.
> - Career will be taught in full later (including the artist's hand), after this chapter.

> [!EXAMPLE] Career question with Sun finger tilted towards Saturn
> - **Interpretation:** career good, but name and fame come **late**; they will come, with some difficulty.
> - **Reasoning / how much difficulty:** check the **Sun mount** condition.
>   - Good Sun line (Moon and Sun line good) → reduce the delay percentage a little.
>   - Many **Mars lines cutting the Sun line** → the delay/difficulty prediction increases.
> - **Principle:** do not predict from one thing; connect the rest, as in jyotiṣa.

## 2.6 Mount tilted but apex on the mount

- **Q:** Jupiter mount tilted towards Saturn, apex in front on the Jupiter mount — who is predominant?
- **Teacher:**
  - Generally, when the apex has shifted, **the mount shifts too** (its influence goes that way). A mount having moved while the apex stays in place is **very rare**.
  - Predominance stays with wherever the apex is. Here: **Jupiter-predominant** — seriousness, likes giving and taking knowledge, may like sweet food (Jupiter qualities).
  - **Way of working** carries **Saturn's** involvement.
- **Q (follow-up):** If the mount is not puffed but the apex is in the centre?
- **Teacher:** A **puffed (phulā) mount is necessary**, but not excessively puffed — that is bad too (taught earlier). A well-puffed mount sometimes also depends on **daśā** (when a daśā is running, mounts appear fully open). If it is not puffed even in daśā, the mount's condition is poor. **Slightly puffed is necessary**, except Saturn.

## 2.7 Knotty hand 

- **Q:** Confusion about the knotty hand.
- **Teacher:**
  - Seen from the **back of the palm**. The finger's **two joints** (where the joint lines fall) are slightly **raised, like knots (gāṃṭh)**.
  - The teacher's own hand has all fingers like this ("all are knotty") and slightly raised.
  - Knotty hand makes the person a little **philosophical (dārśanik)**. The finger with **more knots** shows that quality in the area of that finger: Jupiter → Jupiter-like philosophy; Saturn finger → it comes in one's **karma**; any finger similarly. It makes the person **philosophical and a researcher**.
  - Can also be seen from the **front**, but the back view shows it clearly.
  - **Not everyone** has it. A knotty hand looks different from a normal hand.

> [!WARNING] Not the same as the crooked finger
> Knotty hand = knotty joints. A finger that goes crooked is *not* a knotty hand.

# 3. Age Measurement on the Life Line

- **Q:** When age is measured by drawing two lines from the Life Line, what if someone's Life Line does not reach there?
- **Teacher (method):**
  1. Take the **exact centre of the maṇibandha (wrist)**.
  2. Place paper (or thread) from there across; take the exact **centre point** — this is the **second major point**.
  3. Connect the two points. This marks **30 years**.
  4. Divide **0–30 years** in this part; divide **30 to 75 (or 77; take 75)** in the rest.
  - She stressed trying it **with paper two–three times**, otherwise it will not be understood.
  - In practice, with a client: **ask about events** to calibrate.
- **Why 75 not 100:**
  - **100** was used in the **saptāṃśa** system taught earlier.
  - Earlier there was the **ṣaṣṭhāṃśa** system, which also used 75.
  - Cheiro "98 plus 2" gave the saptāṃśa system.
  - Now age is taken as **75 or 80**; 100 "according to the country's age".

## Split Life Line

> [!EXAMPLE] Case: Life Line that splits
> - **Who:** the student's practice hand (unnamed). **Hand:** not stated.
> - **Observed:** Life Line runs, becomes faint, then **splits**; one line continues, the other ends a little after the split.
> - **Teacher's interpretation:** **look at both lines.** If one branch goes **outward** and one goes **inward**: in later ages, **two places of movement** — like having two homes (children took a house; you move between it and the main homeland). Makes the person a **multitasker**. It does **not** mean the life ends or two lives; it is **expansion**, connected inside and outside. **Shows travel.**
> - **If one branch ends and the other continues:** the one that continues is read; "it will end somewhere". If the inner ends and the outer continues, it was this way until there, and the person may **shift elsewhere**. **Moon** shows travel, so at **50–60 years** the person may shift, e.g. to the children; the cause could be business, health, or any condition.
> - **Outcome (student's anecdote):** as a child, a pandit told her father she would go very far away; she did leave. Teacher: the line must have been visible to him.
> - Teacher: travel, property, and many health combinations will be taught later; lines and yogas are not complete yet.

# 4. Marriage Line — General Principles

> [!IMPORTANT] Understand marriage through deśa-kāla-paristhiti (place, time and circumstance)
> - The line is called vivāha rekhā, but the conditions of marriage change; one may call it a **relationship line, living-in line**, etc.
> - Per books, it was meant for **mental and physical bonding**; the name "marriage" was given later.
> - **No line** does **not** mean no marriage — there are many other conditions for marriage.
> - **More than one line** does **not** mean two marriages. It may show **attraction** or a **crush**.

> [!QUOTE] Teacher's warning
> "Extramarital affair - We never state an extramarital affair from the hand.

## 4.1 Quality of the line

| Line quality | Teacher's reading |
|---|---|
| **Not visible / very faint** | Not called good. If no line: not "no marriage", but you can say the person is **not fully committed**, **not emotionally connected** to the partner |
| **Very dark / heavy (gaḍhī)** | Shows **old thinking** (about marriage; the transcript also says "late" **[unclear in transcript]**), can make one **rigid** and **controlling**. Not an indication of a happy married life; shows **pressure on married life** — all energy focused on marriage due to some problem (family, caring for someone) |
| **Normal line** (neither too dark nor too light) | Indication of a **good married life** |
| **Light** | Also bad |

- *Old-thinking illustration:* nowadays couples go to parks and restaurants; the person may think "I'll stay at home, eat at home, not go out". Thinking varies from place to place.
- **Hand type changes the reading**: fiery hand — independence is shown; airy hand — slightly doubtful/suspicious (शक्की); "ardi" hand (likely earthy) — long-term relationship **[unclear in transcript]**.
- **Rule:** before marriage, career or health, **first see the client's nature** — many predictions change because of it.

> [!TIP] Dark line: check the Life Line for a parasite line
> For a very dark marriage line, check whether a **parasite line** runs alongside the Life Line in the marriage area. Teacher's example: it may mean **someone ill at home**, or (the earlier example) a sistern in law who is divorced and living with them — small matters then affect married life (nature, career, no money, household trouble).

## 4.2 Age on the Marriage Line

1. Area between the **last phalange joint line (sandhi rekhā) of the Mercury finger** and the **Heart Line**. 0 age considered at heart line and 56 at the start of first phalange of Mercury Finger. 
2. Earlier it was taken as **50 years**; now people take it as **56** (transcript: "fifty six month"). Start from the Heart Line (zero) to the joint line.
3. Teacher said it is a **research subject**, since marriages are getting later and every subject changes by deśa-kāla; the scale needs adjusting.
4. Use it to know the **age of marriage** (and compare with children). The teacher's own marriage line sits **a little below the middle** and she was married at 22/23.

> [!CAUTION] Mars line vs marriage branch
> When a line cuts the Heart Line, check whether it comes **from the Mars mount** or from the marriage line. A **Mars line cutting** is **more dangerous** — it indicates fights, accidents, and more trouble. A branch from the marriage line cutting the Heart Line also disturbs the mind. The marriage line must **not have a cutting connection to the Heart Line** anywhere.

> [!TIP] Rule carried from earlier
> Always read the **condition of the line after any mark** (island, cut, break), as for any line.

> [!NOTE] Female and male cases
> For upward turn/branches, in the **female** case Jupiter mount and Mars are also to be seen; in the **male** case the Venus mount. The teacher will give combinations; the marriage line is not read alone.

## 5.1 Islands

- A large island: the person has taken married life this way from the start and moves on in the same way, thinking "this is how future life will be".
- A **small island**: life was good, then trouble came in the middle, then smooth again — the person **feels the trouble more**. Reason: the person is used to the other, so a **small island is more dangerous than a big one**.
- Islands can **disappear**: the teacher's own **Mercury-line island** vanished once she recovered from illness (the phase/age had passed).

## 5.2 Fork

- Two ways it forms: fork at the **start** or at the **end**; neither is called good.
  - **At the start:** problem at the initial level, **distraction**, since lines go in two directions.
  - **At the end:** **interest in marriage is lost**.
- **Fork from the middle** (one goes straight, one goes up): read as **upward and downward branches**. **Upward is always good; downward is always bad.** (Anuradha ji's question; she said hers goes upward towards Mercury and has no downward branch.)

## 5.3 Line reaching Sun mount

- Rare to be this long. If it reaches the Sun mount, good (name, fame, prosperity after marriage). **Cutting through** the Sun mount: a Mercury line cutting the Sun mount (the earlier combination) means trouble **because of a woman**.
- **Cross-check on the Life Line:** if after the marriage point the Life Line improves (dark, islands before; better after), the marriage brought better conditions. Events always show on the Life Line, whether or not they show elsewhere.

## 5.4 A line cutting the marriage line

![Fig 1. Me Line vs. Line Cutting Marriage Line](/lecture-notes-images/28/01-me-line-vs-line-cutting-marriage-line.png)

- Shows **third-party influence**, of any kind, that disturbed married life — an obstruction, or the person's own mental state.
- **See where the cutting line starts.** Do **not** mistake the **Mercury line** (it naturally runs that way and becomes the **healer line**) for it.
- Ask questions: first the **profession**; if medical, you can judge how much time the person can give; healing lines can be of two–three kinds. Ask **four–five questions** if needed to refine the prediction.

## 5.5 Other reading points

- Line is breaking at start and then continuing normally - Starts troubled, then good/bad phases; If the line is **neither too dark nor too light**, & moving straight with no downward branches, later married life will be good.
- **Two lines:** the longer and **darker/clearer** one is taken as the marriage line; the short, light one is not. Sometimes a light relationship or marriage talks/negotiations breaks midway, then a second marriage — understood by **deśa-kāla**. 

## 5.6. Combination: Line from Venus Mount Cutting the Marriage Line

- A line starting near the **thumb (Venus mount)** and going up to cut the marriage line.
- **Per books:** the person may **kill their own life partner**. The teacher said it must be checked by **practice**, not by forcing/drawing any line.
- Does all this apply to male and female? Both; same. 
- **Q:** The spouse could be violent to the other? Yes, can be.

# 5.7. Heart Line Link 

> [!IMPORTANT] Never read the marriage line alone
> Read the **Heart Line** with it.
> - **Heart Line ending under the Saturn mount** (stopping straight, or rising and stopping anywhere there) → somewhat **low interest**, less emotion, **more practicality**; loves with the **mind, not the heart**; "minds their own business".
> - As in jyotiṣa, the **5th lord in the 7th, or the 7th lord in the 5th** gives a love match. Teacher: with deśa-kāla, today this combination is not present yet situations arise.
> - A **Heart Line going straight up to the Saturn mount** (from around the Sun mount side or further ahead) → **arranged-marriage combination**, **not love marriage**. 
> - As much as this goes upto the Jupiter Mount, it increases the chances of love marriage
> - Even more possibility for Fiery-hand and water-hand.
> - **Next class:** combinations for **love marriage, no marriage, divorce**.
