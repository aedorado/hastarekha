# Part 1 — Two Symbols (end-of-line signs)

## 1.1 Diamond Chain — wish-fulfilment sign

> [!INFO] Definition
> A diamond-shaped chain at the **end of a line**. Teacher's name for it: *wish / wish-fulfilment sign* (icchā-pūrti kā chain).

- **Where seen:** it can form at the end of any line, but is **mostly seen at the end of the fate line (bhāgya rekhā) or the sun line**.
- **Fate line example:** the line goes up and then forms a diamond-like shape at its end.
- **Sun line example:** if the sun line is short, the diamond chain appears at its last end.
- **Meaning:** wish fulfilment; it **greatly increases the strength of that line** (the line's power becomes **double**).
- **Clarification:** it is *not* that the result arrives only at the end of life. The line's power is doubled throughout.
- Whatever the line gives — name, fame (prasiddhi), wealth — the diamond fulfils the person's wishes: all desires come to fulfilment.

## 1.2 Canopy — protection sign

> [!INFO] Definition
> A **circle at the end of a line**. Called **canopy**. Like the **square** (taught earlier) it is a **protection sign**.

- Can appear at the end of **any line**.
- **Example (life line / jīvana rekhā):** the life line may have many ups and downs, but if a circle is seen at its end, then however many ups and downs occur, the **whole life is protected**.

# Part 2 — The Brain Line (mastiṣka rekhā)

## 2.1 Recap and importance

> [!NOTE] Recap given
> - All events show on the **life line**.
> - The **fate line** is also taken as *bhāgya rekhā* (fortune), *dhana rekhā* (wealth), and is used for **career**.

- **Brain line = the base of the whole hand.** Analogy given: if the brain itself were dead, it would not matter how healthy the rest of the body is. *(Transcript garbled after this, with a break to ask students to switch cameras on.)* **[unclear in transcript]**
- It shows the **thought process**, and so sets the foundation for everything.

> [!EXAMPLE] Good life, still worried
> Life line very good, career very good, everything going well — yet some people keep getting upset again and again. That is the **brain line** (thought process). If someone is worried without any reason while everything is fine, there is some trouble in the thought process.

> [!EXAMPLE] Hard life, strong head
> If the person is **head-strong** (very strong brain line) but the life line and fate line show many difficulties, then however many troubles come, the person will bear all of them "as a mom". *(Phrase as in transcript; context partly unclear.)*

## 2.2 Brain line as a manifestation tool

- The teacher calls it the **manifestation tool**. *(The sentence explaining "what we take in/demand" is garbled.)* **[unclear in transcript]**
- **Example:** you decide "I must build a good career."
  - In many hands the career line is visible for some time, then becomes faint — only an *impression* appears, not a formed line.
  - But as the person reaches that age, **if the thought process is strong, the line starts forming**.
- Hence **a good brain line is very important**.

## 2.3 Names and nature

- Also called the **air line** (it shows the thinking process). If the thought process changes even slightly, or the mind wanders and many thoughts arise during the day, this is the line that shows it.
- Other names (teacher listed): **mātṛ rekhā** (can tell about one's mother), **śīrṣa rekhā**, **śiro rekhā**, **śikhara rekhā**, **bhoga rekhā**, **mastaka rekhā**, **anya rekhā**, and many more. The teacher said they may be in the PDF; otherwise she will tell them again.

## 2.4 Origin

- **Taken as the base origin:** the point where the life line begins.
- Not everyone's starts there. It can go anywhere: Moon mount, Mercury mount, or end in the middle.

## 2.5 Variations by origin / relation to the life line

### A. Joined with the life line

- The brain line starts with and **runs joined to the life line**, for any length of time (e.g., up to age 5, up to 10 years), and then may separate.
- **Meaning, for the period it stays joined:**
  - The person **follows family's words**; listens to the parents' guidance.
  - Is a **follower of tradition**.
  - May **depend on others** (in childhood this can only be the family).
  - **Confidence level** may be slightly lower — but the teacher said **confidence is not judged from this alone**; thumb, Sun finger and whole hand must be seen.
  - Does not oppose much. (A slight condition of opposing arises later; teacher will explain — "remind me".)

> [!WARNING] Common mistake
> A student must not say "mine is joined but I never obeyed my family." The meaning is that you **listen to the guidance your parents give**; this must be understood properly, not mechanically.

### B. Slight gap from the life line

- A small gap: one side is the family, the other is my own thinking.
- Such children **form their own opinions and present them**, but **do not oppose**; they listen to you and also put forward their views.
- Example: children who show interest from childhood when family members sit and talk.
- They will not take the opposite meaning and cut your words.

### C. Large gap from the life line

- Measure: about **the back end of a pen**, or **about one finger's width**; the teacher said generally you will not see more than this.
- **Meaning:** can be a **risk taker** and **self-willed** (apni chalane wala).
- **Self-willed condition arises when:** the thumb is very big, the Mercury finger is set apart, the Jupiter finger is **not** tilted toward Saturn, and the will-power area (thumb) is strong.
- Remark: nowadays every child shows this independence — wants to state what he wants to do, asks "why is this happening?", unlike earlier parents-obeying children.

> [!CAUTION] Exception — same gap, but the person says "I have never disobeyed my father"
> If a **line is seen joining between the two lines** (life line and brain line), then thoughts may differ but the person **will not cut the other's words and will obey**.

> [!EXAMPLE] Teacher's own illustration of independence
> Teacher went away from home to study. At the **5th-class admission** she did her own admission, bought her own copies and books herself. Whether family did it or not was fine — independent at that level.
> - One child goes walking with mother; another goes alone on a cycle and needs no one.
> - One child's parents will come to deposit college fees; another will say "I'll go, check, deposit fees myself."
> - **Principle:** this condition differs for each person according to **place, time and circumstances (deśa-kāla-paristhiti)**. "Book language" is the same but in a real hand the details change.

> [!NOTE] Teacher's observation (not in books)
> When the two lines **separate**, the person can stay away from home, e.g., study in a **hostel**. The teacher saw this in her own hand and in 3–4 other hands; "you will not find this written anywhere."

### D. Island where the brain line joins/starts

- An **island** at this junction indicates **mental pressure** at that age.

### E. Aging of the brain line — important rule

> [!IMPORTANT] Brain line is not aged like the other lines
> - Age systems learned so far: life line, fate line (and heart line also has ages).
> - **The brain line is not read by age.** To calculate timing for brain-line indications, go to the **other lines**.
> - **Reason:** a thought that enters the mind in childhood may stay for a long time. *Example:* if a relative said something to you in childhood, will you like to go to that relative's house even when grown up? You'll say "no, they behaved that way with me." So the brain line is not about one period; it is an **overall life pattern** (e.g., a pattern of depression or not — you can tell from the line's condition).
> - The method of aging it will be taught in **tomorrow's class**.
> - Don't read in "bookish language" only.

## 2.6 Origin on the mounts

- Generally the brain line starts from **either Jupiter mount or Mars mount**.
- Starting slightly above is also treated as the **Jupiter area**.
- **Interpretation:** the mindset follows the starting mount.
  - From **Jupiter** → *guru-like* mindset.
  - From **Mars** → *Mars-like* mindset.
- This sets the pattern of one's thinking.

## 2.7 Variations by course and shape

### A. Straight brain line to Mercury mount

- Runs perfectly straight across the whole palm up to the Mercury mount, **not touching the heart line**.
- **A very good brain line; found in few people.** Very logical, very creative, out-of-the-box thinker, very intelligent ("the first one").
- Here the **gap between heart line and mind line is larger** (about a finger).
- Many variations exist.

### B. Sydney line (semi-simian) — smaller gap

- Heart line and brain line run with a **smaller gap**, but the brain line still **cuts across the whole palm** and may go straight to Mercury.
- **Name:** teacher says to call it **Sydney line**; (she said it is called *semi simian line* or *Sydney line*, and some places call it *senior line*, but to avoid confusion use **Sydney line**).
- **Traits:** stubborn (**jiddi**); once an idea sits in the mind it cannot be removed — if they are after something, they are *after* it; very practical (but also check the heart line — head and heart both); can also be very emotional, yet a different way of thinking; motivation "show me something different"; **research-oriented / research mind**; prone to "**policing anxiety**" **[unclear in transcript]**.

> [!EXAMPLE] Umang Taneja
> Teacher knows **Umang Taneja** (the nāḍī astrology presenter "who troubles all astrologers these days"); she photographed his hand from a video of his hand. A **perfectly straight line**. Whether it is the Sydney or the "senior" line is a small distinction; she will show the picture if found. His nāḍī astrology runs in his own name — he built an entirely separate identity.

### C. Merged head-and-heart line ("senior line" in transcript)

- **Only one line**: heart line and brain line are **merged**; there is no third line. The other lines are normal.
- Name in transcript: "senior line" **[unclear in transcript — name garbled]**.
- **Traits:** very sharp mind, very practical, very stubborn. The person takes heart and mind together and knows how to balance them (teacher's analogy: like **Mercury in Libra** — knows how to balance).
- **Biggest problem is prediction;** teacher will give the prediction when teaching the heart line.
- **Judge the line's quality:**
  1. **Dark vs light** (faint or deep).
  2. Whether it **rises in the middle or slopes down** (e.g., dips toward the Moon, rises, then comes down).
- **Rule:** if it slopes **downward** → the person listens to the **mind** more (brain line down, heart line up). If it goes **upward** → listens to the **heart** more.
- *(Teacher also said ages for when heart/mind are followed come from the "186" patterns — garbled.)* **[unclear in transcript]**
- If the person is not able to balance — sometimes hears the heart, sometimes the mind — **anger increases, becomes irritable**; the person has the most trouble.
- They are as intelligent as they are **anxious**; can become **suspicious (śakkī)** in mind.

### E. Wavy brain line

- Calm such a person first; ask "why are you so troubled/confused?" They **cannot stay firm on one statement**.
- Line goes **up** → very happy, learns new things. Line comes **down** → emotionally disturbed again. **Mood swings very high, mental clarity lacking.**
- **Check where the end goes.**
  - Going **straight** to the Moon mount is seldom seen (it would have to cross the middle).
  - A **sudden** drop to the Moon mount: the brain that was very logical/practical (Mars-like) suddenly changes.
  - A **wavy line going toward the Moon mount** is very dangerous: mood swings, **living in imagination, daydreamer** (not necessarily bad), **depression possible**; changeable like the Moon; thoughts change every little while (like children who every few minutes want to order something from outside).

### F. Lines cutting the brain line

- **Mars line** (from **lower Mars**) cutting the brain line: all are **enemy lines**. Many problems possible: **depression, anxiety, thought problems, lack of mental clarity, confusion, headache, migraine**. Feels "however much I try to do right, some trouble comes."
  - Even a good life line shows trouble when this line cuts across.
  - If it cuts and goes on to the **fate line**: fate line and career may be good, yet because of some reason (e.g., a **child's health issue**, own health) there is **mental trouble**. Physical trouble vs mental trouble.

### G. Downward branches at the Moon mount

- Brain line goes to the Moon with many small **downward branches**.
- Indicates **danger from water** (also true if the life line goes toward the Moon, or the brain line toward the Moon).
- Danger from water can mean: **accident**; **cough-related** trouble; **anxiety**; **addiction**; trouble from a **female**. Mental trouble may be linked to any of these.

### H. Fork at the end — "writer's fork"

- Brain line splits into two at the end (looks like a "four"/fork).
- **Name:** *writer's fork* (post-writers) **[partly unclear]**.
- **One branch to Mercury; other to Mars or Moon.** Qualities of Mercury **and** Mars (or Moon) both.
- Can be a good writer, good artist, mind runs to business, can handle many things — **multitasking**.
- **Drawback:** brain line divided in two = **the mind is not calm**; however much multitasking, the mind stays disturbed.
- **Double brain line (case of S6):** highly intelligent but **duality in the mind**; scattered, "bitar-bitar" — one is busy with this, looks after that. If also more sensitive, more trouble. If the end becomes **watery**, trouble does not go.

### I. Broken brain line

- Shows **lack of stability in thoughts** or **drastic change**.
- **Example:** a very addictive person suddenly leaves everything and becomes a *sādhu*.
- If the line breaks again and again, thought patterns keep changing.
- If **influence lines on the fate line** are also seen, it means you took someone as a **role model** for some time; you changed your life suddenly, accepted that life; later another person's influence comes and you change again.
- Also: **head-related problems and headache** are seen.

### J. Scorpion tail

- Brain line goes on and then **curves at its end like a scorpion's tail**. Can be short or long (e.g., fully straight then curved).
- **Traits:** **secretive** (you can never ask the full matter); **strong personality, stubborn** — especially when the line is long/straight before the tail (the straight line gives its own identity).
- **Past orientation:** cannot leave **old habits/old memories**; may **collect old things (antiques)** — which can be good (old things sometimes prove useful) but others at home may be troubled. Thinking pattern becomes old; becomes somewhat **suspicious**; **lives in the past**; the longer/straighter the line before the tail, the more it troubles others; **anxiety** also present.
- Both negative and positive can be seen. *(One sentence about "giving up old habits / turned the home" is garbled.)* **[unclear in transcript]**

# Part 4 — Chain (jañjīra) vs Parasite Line

> [!FAQ] S7: What is the difference between the parasite line and the chain line?
> **A (teacher):**
> - **Chain (jañjīra):** a line with chain-like links or clustered patterns; seen most on the **family ring (below the thumb)** and at the **start of lines**. Do not call each a separate island; call it a chain.
> - **Parasite line:** runs **very close, touching**, along a line (e.g., fate line); may sometimes be hardly visible because it is so close. Like a **termite (dīmak) — lives inside the house and troubles**. Anuradha ji's fate line has one.
> - Lines that come from **afar** (e.g., Mars line, supportive line) are different; if they cut a line, they make a **chain**; if they run uncut and attached, they are the **parasite line**.
> - S7's summary: cutting → chain; sticking → parasite. Teacher agreed.
