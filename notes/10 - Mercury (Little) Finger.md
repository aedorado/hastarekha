# Lecture #10 — Budh Rekhā/Aṅguli (Mercury Finger) and Finger Analysis

> [!INFO] Topic of This Lecture
> This lecture focuses on the **budh aṅguli (Mercury finger)** — the smallest finger in the hand. It covers what the Mercury finger signifies, its three categories (normal / long / short), crooked (bent) fingers, gaps between fingers, the tilt of the Mercury finger toward or away from the Sun (ring) finger, and general principles about finger thickness/length. The lecture closes with live hand-reading demonstrations on students' own hands.

---

## 1. Introduction to the Budh Aṅguli

- The smallest finger is called the **budh kī finger (Mercury finger)**.
- At this stage only the "empty" finger (without mounts or lines) is being read — once the **parvata (mount)** is studied, its influence will become clearer.

> [!NOTE] Order of Influence
> - **Finger** → indicates nature/manner (tarīkā).
> - **Main influence** → comes from the **mount (parvata)** and its **lines (rekhāeṅ)**.

---

## 2. What the Budh (Mercury) Finger Reveals

### 2.1 Education
- The Mercury finger does **not** show education itself — it shows **how well** a person understands a subject, and how well they can speak about it once asked.
- What kind of education one will receive is shown by the **lines and mount**, not the finger.

### 2.2 Business Sense
- Mercury shows **understanding of business** — not that everyone will go into business, but the ability to run one's household "like a business" — weighing every expense, managing income and outgo carefully.

> [!EXAMPLE] Growing the Nail
> Many people (especially men) **grow the nail** of the little finger long so the Mercury finger appears longer — this is believed to be good for business.

### 2.3 Normal Measurement of the Finger
- Normal measurement of the Mercury finger: **up to the first phalanx of the Sun (ring) finger.**
- The higher above this point the Mercury finger extends, the better — considered good for business understanding.

### 2.4 Vāṇī (Speech/Communication)
Mercury shows:
- Whether the person speaks openly and fluently, or sharply, or too little
- Whether they get nervous while speaking
- Whether they can express themselves, and whether the listener actually understands them

### 2.5 Childbirth / Sexual Intimacy
> [!IMPORTANT] Covered Later
> Childbirth and sexual intimacy are read from Mercury (astrology also brings in Mars and Venus mounts), but this topic will be discussed in detail **when the lines chapter is taught**.

### 2.6 Anxiety
- Any weak finger can be a source of anxiety, but the **Mercury finger specifically** is examined for this.

### 2.7 Writing Skill, Creativity, and Innate Talents
- Creativity is seen from the **Sun finger** as well as from **Mercury** — but Mercury specifically shows **writing skill (lekhan kalā)** and **innate/inborn talents** (as opposed to skills that were learned).
- A love of gardening, writing, or fine craftsmanship (shilpkalā) — all of this is seen from Mercury.

### 2.8 Research
- Research is read from the Mercury finger, with **Mars (Maṅgal)** also playing a role.
- If research brings name and fame, **Sun (Sūrya)** plays a role too; if it becomes a career/duty (karma), **Saturn (Shani)** also comes in — many planets can contribute.

### 2.9 Expression, Manipulation, and Diplomacy
- Every task benefits from having some **expression** — it makes the work more appealing.

> [!EXAMPLE] Example Sentence
> Changing punctuation or phrasing can completely change the meaning of a sentence — e.g., rearranging "God is nowhere" into "God is now here."

- Someone with a good Mercury finger/mount/lines knows how to turn any situation into a **positive framing** — this is also linked to **manipulation**.
- This expressive ability can show up in relationships, speech, or career.
- **Diplomacy** is also read from Mercury.

## 3. Three Categories of the Budh Finger

> [!SUMMARY] Three States
> 1. **Normal**
> 2. **Long**
> 3. **Short**

### 3.1 Normal Budh Finger

> [!INFO] Definition
> If the Mercury finger reaches up to the **joint line of the first phalanx of the Sun (ring) finger**, it is called a **normal Mercury finger**. (Measure without counting the nail.)

A person with a normal Mercury finger may have:
- **Multi-talented** personality
- Good at speaking and social interaction
- **Quick-witted** (hājirjawābī)
- **Love of the arts**
- Reasonable logical/argumentative ability — though not as strong as with a long finger

> [!SUCCESS] Normal Traits
> With a normal finger, all the good qualities of Mercury are present in **balanced measure** — neither too much nor too little.

### 3.2 Long Budh Finger

> [!INFO] Definition and Measurement
> If the Mercury finger extends **above** the first phalanx of the Sun finger, it is long. Up to **half the length of the Sun finger** is considered a good, long Mercury finger. **Going beyond half is not considered good** (though this is rarely, if ever, observed).

- Mercury is a finger that is considered auspicious when **long** — this is why the nail is often grown longer.
- **Law/advocacy** requires strong argumentative ability — a long Mercury finger gives stronger reasoning/logic power.
- Talent and **intellectual capacity** increase beyond the ordinary.
- Good at last-minute preparation — the type who "opens the book at the last moment and still does well."
- May excel at **research**.
- Intelligent in the sense of being a good judge of people and situations (similar to a psychologist's insight).
- Skilled at persuasion — turning a situation to their favor, convincing others of their point of view.
- Good at reading, writing, comprehension, mathematics, calculation — scientist-level individuals often show a strong Mercury finger.

> [!EXAMPLE] The Teacher's Father
> The teacher's father had a very good Mercury finger. He was a farmer, yet had a strong sense of **turning money into more money** (business acumen). One incident: in the village, the lantern light was dim, and the teacher (as a child) complained she couldn't see well to study. Her father walked over to the lantern, appeared to adjust something, and asked, "Can you see now?" She replied, "Yes, much better now" — even though he had not actually touched the lantern at all. He said: **"This is just your mind playing tricks."** This illustrates the Mercury quality of reading/manipulating perception.

### 3.3 Short Budh Finger

> [!WARNING] Negative Influence
> A short Mercury finger is **not considered good** — it is directly associated with **dullness of intellect (mand buddhi)**.

- Difficulty applying logic, difficulty grasping concepts.
- Tendency to forget what was studied repeatedly.
- Sometimes the person becomes **stubbornly fixated** on one idea ("Why was it said this way?") — a rigid, argumentative streak.

> [!CAUTION] Short Mercury + Long Jupiter / Strong Willpower Area
> Such a person may be unable to distinguish right from wrong and becomes fixed on their own understanding.

- Tends to give indications of **problems related to the nervous system** — issues in childbirth, **miscarriages**, **abortions** (all read from Mercury; detailed later in the lines chapter).
- **Decision-making:**
  - A person with a long finger makes decisions quickly, having already thought through Plan B/C/D.
  - A person with a short finger struggles to decide and gets stuck.
- **Speech:** Long-fingered people tend to be quicker-witted; short-fingered people tend to be **timid** — hesitant to speak up or present themselves.
  - If it also tilts slightly toward the Sun finger, the person may be introverted but still possess courage (in a normal, not overly long, state).

> [!SUMMARY] Speaker Emphasized Repeatedly
> A short Mercury finger is quite unfavorable; a normal one is good; a normal-to-long one is even better.

## 4. Crooked/Bent Fingers

> [!CAUTION] Mild Crookedness Is Acceptable
> In this age (Kalyug), some degree of crookedness is common, but **excessive crookedness** is not considered favorable.

- Example: **Nita Ambani's** Mercury finger, which bends in this manner — such a bent finger is associated with a **"cunning" (clever/shrewd)** quality. (The speaker noted she could not recall the exact classical term for this finger position and would check reference books.)
- If the crookedness comes from the **knuckles/joints**, the domain governed by that finger shows a particular effect in that entire field — for example, if it's the Mercury finger, the effect appears in one's writing.

## 5. Gaps Between the Fingers (Spacing)

> [!TIP] How to Check
> Close the fist and open it suddenly — this reveals the true gap (deliberately spreading the fingers apart won't give an accurate reading).

- If there's a gap, light is visible through it.
- **Gap → Generosity:** willingness to give everything, to devote oneself; if someone asks for help, the person is quick to offer it.

> [!NOTE] How a Gap Forms
> When a gap appears between fingers, it's usually because one finger's **base phalanx** has **collapsed/sunk in** on one side.

### 5.1 Class Demonstration — Gap Caused by Sunken Saturn Area
- A student's hand showed a prominent gap caused by the **area of the Saturn finger's base** being sunken on one side (Jupiter's third phalanx, by contrast, was open/in good condition).

| Affected Area | Result |
|---|---|
| Saturn's third phalanx (base) sunken | Poor results despite hard work |
| Saturn's third phalanx weak + finger trouble | Indicates **health-related problems** |

> [!NOTE] Saturn's Third Phalanx
> Saturn's third phalanx reflects "results" (outcomes of effort).

## 6. Mercury Finger's Tilt Toward or Away from the Sun (Ring) Finger

> [!TIP] How to Observe
> Close the fist and open it suddenly to see whether the Mercury finger is **attached/close** to the Sun finger or **separated/drawn away** from it.

### 6.1 Mercury Attached/Close to Sun
- Speech gains a sense of **command** — the person wants to speak with impact.
- In astrology, this can be compared to a Mercury–Sun conjunction (yuti).
- May indicate **cleverness/shrewdness** — a desire for **name and fame**: "If I say something, people will praise me."
- If the attachment is excessive, the person may become introverted in mind — not speaking much outwardly, but inwardly craving recognition and praise.
- Such a person tends **not to break social norms** — because of the underlying fear that others may disapprove.

> [!IMPORTANT] Two Ways of Maintaining Social Norms
> 1. Jupiter finger tilting toward Saturn — the person does not break social rules.
> 2. Mercury finger attached to Sun — the person does not break social rules.

### 6.2 Mercury Separated/Drawn Away from Sun
- This means the person will do what they have **decided in their own mind**, regardless of others.
- Such people prefer to **work outside conventional norms**, even willing to break social rules to do so.

> [!NOTE] Clarification
> Breaking social norms does **not** mean doing something bad. It may simply mean taking a step that one's family or community never considered, or wouldn't have approved of. These are people who **enjoy freedom** and hold **independent, open-minded views**.
- Such people are **not afraid of criticism** — "people will keep criticizing regardless, it makes no difference to me."

## 7. Finger Length, Thickness, and Temperament — General Principles

| Finger Type | Temperament/Traits |
|---|---|
| **Very long fingers** | Research-minded; argumentative/legalistic ("nitpicking") disposition; stubbornness in small matters; **emotional**, sometimes emotionally disturbed; tendency toward **depression** (especially with soft hands); prefers a limited, familiar comfort zone |
| **Thin fingers (especially at the base)** | Sweet/pleasant in speech; some tendency toward pettiness/meanness as thinness increases |
| **Thick fingers (especially at the base)** | Enjoys cooking and eating well; good at hospitality; per classical texts, food-loving but may also lean toward **laziness** |
| **Short fingers (shorter than normal)** | Hasty/impulsive; prone to **anger**; more hard work required in life |
| **Long and thin fingers** | Creative individual; less hard work required in life |
| **Crooked/bent fingers** | Tendency toward **cruelty** of temperament |
| **Both short and thick together** | Both stubbornness and anger increase |

> [!WARNING] Important Caution
> Never judge "hard work" purely from the finger alone — the rest of the hand's lines and features must also be examined.

## 8. Depth of Lines (Deep vs. Light) — General Principle

> [!INFO] Two Types
> - **Light lines**
> - **Deep/dark lines** ("bleeding lines") — appearing almost as if bleeding

> [!WARNING] Deep Lines
> A hand with many deep lines is **not considered favorable** — it indicates a **tough life**.

- If a single line alternates between deep and light along its length (deep at the start, then light, then deep again...), the **light** portions correspond to relatively easier phases of life, while the **deep** portions indicate periods of significant **pressure** in that particular area/timeframe. (To be covered in detail in the lines chapter.)

## Questions & Answers

### Question
In one student's (Anuradha ji's) hand, is the gap appearing near Saturn or near Jupiter?

### Answer
**Saturn** — the gap is caused by one side of the Saturn finger's base area being sunken. This can result in poor outcomes despite hard work, and if the Saturn phalanx or finger itself shows further trouble, it can also point to health problems.

### Question
Can wearing a ring cause the Mercury finger to appear detached from the Sun finger?

### Answer
Yes — wearing a ring can make the finger appear **slightly detached**; it's important to remove the ring to check whether the detachment is genuine.

### Question
What does a gap in the Mercury finger area signify — only generosity, or something more?

### Answer
A gap indicates **generosity** as well as **spending** — the two are read together.

### Question
In the fiery-hand example (Abhijit ji), where both the Jupiter and Sun fingers tilt toward Saturn, what does the combination mean overall?

### Answer
This combination (a longer, risk-taking Sun finger + a fiery hand + a detached Mercury finger) suggests the person will **do what feels right to them**, working outside conventional social patterns, but will **not break the actual rules of society** — because they do not want to be criticized or judged by others.

> [!MEMORY] Key Points to Remember
> - Normal length of the Mercury finger = **up to the first phalanx of the Sun finger**.
> - **Long Mercury** = favorable (up to half the Sun finger's length); **short Mercury** = unfavorable/dull intellect.
> - **Mercury attached to Sun** = norm-following + commanding speech + shrewdness/desire for name-fame.
> - **Mercury detached from Sun** = independent thinking, working outside social norms, unaffected by criticism.
> - **Gap between fingers** = generosity + spending; a sunken area near a mount affects that mount's results.
> - **Deep lines** = signal a tough life.
