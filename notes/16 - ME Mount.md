## Recap — Mercury Finger (From Earlier Lesson)

> [!INFO] Quick recap of the Mercury finger-length rule (previously taught)
> - If the finger's length reaches up to the **sandhi rekhā** (joint line) of the **first phalange**, it is a **medium/normal ("sāmānya")** Mercury finger.
> - Longer than that → a **long** Mercury finger.
> - Shorter than that → a **short** Mercury finger.
> - Each of the three has its own distinct qualities (already covered in the finger lesson).

> [!IMPORTANT] Finger and mount don't both need to be good
> It is **not necessary** that both the finger and the mount be good together. If even **one** feature is good — e.g., a good finger even with a mount that is somewhat lacking — the person still benefits ("kaam chal jaayega"). If the **mount** is well-puffed **and** good signs/marks (chinh) are also present, the effect becomes **more powerful/influential**.

## Mercury Mount — Three Conditions

| Condition | Description |
|---|---|
| **Sāmānya (Normal)** | Similar principle to Saturn's normal condition — should be **somewhat sloped ("dhalwa")**, not overly raised compared to Sun and Jupiter mounts |
| **Atidikshit (Over-developed)** | **Excessively puffed** — distinctly noticeable on sight |
| **Nimna (Under-developed)** | **Somewhat low/pressed** — the mount appears flattened |

> [!NOTE] Common tendency to shift toward Sun
> In **most hands**, the Mercury mount's inclination tends to shift **toward the Sun mount** — the two mounts often merge in the middle, between the Sun and Mercury fingers, appearing raised at that shared midpoint area rather than in their separate designated spots. This pattern is common across most people's hands; a hand where this does **not** happen is comparatively less common.
> - [!CAUTION] **Daśā (planetary period) effects can also influence** how raised or pressed a mount currently appears — this should be kept in mind when reading.

## Mercury Mount — Sāmānya (Normal) Condition: Qualities, in Teaching Order

### 1. Intelligence
- The primary quality associated with Mercury.
- [!IMPORTANT] Clarification of meaning: intelligence here does **not** simply mean "smart" or "not smart" as a blanket label depending on mount condition. It specifically means: **how well a person understands any given subject, and how well they can judge correctly when and how to apply what they've learned.**
- **Contrasted with rote memorization ("ratna")** — memorizing exactly as taught, versus genuinely using one's intelligence to apply, adapt, and integrate new material with what was previously learned (e.g., not discarding earlier lessons — Jupiter mount, Saturn mount, finger tilts — while learning new topics). **True intelligence = integrating and applying knowledge, not just memorizing it.**
- [!NOTE] Books commonly use the phrase **"alpa buddhi"** (little/low intelligence) for a nimna (under-developed) Mercury mount, but the teacher explicitly **avoids that harsh label**, preferring instead: *"they may simply take more time to understand a subject."*
- [!EXAMPLE] Combination illustration: if the **Jupiter mount is low**, **AND** the **thumb is stiff/rigid ("stiff thumb")**, **AND** there are many **grilles (jaali/grid marks)** on the **Venus mount** — this combination makes the person **very difficult to explain things to**, because they have become excessively stubborn.

### 2. Communication
- Mercury governs communication broadly — it can be **two-way** (you speaking and being understood, or understanding what someone else says), and can include **expressions** — hands, eyes, facial expressions used to convey meaning.
- Communication can also be **internal vs. external** — sometimes what is said on the surface doesn't fully capture what was actually meant internally; some people are skilled at externally conveying their true internal thoughts clearly. **A person with a very good Mercury mount will convey their thoughts effectively.**
- [!TIP] Combination-building approach (same principle as combining planets in astrology):
  - Mercury **+ good Jupiter** → explains things with a **philosophical approach**.
  - Mercury **+ good Moon** → explains things **like a story**.
  - Mercury **+ good Mars** → explains things with **reasoning/logic**.
  - The teacher emphasized: *don't memorize a fixed list — learn to build combinations, the way combinations are built in astrology.*

### 3. Adjusting Nature
- Mercury has a generally **good adjusting nature**, because — as in astrology, where Mercury takes on the nature of whichever planet it's conjunct (benefic with benefic, malefic with malefic) — Mercury in the hand similarly **tends to take on whatever influence surrounds it.**
- [!CAUTION] Important qualifier: this is only a **possibility ("sambhāvanā")**, not a guarantee of 100% adjusting nature. **Where the person sees their own advantage, that is the direction they will lean.** This introduces a natural element of **diplomacy** — and potentially **manipulation** — into their nature, regardless of how good the mount otherwise looks.
- The **manner** in which someone gets their way (through pressure, versus through ease/charm) must be judged by examining the **rest of the hand and other mounts** together — not from Mercury mount alone.

> [!EXAMPLE] Extended case — the lady with the very long Mercury finger
> While travelling, the teacher stayed one night at the home of a relative — a lady whose **Mercury finger was notably very long** (observed from the back of the hand; front/palm side not seen). The hand was described as beautiful, with long, elegant fingers.
> - **Situation:** many guests had gathered at her home unexpectedly.
> - **Observed behaviour:** she handled every guest gracefully and without visible stress, despite one guest arriving late. She had that late guest quickly prepare something (aloo parathas) and then arranged for everyone to formally thank/appreciate that guest for it.
> - **The paratha incident:** the late guest had made the parathas somewhat dry (using a glass base dipped in ghee rather than a spoon to grease them, resulting in less ghee than usual) — yet everyone still enjoyed them. The hostess **praised the dish warmly** rather than criticizing the guest for being late, then jokingly told the teacher **"you owe me guru dakshina for that recipe"** — smoothly turning a moment that could have caused embarrassment (the late arrival, the imperfect dish) into a positive, appreciated moment.
> - **The parting-gift trick:** when giving customary parting money to the younger family members, she used clever, charming phrasing to insist they take it ("this tradition must continue with us and end with us; you youngsters won't accept this any other way, so you must take it") — neither too much nor too little given, and delivered so smoothly no one felt uncomfortable.
> - **Conclusion drawn by the teacher:** *"Her Mercury's influence was 100% clearly visible on her, through her manner of speaking."* This is offered as a model for how to observe real people's speech patterns and connect them back to their likely Mercury finger/mount quality.
> - Also noted: she appeared **younger than her actual age** — illustrating the next point below.

### 4. Ageless Appearance
- A good Mercury mount is associated with **looking younger than one's actual age** — this is why Mercury is sometimes called **"ageless"** in palmistry books.

### 5. Hājirjawābī (Quick-wittedness / Presence of Mind)
- Such people respond to any question **quickly and skillfully**, and their manner of answering **never comes across as offensive** — even while making their own point, the delivery feels pleasant. A bit of **humour/playfulness** naturally comes through in how they speak.

### 6. Business-Minded
- People with a good Mercury mount **can be** business-minded, though this does **not mean they will necessarily run a business.**
- Even without running a business themselves, they may have good **business understanding** — offering useful ideas when business is discussed, or privately reflecting on how they might have approached things differently had circumstances allowed.

### 7. Management
- Generally **good money management** ability — as illustrated in the earlier example, where the hostess managed her guests and gifts precisely, neither over- nor under-giving.

### 8. Creativity
- A good Mercury brings **some form of creativity**, and the **best expression of Mercury's creativity is communication itself** — the ability to speak persuasively, explain well, and (in the over-developed condition specifically) even make a lie sound convincingly true.
- Some **reasoning power** can also be read via Mercury (though this is more traditionally associated with Mars).

### 9. Impressive Presence
- Meeting such a person tends to leave a **lasting impression** — people will recall having met them. This is described as "leaving a mark" on others' minds.
- They also tend to have a good **sense of dress**, sometimes favouring **colourful clothing**.

### 10. Analytical Power and Research Ability
- Both **analytical power** and **research ability** are read via Mercury.

### 11. Mathematical / Calculation Ability
- Good with **math and calculations** — may mentally track detailed figures (e.g., remembering exactly how much was spent last month, who owes what, and on which date something was given).

### 12. Writing Skills
- Can have good **writing skills**, may enjoy writing, and can be **good writers**, able to convey their point clearly in few words — whether spoken or written.
- **Systematic approach** to work is also associated with this — the **Jupiter mount** can be checked alongside Mercury for this trait.

### 13. Multitasking
- Mercury also relates to the ability to **handle multiple tasks simultaneously** — such a person has the underlying support/capacity for this.

### 14. Possible Mild Selfishness
- [!CAUTION] If **Mercury is much more puffed than Jupiter**, or the **Jupiter finger is short**, such people can sometimes end up thinking primarily of **themselves** rather than others. (Check the **Jupiter mount** to assess this specifically — a poor/afflicted Jupiter reduces concern for others.)

### 15. Married Life (preview only — full topic later)
- Married life is read via the Mercury mount **combined with lines** — small lines appearing on the Mercury mount are called **marriage lines**; this will be covered in more depth when lines are taught.

### 16. Memory
- Memory is associated with Mercury, but **Mercury mount alone is not sufficient** to fully assess memory — the **Mind Line** and **Heart Line** must also be considered, since emotional distress (e.g., depression, difficulties in married life, or other emotionally taxing life events) can impact a person's memory and thinking capacity, and the Mind Line will reflect that impact.

### 17. Mild Cunningness
- Some degree of **cunningness (chālākī)** is possible, but **not to the extent of harming others**.

### 18. Healing Ability
- Mercury also relates to **healing** in a broad sense — since the hands themselves are the medium for many healing modalities (Reiki, Pranic healing, or medical/clinical touch). Healing can also occur simply by placing a hand on someone's head.
- [!INFO] Definition — **Healer line**: fine, often **broken-looking** lines found running toward the Mercury mount (not necessarily straight). Students in medical fields, or any doctors in the class, may notice this line on their own hands.

### 19. Relationship Issues, Conception, Miscarriage (topic deferred)
- These are also read via Mercury, but the teacher explicitly deferred this material to a later, dedicated chapter — not covered in this session.

## Mercury Mount — Atidikshit (Over-Developed) Condition

> [!WARNING] Overview
> If the mount is **significantly more puffed** than normal, and the **finger is also large**, several of the normal-condition qualities intensify — some positively, but several negatively.

1. **Increased intelligence** — very sharp, learns and remembers quickly ("read it once, remembered it instantly").
2. **But cunningness increases alongside it** — some **cunningness (cunning-ness)**, a tendency to think of **their own advantage**, and possible **selfishness**. Such a person may apply their intelligence in a somewhat self-serving direction.
3. **Communication becomes self-promoting** — mostly used for **self-praise**. Such a person is **more invested in making others understand them than in understanding others.**
4. **Excessive talking** — talks more than necessary, even where no talking is needed. [!NOTE] Astrological parallel offered: this is compared to the effect when **Mercury is excessively strong/"vakrī" in the chart**, which shows its own distinct, separate impact.
5. **Criticism enters communication** — such a person **exaggerates their own image** while subtly **putting others down**, and a tendency toward **backbiting (talking behind someone's back)** appears.
   - [!EXAMPLE] Illustration given: at a gathering discussing business or a money matter with three or four people, the person is careful to stay on good terms with everyone in the room, then **privately pulls one person aside afterward** and says something like: *"What I said in front of everyone was just for show — but you're truly special to me, I'd never really say that about you."* This is the "cunning" pattern showing up in their communication/intelligence.
   - [!NOTE] If, in addition, the **top phalange of the thumb (willpower)** is very strong, such a person can **successfully get others to agree with them.**
   - [!NOTE] If the **Jupiter finger tilts toward Saturn**, however, they will still get their way **without breaking social norms** — they won't cross ethical/societal lines even while being persuasive.
6. **Increased reasoning power** — in the over-developed condition, this becomes **excessive logic/questioning about everything** — asking questions, demanding reasoning, applying logic in situations where it isn't especially needed; a generally **very investigative ("khojī") mind.**
7. **May act for the sake of showing off** — if the mount is unusually large or the finger unusually large, some degree of **show/display (dikhāvā)** can appear in their speech, their working style, and how they conduct business.
   - [!EXAMPLE] Illustration: at a wedding or social gathering, would the average person proactively step forward and announce, "I'm studying astrology these days — let me read your chart right now, I can tell you everything in a minute"? Most people would **not** do this. **An over-developed-Mercury person will.** They may also **indirectly** self-promote — e.g., approaching a stranger, reading a stress mark on their face, and remarking, "You look troubled — and I can see it on your hand too," thereby introducing themselves to the whole gathering **indirectly**, without ever directly stating "I do this professionally."
   - **Contrast with the normal condition:** a normal-Mercury person does **not** do this — they may work quietly for years without others even realizing they practice this skill.

> [!CAUTION] Contrast with the nimna (under-developed) type
> The **nimna** type shows the opposite pattern entirely — extreme **hesitation**, complete inability to reveal or express what they know. Even if someone else were to actively introduce them ("look, this person has learned this!"), the nimna-type person would grow **fearful** — worried their prediction might be wrong, unsure whether they'll be able to explain themselves at all.

## Mercury Mount — Nimna (Under-Developed) Condition

1. **Constant hesitation, fear of speaking up.**
2. **Cannot explain what they actually know** — even if they possess real knowledge, they are unable to communicate it. This creates a **fear-based feeling ("bhaya")** specifically around communication.
3. **Instability in everything** — including possible **mismanagement of money**, since money management (a normal-Mercury quality) is weakened here.
4. **Speech-related issues possible** — e.g., **delayed speech onset in children**, or **stammering**; throat-related ("gale se sambandhit") or voice-related ("vāṇī se sambandhit") difficulties can be associated with this weak condition.
5. **Skin-related issues** possible.
6. **Miscarriages** (in the case of females) — can be an associated indicator.
7. **No real understanding of business** — such a person **cannot independently complete a task** even when heavily supported by others.
   - [!EXAMPLE] Illustration: imagine ten out of eleven steps of a task have already been completed and handed to this person, with only the eleventh step left for them to do independently — **they will not be able to complete that final independent step.**
8. **No meaningful creativity to speak of.**
9. **A "lower-class" mentality pattern** — simply doing the assigned work and nothing more, without any inclination to learn something new or improve a process, sticking rigidly to whatever the "set pattern" already is.
   - [!EXAMPLE] Teacher's illustration: she noticed another practitioner's YouTube channel had all their social media links neatly consolidated in one place; she immediately asked her own social media team to replicate this, since she didn't want to manually copy separate links every time someone asked. **A nimna-Mercury person would not think to make this kind of improvement even if handed the finished tool** — they would struggle to use or present it effectively at all.
10. **Anxiety** — Mercury also governs anxiety, and this appears strongly in the nimna condition; a **100% presence of anxiety** was emphasized.
11. **Relationship issues will occur.**
12. **No research ability whatsoever** ("research ki baat hi mat karo" — don't even bring up research ability for this type).

> [!NOTE] Teacher's aside
> She noted that essentially no one in the class was likely to have a genuinely nimna Mercury mount, since anyone actively struggling to grasp and retain a subject like this course would face real difficulty engaging with it at all — the class's engaged participation itself was offered as informal evidence against a nimna condition among the students.

## Mercury Mount — Apex Direction

> [!INFO] Two possible directions for the Mercury Apex
> 1. Toward the **Sun** (mount)
> 2. Toward the **outside of the palm** (beyond/past the natural edge of the hand — reached only by crossing over existing lines)

### Apex Tilted Toward Sun
- **Increased confidence in speech** — since Sun brings confidence.
- **"Ojasvī vāṇī"** (radiant/authoritative speech, like the Sun) — impactful, effective delivery.
- **Possible success in business** — since Sun is involved, **name and fame** may follow in their work — at minimum, recognition within their own local community/area (not necessarily large-scale fame, which would additionally require checking the **Fate Line** and **Mercury Line**).
- **Expresses themselves well** in whatever domain they engage with.
- **Responsible**, and **does not break rules** (confirmed by student contribution during the live discussion).
- Can be described using their **cunningness constructively**: getting their way skillfully, **without hurting anyone's feelings**, while still accomplishing their own goals.

### Apex Tilted Outside the Palm (Beyond the Natural Edge)
> [!WARNING] Generally not considered a favourable placement
> Palmistry texts generally regard the Apex reaching **outside/beyond the palm's natural boundary** (crossing over existing lines to do so) as **not a good sign** — traditionally said to **increase struggle in life**. This appears as an unusually **jagged/rough-edged** look to that part of the hand.

> [!IMPORTANT] Distinguishing this from an unrelated earlier case ("breaking rules")
> A student raised an earlier example (from a prior recording) about someone who acted **outside social norms** — that earlier case was actually a matter of the **finger's** natural positioning: when opening the hand normally, the finger sits **naturally away from the Sun finger** (without needing to be pulled apart deliberately). This creates **independent-mindedness**:
> - If **other people criticize** such a person for going against convention (e.g., "who told you to quit your stable job?"), **it genuinely won't bother them.** They respond with confidence: *"I'll prove myself, I'll find success ahead."*
> - If, **additionally**, the **thumb** is in good condition **and** the **Sun finger** is in good condition, the **likelihood of achieving success increases**; and if the **career line (the Saturn-associated line, to be studied later)** is also good, the probability of success rises considerably further.
> - Someone with this finger placement is described as having a **"svatantra vichārdhārā"** (independent way of thinking / independent ideology) — unaffected by others' opinions of their choices.

## Crookedness in the Mercury Finger

- A **mild crookedness** can appear in some hands, giving a modest degree of **cleverness in business dealings** — but not to an extreme degree.
- **Two distinct sub-types of "crooked":**
  1. **Bend** — only a **portion/section** of the finger curves.
  2. **Tilt** — the **entire finger** leans toward a neighbouring finger (e.g., leaning as a whole toward the Sun finger).
- [!EXAMPLE] Teacher referenced a well-known businesswoman (**Nita Ambani**, based on news photographs) as an example of a finger that, by this framework, would be classified as a **"crooked finger."**
  - [!CAUTION] Explicit disclaimer: the teacher clarified she has **only seen this one example** and has not personally examined this person's hand up close — she is going only by what's visible in photographs, and has not seen this particular crooked-finger pattern elsewhere. She noted that such crooked-finger types are generally described in palmistry tradition as **very clever people**, sometimes said to be willing to **cross ethical boundaries** for their own advantage — but stressed this is a general tradition-based description, not a personal verified claim about the specific individual referenced.
- [!NOTE] Related note on the **philosophical hand** (knotted finger joints, discussed in an earlier session): the teacher's own hand shows this knot pattern on the **Jupiter and Saturn fingers only**, not on Mercury or Sun. If this knotted pattern appears specifically on the **Mercury finger**, it indicates a **Mercury-related philosophy** (philosophical inclination expressed through communication/intellectual domains) rather than the more general spiritual philosophy associated with Jupiter/Saturn knots.
