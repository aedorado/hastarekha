# 2. Method 1 – Old Indian method: ṣaḍaṁśa paddhati (division by 6)

> [!INFO] What it was
> - Old method; **no longer used**, but students should know it so that they recognise it when they read/hear of it.
> - The **Life Line is measured** from the thumb-side origin (**zero**) **to the maṇibandha rekhā** (Wrist line). The total age taken is **70 to 72**.
> - **Procedure:** take a **thread (or paper)** and lay it **along the line, following its curve** (not as a straight line). Mark the length. Divide it into **parts of 6 years each** — "like the table of six" — **0, 6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36, 42, 48 … up to 72** (12 equal parts).
> - Teacher's demonstration with a straight line showed the parts coming out too wide or too small; she said she must even them out and put a proper version on a hand in the group for the notes.
> - **Note on 30:** in the method she uses now, 30 is exactly the midpoint; in the old six-based method **age 30 falls at roughly 24 on the line** *(teacher: "this thirty in the old one comes around the 24 place")*. **[exact wording garbled in transcript]**

> [!IMPORTANT] Old Indian palmists used the Heart Line, not the Life Line
> - **Indian palmists of that period did not calculate age with the Life Line; they took the Heart Line (hṛdaya rekhā) as the Life Line** — reasoning: if the heart stops working, life ends.
> - Later, because more experiments were done in the West, the **Life Line** began to be used for age.
> - **Why the assumed lifespan rose:** medical improvement, e.g., earlier a woman could die in childbirth; now it is less so; even in Corona many survived. So **by deśa-kāla-paristhiti the assumed age was raised**.

# 3. Method 2 – Cheiro's method: saptāṁśa paddhati (division by 7)

- Cheiro (a western palmist) did much research; some still follow his method.
- **Total age taken: 98 (about 98–100).** **Division by 7**, i.e., the "table of seven": 98 ÷ 7 = **14 equal parts**. Called **saptāṁśa paddhati** — "the six-based one is the **ṣaḍaṁśa paddhati**."
- **Procedure:** same centre/origin **zero** at the Life Line's start and the **base line (maṇibandha)**; lay a thread/paper along the line; divide into **14 equal parts**.
  - **Practical tip from a student:** cut the thread to the measured length, then **fold it in half, then half again**, and so on, to get equal parts; the teacher agreed.
  - In her demonstration, **35 years** fell at a certain point on the line; she noted this is the pattern **most places teach**.
- **Teacher's own practice:** *"If you ask me which practice I follow — it is the one taught earlier"* (the 30-midpoint method of §6), because its **results are coming fairly well**; she has never followed Cheiro's pattern, but students may try it.
- **Cheiro's advice:** when giving a prediction, **do not give more than 7 years ahead — even less**, because **the lines of the hand keep changing**.

# 4. Method 3 – Three-line average (100 years per line)

> [!IMPORTANT] Steps
> 1. Take **three lines: Life Line, Mind Line and Heart Line** (the **Fate Line is left out**).
> 2. For **each** line, take the **full length as 100 years** (the "98 to 100" of Cheiro; "theory").
> 3. **Estimate where the line ends** within that 100-year scale and write the age:
>    - **Life Line:** measured from its start (thumb side) to its end — e.g., *70*.
>    - **Heart Line:** measured from its start to its **very end** (**some end on Saturn, some elsewhere**; take it to the last point) — e.g., *75*. Divided in the "seven-seven" way as in the Cheiro method.
>    - **Mind Line:** counted from **where it actually starts** — if joined with the Life Line, from the joining point; if it starts **away** from it, **count from there. "No mistake here."** The origin is zero and the full length is 100 — e.g., *60* (anywhere 60–62).
> 4. **Add** the three (example: 70 + 75 + 60 = **205**).
> 5. **Divide by 3** (three lines) → **about 68** (the class worked it out: 205 ÷ 3 ≈ 68); say **"68 to about 70"**.

- **Western origin:** this is how it is done in the West, where **100 years** is taken.
- A student remarked that Cheiro is Western, hence the long lifespan; **Teacher:** yes — **it changes with deśa-kāla**; in **India the average age is taken as about 70 or 72**, so **we may use 70/72 or 75–80 in this method** instead of 100; or use Cheiro's method. She said one would have to **research current approximate lifespan (newspapers etc.)**.
- **Best way to practise:** use the hands of people **who have already passed away** and calculate their age.
- **With a live client:** use the client's **actual age** and calibrate — e.g., for a client **aged 85 (Dave ji)**, calculate on **100**; then **ask one or two known events** (e.g., "a triangle on the Life Line — in what age did you buy property?") to see what age that point corresponds to.
- **Practice needed:** print many hands so that one becomes quick: "here it is 7, here 14, here this."

# 5. What the class is following now – Method 4: the "30 at the midpoint" method

> [!IMPORTANT] Life Line (as taught, now repeated with paper)
> 1. **Zero** at the Life Line's start (thumb side); lay the thread/paper along the line up to the **maṇibandha rekhā** and join it.
> 2. **Second centre point:** the **Mercury line and the palm's last line, the *sandhi rekhā***; take the **centre point by folding the paper in half** — it should come "a little above" the hand's mark. Join this centre point with the starting point.
> 3. **This centre point is always 30** — "**fixed**."
> 4. After it, the **average age is 75** (approximate); the **second half runs 30 → 75** and is **halved again and again**.
> 5. For the Fate Line it is **done exactly the same way, from the maṇibandha rekhā**: even if the person's Fate Line starts elsewhere (e.g., from Venus or the middle), **age is counted from the maṇibandha rekhā** to the top (**75**), centre = **30**, then halve onward; **30 to 75** is the duration to subdivide.
>    - If the Fate Line starts higher, the **gap in between** may mean the person did not **focus on career** as much until later; **but the line's age still starts from the maṇibandha rekhā**.

> [!FAQ] Q (Dravin ji): If the Fate Line is **not visible**, how do I calculate its age?
> **A (Teacher):** Normally you will see it. **If you cannot see it, do not calculate its age; see events on the Life Line and do all the aging there.** Many hands have very faint lines. If it is **joined to the Life Line** and you can see it going up, you can do it; otherwise use the Life Line.

# 6. Heart Line age (two methods)

> [!IMPORTANT] Method A (preferred)
> - From where the Heart Line **starts**: **18 years**; then the **gap in the middle = 6 years**; then **18 years**; then **6 years**; then **18**; and so on — **18 + 6 + 18 + 6 + 18 …** — until the point where the Heart Line **stops**.
> - If the last block is **incomplete** (e.g., it covers only part of an 18), **estimate** — e.g., 15 or 14 — and add: *18 + 6 + 18 + 6 + 18 + 6 + 18 + 6 + 14*.

> [!IMPORTANT] Method B
> - Take the **centre** of the Heart Line as **16** (the side toward the start); then **36** at the next marker; then **50** at the next; and beyond that "**50 and above, any**".
> - **Teacher's view:** **Method A is better**, because you generally find the Heart Line's data only **up to about age 50** — beyond that it is not found.

# 7. Mind Line age

- The Mind Line's age is **generally not calculated** — "all aging should be done on the **Life Line**; the **best aging** is obtained there."
- If you still want it: **start at zero where it starts**; do **exactly as for the Heart Line**: **18, then 6, then 18, then 6 …** (e.g., "this is 18, this 6, this 18, this 6 … this 24 here").

> [!SUMMARY] Methods taught today (as the teacher counted them: "three methods studied today")
> Old six-based Life Line (72) → Cheiro's seven-based (98) → three-line average (100 each) → the **current** 30-midpoint method (Life Line, Fate Line), with **18+6** for Heart and Mind Lines.

# 9. Budha Rekhā (Mercury Line)

> [!INFO] Definition
> - **Any line going toward Mercury is the Mercury Line.** It is **simple — not much in it.** Also called the **health line**.
> - **Not present in every hand** — and "it is good that it is not": **if the line is there it is not good for health**, though it **can be good for business**.

## 9.1 Where it starts

| Start | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Starts from below the Heart Line** (and goes on) | More **health problems**. |
| **Starts only above the Heart Line** (not below it) | **Business indications in later ages.** Called the **healer line** (also another medical-related name the teacher could not recall). |

- **Healer line** — found in the hands of **doctors, healers, counsellors** — anyone **whose talk leaves you better and removes your trouble**. **Reason:** Mercury is the significator of **speech (vāṇī)**; if you can **heal even by talking**, this line will be in your hand.

## 9.2 Intuition line

- A line **starting from the Moon Mount and going to Mercury** is the **intuition line** (rare).
- It can also form another way: **the Mercury line is already running, and another line from the Moon side joins it** → it **develops intuition**.
- **If it is not there, do not conclude you have no intuition** — "many more combinations of intuition will come in later chapters; all will be revised."
- **Meaning:** you get **foreboding (pūrvābhāsa)** — you know beforehand something is going to happen.
- **Q (student, whose hand has a line from the Moon side joining the Mercury line, making a "7" shape):** confirmed that **a line from the Moon joining the Mercury line indicates intuition** ("yes, absolutely, if from the Moon"; the teacher also asked whether it came from Mars).
- **Q (Harishchandra ji):** What is an intuition line? **A:** you get a **premonition**; "you see".

## 9.3 Many Mercury lines; liver line

- **Having a Mercury line gives health problems.** It is also called the **liver line**. Anyone who has it should **take care of food and health**; where it is absent, don't call the person healthy — see the other combinations — but **those who have it have more health problems**.
- *(A student said she has many Mercury lines — "you will have many, many".)*

## 9.4 Signs on the Mercury Mount

| Sign | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Star** (elsewhere bad; **on Mercury Mount some regard it as good**) | The person is **very clever in speaking**; can be a **researcher**; good speaker. (Teacher recalled a star seen on a hand the day before.) |
| **Square** | Good. |
| **Triangle** | Good. |
| **Cross** | **Not good**: trouble in **partnerships** (business partnership) — **and also the marriage line** (it lies here). |
| **Island** | Not good. |
| **Mole (til)** | Not at all good; **very bad for honour (mān-samman)**. |
| **Jālī (grille)** | Not good — see conditions below. |

- **Marriage line (vivāha rekhā):** not long — it is short; **generally it extends only up to a limited place**. **A line that goes beyond that and cuts the Sun Line** (taught the day before) **shows trouble because of a woman**: in a male's hand it may be the **wife or another woman of the house**, through whom **insult** must be borne.
- **Jālī on Mercury:** possible, because **horizontal lines are there on a healer's hand**. **Conditions (repeated):**
  - **At least three vertical and three horizontal** lines.
  - If the vertical is dark and horizontal light, **not counted** — "the jālī is not fully formed", a "light jālī" (common on Venus).
  - **More than three** is still a jālī; **less than three** is not.
- **Jālī under the Sun finger** (a student's hand) → **not good**, both for **health** and for **Sun-related events**.

## 9.5 Wealth indications (Mercury)

> [!SUCCESS] Single vertical line on the Mercury Mount
> - **Recall:** the **white spot on the nail** was a wealth sign (taught earlier). **Another on Mercury:** **if only ONE straight line is found there** → **sudden money gain**.
> - It may be forming now or may form later — "lines keep forming; **take photographs of your hand over time**."
> - **If there are more than two**, they become **healing lines**; **one line = sudden money gain**.

> [!SUCCESS] "Treasury of money" – the triangle (repeated)
> - The triangle formed by the **Mercury line, the Fate Line and the Mind Line** is the **dhana kī koṭhī (money's room)**.
> - **The cleaner it is, the better**: if **crooked cross lines** fall inside it, **"there is money but expenses are more than the money"**.
> - If it is clean, the **possibility of money increases** — **but always check the hand's condition**: "the **softer, better, cleaner** the hand, the more the chance of a better financial condition."

> [!SUCCESS] Circle
> - **Not a dot** (a thick dot/mole is bad). A **circle with skin showing in the middle** is a **sign of protection from illness**. **A square or a triangle too** — **all save you from disease**; even if illness comes, you escape it.

> [!FAQ] Q5 (Dravin ji): A line **from the Heart Line to the Mind Line** vs a line **from the Mind Line to the Heart Line** — are the predictions different?
> **A (Teacher):** **First see the distance between the two lines.**
> - **If they run very close and a line connects them** (from either side): **treat it as a semi-simian line.** (Generally the connecting line comes from the Heart Line; **from the Mind Line it is rarely seen**.)
> - **If the distance is greater**, it is **not** a semi-simian line.
>   - **A line from the Heart Line to the Mind Line** → **heartbreak, family disturbance, emotional issues.**
>   - **A line from the Mind Line up toward the Heart Line** → the mind turns **toward the heart**: someone who says "I think practically; emotions don't dominate me," but still **keeps an emotional touch in speech**, looking at how the other will feel. **This is seen rarely.**
> - **Usually** such lines are **those coming from Lower Mars**: they may be faint and become **dark further on**; **treat them as an enemy line — not good, they give disturbance.**

# 12. Learning Summary

> [!SUMMARY]
> - **Old Indian method (ṣaḍaṁśa):** Life Line to maṇibandha = 72, in **6-year parts**; early Indian palmists used the **Heart Line** as the life line.

> - **Heart/Mind Line ages:** **18 + 6 + 18 + 6 …** (Method A) — or 16/36/50 (Method B, less useful).
> - **Mercury Line:** health/liver line; **below Heart Line = health problems; only above = business/healer**; **Moon→Mercury = intuition**; **single line on Mercury Mount = sudden money gain**; **star on Mercury Mount = clever speaker/researcher**; **cross = partnership/marriage trouble**; **jālī ≥3×3 equal = bad**.
> - **Money triangle (Mercury/Fate/Mind lines):** clean = money possible; crooked lines = expenses exceed money.
