## 3. The "Money Line" — what the fate line can and cannot tell

> [!WARNING] Do not read the amount of money from the bhāgya rekhā
> - Just as in astrology you **cannot say how much money a person will have**, you cannot say it here either. Teacher asked everyone to hear this answer clearly.
> - What *can* be said: whether the person's **life will be comfortable or not**.
> - **Reason/example:** many gurus have no money, yet people serve them so much that wherever they go, lodging, food, everything is available. No money, but all things are provided → this shows a *comfortable life*.
> - Therefore the bhāgya rekhā is **not linked to money**. It *can* be linked to **career**, with some variations (taught later).

> [!NOTE] Where money may come from (all can be read)
> - Career.
> - An **investment** made somewhere — the bhāgya rekhā also shows this.
> - Ancestors may have left enough that the person never needs more.

> [!TIP] First thing to check for money / comfort: quality of the hand
> - The **softer**, **cleaner** the hand, the **fewer negative signs** and the **fewer lines** it has, the more money / the more comfortable the life.
> - Teacher's instruction: stop discussing money in class from now on. (Dravin ji later remarked that "madam said no one will talk about money today".)

## 4. Possible presentations of the fate line

> [!INFO] Any kind of hand may be seen
> - A hand **with** a fate line.
> - A hand with **no fate line at all** — the teacher has not seen one and asked students to look for one.
> - A **broken, cut-up** line, or a line visible for a very short time and then stopping.
> - **More than one** fate line.

> [!WARNING] Common mistake
> Do **not** assume "no fate line = no career." Many people in some sections of society do not do paid work, yet have a fate line.

### What the fate line really shows
- Do not tie it directly to money. The work can be **any kind** — even **work done at home**.
- It shows the person's **thinking and planning**: a **direction**, how **focused** the person is toward it (e.g. planning for tomorrow's work). This applies even to career.

### Variations the teacher gave
1. **No fate line but a good career** is possible.
2. **No fate line, but working anyway:** the person works because of family and many responsibilities, but not in work of their choice — e.g. a **nine-to-five job** or **government job** taken and continued as a compulsion. So absence of a fate line can indicate a job done out of compulsion, not liking.
3. **Good fate line but the person is not working:** do not judge from the fate line alone. Check the **life line, brain line and heart line** also, because:
   - there may be **disturbances in the family** preventing work, or
   - the person gets **all comforts** and sees no need to work — e.g. children in rich families who are less focused on work, thinking "my parents are here to look after everything; even if I do nothing it will be fine."

> [!TIP] Method
> Set aside the narrow view and read with **deśa–kāla–paristhiti** (place, time, circumstance), exactly as in astrology where everything is connected.

## 7. Variation 1 — Origin of the fate line

> [!TIP] First step
> First see **where the fate line originates**. It will head toward Saturn. Some lines reach Saturn exactly ("click" right to the end); some stop midway; this depends on the hand.

### 7.1 Origin from the Moon mount (Cāndra parvata)
- The line starts on the Moon mount and may go any length (up to Saturn, or only part of the way).
- It may be strong/deep at the start and then become **lighter and lighter**.
- It may be one line or more than one.
- Method: **connect** the person's fate, destiny, career and planning with the **Moon**.

> [!NOTE] Moon significations as listed in class
> - **Students:** mind matters, anxiety, mother.
> - **Teacher:** if seen directly as career — **creative career** (Student: *flexible*, doesn't stay in one place). **Healing line** is also possible.

> [!IMPORTANT] Rule: Moon-origin fate line → need of support
> - Generally said: if the fate line comes from the Moon mount, the person **needs support**.
> - Kinds of support: **public (janatā) support** — e.g. **politicians**, who cannot work without the public; support of the **surroundings**.
> - Also **state government job**, because the Moon indicates state government.
> - **Example:** Arvind ji's hand (Moon-origin line): teacher said he cannot work without public support; Arvind ji agreed — his work cannot be done without the public. Teacher: without the public, there is no work.

> [!SUCCESS] Smoothness rule
> - The **smoother** the line — **no island, no cut/cross, no dot, not too thick/dark** — the **smoother and better** the career.
> - The more cuts, crosses and negative signs, the **more trouble**.

### 7.2 Age and starting point of the line
1. If the line starts from the very beginning (Moon mount start / near the wrist), it means **work started early**. "Early" depends on circumstances, e.g.:
   - a child of 10–15 years already going with father and watching his work, or taking on a mother's work;
   - the person already had in mind "I have to do this work" and prepared — e.g. someone who wants to join the **Army** begins exercise early, so their **planning becomes very good**.
2. If the fate line goes **straight up from the wrist** (**kalāī**): the person is **purely a self-made, hard-working person**. Even here **someone's support** may exist — father, family, surroundings, or another influence.
3. The **later the line starts**, the **later the age**: e.g. job at 25, 30 or 35. Age is increased along the line.

> [!TIP] Calculating age on the fate line
> 1. The **zero point** for fate-line age is at the **wrist** (the bottom), *not* where the line happens to start.
> 2. If the line **curves**, imagine **straightening it** and read where it lands. Teacher's demonstration: a **pen** — a straight pen is measured as is; a bent line is mentally straightened and then measured. Needs some judgement ("idea").

4. If the line starts a bit later but is from the **Moon mount**: it has often been seen that **work started after marriage**.
   - The teacher noted marriage need not come later; formerly family was good, so the boy was married first; now people **first look at work, then marriage**. For marriage, other lines' impressions must also be checked.

> [!EXAMPLE] Arvind ji
> - **Who:** Arvind ji (Student, Speaker 6).
> - **Observed/Reported:** His line starts "from the top" (higher up, not from the base).
> - **Interpretation given:** Matches the rule — **he married first and began work afterwards**.
> - **Outcome:** He confirmed; the teacher accepted it.

## 13. Stopping and restarting fate line; second fate line

### 13.1 Line that stops and starts again
- Fate line goes, **stops**, goes again, stops, goes again → **career stops, then resumes**, repeated.
- Examples of such careers: **contract work** — you go for some time, contract ends, a gap, another contract, ends again.
- **Use:** when someone comes for guidance, advise work **suited to this pattern**, "because such work will run for you."

### 13.2 Second fate line starting
Two conditions for a second fate line starting while the first continues:

- **Overlapping (no break):** The career keeps running; **no break is shown**. This is read as: at this age, while in this career, the person **started preparing for another career** — e.g. created another source of **investment/income**. **Example:** Arvind ji said he bought a **property to give on rent** — that became a second source of income.
- **Where the first line ends and the second continues:** there **is** a break (the earlier line ends). The first career is finished; the second starts and sometimes the person **leaves the first altogether**.

> [!NOTE] Related remark
> This is the same logic as in the life line (earlier class).

### 13.3 Where the second line starts from — inside or outside
| Second line starts | Reasoning | Prediction |
|---|---|---|
| **From inside** (toward the life-line side) | Very likely to **join or touch the life line** | Career connected to **motherland/native place**; or **family business**; or two other family members join together; or another work done with **someone's support** |
| **From outside** (toward the Sun mount) | See **how far outward** it goes; if it enters the **Sun mount area** | **Name, fame, prasiddhi**; you become known; you give **output in others' work**; your words are heard (e.g. in an office, you rise one level and your words are now listened to) |

### 13.4 Third condition — two separate lines joined by a line from the middle
- The two lines are separate, but a line from the middle joins them. How to read: **estimate it yourself like reading planets** — "10th lord gone to the 3rd; 10th lord gone to the 8th."
