# Saturn Finger (Śani) and Sun Finger (Sūrya)

> [!INFO] How a Finger Is Read
> Every finger is analyzed through **three parameters**:
> 1. **Length** (लंबाई)
> 2. **Phalanges** (पर्व) — which phalanx is weak/open, which is thick or thin
> 3. **Base position** — whether the base sits higher or lower relative to the other fingers

---

## Saturn Finger (शनि की उंगली / मध्यमा — Madhyamā, the middle finger)

### Length: No Comparison Finger
When Jupiter finger was studied earlier, its length was compared against the **Sun finger**. The **Saturn finger is not compared to any other finger**, because Saturn is naturally the longest finger in the hand (this can vary slightly person to person, but generally across all hands Saturn is the longest). So for Saturn, only its **base** is examined.

### Base Position
- Sometimes a finger's base sits lower or higher than the others.
- A **low-set base is generally rare in Saturn**; it is seen more often in **Mercury** and, to some extent, **Jupiter**. In Mercury the base may be noticeably low; in Jupiter it can go quite low in some hands.
- Saturn rarely shows this low-base pattern.

### General Significations of Saturn Finger
> [!INFO] Core Meaning
> Saturn is the **karaka (significator) of karma (कर्म)**. Every finger reflects karma to some degree, but a finger alone is never used to judge career — that is read primarily from the **lines**, which give the actual results.

- The finger shows the person's **thinking/attitude toward karma** — e.g., some people are purely karma-oriented (action-focused), some are more fatalistic ("जितना भाग्य में होगा उतना मिल जाएगा" — whatever is destined will come, so why put in effort), and some are lazy about even small tasks.
- "Karma" here does not mean only earning money — it also covers a person's **daily routine**, morning-to-evening activities.
- Saturn finger is also related to **wealth (धन)**, though this must be combined with karma-reading, not viewed in isolation.
- Saturn finger indicates:
  - **Maturity** — how serious the person is about life
  - **Practicality**
  - **Responsibility** toward life, family, and relationships
  - Saturn makes a person a **researcher** — hence in Kālapuruṣa Kuṇḍalī, Saturn is the **sthira kāraka (permanent significator) of the 8th house**, since the 8th house relates to research
  - **Hard work**, but also **delay, disappointment**, work getting late, sorrow, and **health problems**

> [!NOTE] Wealth and Hand Quality
> Wealth should never be judged from the finger alone — the **quality of the hand** matters too. A very rough/hard hand means effort (मेहनत) will increase no matter how good the lines are, because "the hand is the soil (भूमि) in which the lines grow." Even a very good wealth-line configuration will underperform if the hand's quality (softness, cleanliness, good mounts) is poor. Generally, a clean, soft hand with good fingers and mounts indicates a good wealth position. (The wealth chapter itself comes later.)

### Length Categories

#### Normal Length Saturn Finger
A "normal" length is judged relative to the hand — neither unusually long nor unusually short.

> [!TIP] Judging "Normal" vs "Very Long"
> In long, thin hands, an unusually long Saturn finger is more **noticeable** at a distance; in heavier/broader hands, this is less obvious. Judge each hand relative to its own proportions.

Traits of a person with a **normal** Saturn finger:
- Balanced Saturn qualities — not too little, not obsessive
- Responsible, karma-oriented
- Works in a way that **does not harm the other person while still getting their own work done** — i.e., doesn't take the wrong path
- Shows understanding/wisdom (समझदारी) when given responsibility; follows **rules, regulations, and traditions**
- Can be called a person of **discretion (विवेक)** — in palmistry, discretion is read both from Jupiter and from Saturn
- Good for wealth (with the caveats above about hand quality)

#### Very Long Saturn Finger
> [!WARNING] Suspicious Nature
> A person with a Saturn finger noticeably **longer than normal** tends to be **suspicious (शक्की)** by nature.

- Astrological parallel given by the teacher: like Saturn retrograde in the 5th house, lord of the 6th house.
- Numerology parallel: **Root 8, Destiny 8, with double 8 / triple 8 in root.**
- Even minor things feel to them like a "conspiracy against me."
- Can be **inclined toward wrong deeds** — "काम का भूत चढ़ जाना" (becoming obsessed with getting something done, by any means). A normal-finger person also works hard, but through the right method; this person's method turns wrong.
- Overthinking → **mentally disturbed**; "ना खुद जिएंगे, ना जीने देंगे" (neither at peace themselves, nor let others be at peace)
- Little connection with emotions
- **Speech/tone is also not pleasant**; described as having a "cold nature" — no visible facial/emotional expression
- Very high seriousness, high maturity, but **lower responsibility** than the normal finger
- Prefers **isolation** — being alone in a room, not talking to anyone, absorbed in their own work
- The exact expression is further modified by which direction the finger is **tilted** (see below).

#### Short Saturn Finger
> [!CAUTION] Rare But Significant
> A truly very short Saturn finger is rare (since Saturn's base size is naturally large). It shows up as: the Saturn finger ending around the same height as where the person's Jupiter finger ends, with Sun finger also at that same height — i.e., Saturn barely rises above Jupiter and Sun. (The teacher notes she has rarely/never seen a hand where the Sun finger rises above Saturn.)

- **Discretion/judgment (विवेक शक्ति) is almost absent** — little sense of responsibility, e.g., toward the family
- Compared to the mindset of people from "lower class who commit wrongful acts" — the teacher clarifies this refers to a *mindset*, not a social class judgment
- Generally **does not follow tradition**; tends to be a **rule-breaker**, with no real regard for family honor/reputation
- Carries **negativity** and negative feelings toward others internally
- A short Saturn finger by itself would still increase struggle/hard work in life, but the *feeling* of wanting to work is missing — a long finger drives action despite the struggle; a short finger removes the internal motivation to act at all
- If Saturn is additionally **afflicted (पीड़ित)**, weak (अस्तु), and the **Moon is also weak**, then the person will have no inner drive to do anything at all
- This finger type is also **suspicious**, like the long finger, but here the suspicion can lead to **actually committing wrong deeds** (e.g., committing harm out of suspicion) — though for that, **Mars must also be checked**, since courage for such acts comes from Mars
- Saturn also governs **longevity (आयु)** — a short/afflicted Saturn finger can indicate problems here, including **shortened lifespan** through poor food/drink habits, alcohol, etc.
- **Addiction** is possible with a short finger too, but its *pattern* differs by finger type:

| Finger condition | Addiction pattern |
|---|---|
| Very long Saturn finger | Addiction runs from morning to evening continuously |
| Short Saturn finger | Addiction occurs twice a week |
| (third comparison type) | Addiction occurs daily in the evening |

> [!NOTE] The teacher asked students to distinguish these three patterns themselves based on what was taught.

### Tilt / Inclination of Saturn Finger
As with Jupiter finger (which could tilt toward the Thumb or toward Saturn), the **Saturn finger has two possible tilt directions**: toward **Jupiter** or toward the **Sun** finger.

> [!TIP] How to Check
> Hold the hand straight (not tilted as a whole) and observe which direction the Saturn finger leans — this is different from a naturally crooked/tilted hand.

#### Saturn Tilted Toward Jupiter
- Adds **responsibility** to one's karma; **discretion (विवेक शक्ति)** in the work/career field increases
- Person becomes more **serious**, may develop a **philosophical mindset**
- Work may become connected to **Jupiter-type themes**; desire for people to know, recognize, and respect them
- **Caution:** if the Jupiter finger is also long, this tilt can **increase ego (अहंकार)**
- Saturn's natural tendency toward **solitude (एकांत)** remains, but this solitude is now combined with **optimism** — the person's mindset stays positive even in solitude, "because Jupiter is ahead of it"
- In the birth chart, this often corresponds to a **Guru-Shani yuti (Jupiter-Saturn conjunction)**

> [!EXAMPLE] Live Discussion
> A student (Jīveś jī) mentioned having a Guru-Shani yuti in their chart along with a Jupiter finger tilted toward Saturn. The teacher assigned **homework**: post the exact **degrees** of both Jupiter and Saturn in the group, so she could study the correlation further. Another student (Aravind jī) noted they also have a Guru-Shani yuti with Jupiter, Saturn, and Mercury all conjunct ("चारों ही युति").

#### Saturn Tilted Toward Sun
Sun = the king (राजा); Saturn = the subjects/labor class (प्रजा). Saturn moving toward Sun means:
- **Confidence level decreases**, but **hard work (मेहनत) increases**, since Saturn represents karma-orientation
- (This effect is only *confirmed* after also checking the **thumb** and the **length of the Sun finger** — if the Saturn finger is itself short and tilted, the drop in confidence will be more pronounced, with pervasive negativity: "I don't even know if I can start this work, let alone finish it, and if it's handed to me, how will I carry it through.")
- Saturn also represents **traditions, rules, and regulations** — so this person will be somewhat **afraid of wrongdoing**
- **Risk-taking ability decreases**, because the person is conscious of social rules and fears **defamation**
- This fear reduces **only** when the Sun finger is very long, along with a strong thumb (willpower) and a well-formed Jupiter finger — in that case the person develops enough self-assurance that "no one can say anything to me," even regarding wrongdoing
- Positive trait: such people tend to maintain a **good balance between personal and professional life**

## Sun Finger (सूर्य / Ravi Finger)

### Length & Comparison Method
- Sun finger length is compared against **Jupiter finger** (as taught previously).
- **Normal length:** should reach roughly to a little above the **first phalanx of the Saturn finger** — going slightly higher than that is fine.

### General Significations of Sun Finger
Sun governs (Kālapuruṣa parallels noted by the teacher — Sun is lord of the 5th house):
- **Name and fame (मान-सम्मान)**
- **Confidence level**
- **Self-knowledge / self-realization**
- **Discretion (विवेक शक्ति)**, **wisdom**
- **Royal lifestyle**
- **Creativity** (strongly, since Sun rules the 5th house in Kālapuruṣa)
- **Perfection** — how finely/meticulously a person wants to execute a task
- **Distinguishing right from wrong** — like a king who must stop wrongdoing and promote what is right, ensuring justice for all — even if advised by a guru or minister, the final decision/duty is the king's own
- **Self-introspection (आत्मनिरीक्षण)**
- **Plainspokenness / weight of one's word (स्पष्टवादिता)** — how much conviction is behind what is said; whether one's word becomes "a line etched in stone." If a parent tells a child not to go out after 10 PM and the child doesn't listen, it means the parent's Sun finger/quality is weak in this sense — their words carry no weight
- **Initiating things** — Sun rises before anyone else wakes; a person with a good Sun finger has no hesitation about being the one to start something, without feeling "why should I have to start, I am the king"

### Bent/Crooked Sun Finger
> [!WARNING] Two Causes of Crookedness
> As taught earlier, a finger can appear crooked for **two reasons**: (1) the finger itself is naturally bent, or (2) it has **knots (गांठें)** at the joints, which can make it *look* bent.
> - If due to **knots**: this can make the person a **philosopher** in that finger's particular field.
> - If due to a genuine **defect/deformity**: it gives **negative** effects related to that finger.

If the Sun finger itself is crooked (due to defect):
- Excessive **ego/arrogance**
- Inability to distinguish right from wrong at the relevant time; **wrong decisions**
- Reduced name/fame — Sun's positive qualities diminish
- Confidence may appear greater than it actually is — praise from others inflates the ego disproportionately

### Length Categories

#### Normal Sun Finger
Reaches the middle of Saturn's first phalanx, or slightly above it.
- All the positive Sun qualities present: gains respect **and** gives respect in return (accepting respect without reciprocating it would itself indicate a Sun-finger problem)
- Respects even a small child's good qualities/achievements
- Accepts others' respect gracefully; also **accepts their own mistakes**
- Their words carry weight, but they **do not impose** their views forcefully — "not a ruler who dictates"
- (Note: an overly long Jupiter finger can also indicate a domineering/dictating tendency, but that is a separate case)
- Wants to **acquire knowledge**, and shares knowledge only as much as necessary; tolerant of generational gaps (e.g., accepting that today's youth may be 20–30 years apart from parents in outlook, without dismissing them as simply "wrong")
- Highly **creative**; appreciates art and artists, does not belittle art or entertainment
- **Very self-satisfied** — Jupiter finger is also checked for this quality, since Jupiter relates to satisfaction too
- Good **willpower** — does not give up easily

#### Very Long Sun Finger
A finger noticeably longer than normal (reaching further up than the standard middle-finger-first-phalanx marker):
- All Sun qualities become **excessive**
- Anger increases — not just on genuinely wrong matters, but **finds fault even with correct things**; argues, insists, gets into disputes over small matters
- Demands respect/name-fame even if it is not freely given — "get me my respect," seeking respect by asserting dominance/authority rather than earning it naturally
- May resort to **wrong paths** to obtain name and fame
- Remains creative, but becomes **self-praising** about their own creativity/talent; ego increases
- Example given: at a wedding, a normal-finger person would invite a performing artist with full respect and personally; a person with an overly long Sun finger might avoid direct contact, sending someone else, fearing "meeting them personally might make me look smaller"
- **Authority-seeking**, hungrier for **power** and **success**, wants only the highest possible position ("मुझे तो बिल्कुल जो सबसे ऊंचा पद होगा ना, वही चाहिए")
- Numerology parallel: **Destiny 1, Root 1, multiple 1's**

#### Short Sun Finger
Compared against Jupiter finger and against Saturn's middle portion — shorter than both:
- **Low confidence** — the only partial exception is if the **thumb** (specifically the willpower area, the upper mount) appears puffed/full, which can give a little confidence despite a short Sun finger
- Otherwise: low willpower, **loss of ability to distinguish right and wrong** (doesn't even think about it)
- Becomes a **follower**, not a self-starter — will act only if someone else initiates or instructs
- **Almost no sense of creativity** — unless the **Mercury finger** is good, in which case some creativity can come from there instead
- **Health issues** — Sun governs **immunity**, so Sun-related health problems appear here
- Reduced discretion/wisdom; reluctance to acquire knowledge, even reading/writing
- Low confidence especially visible in social/public settings — e.g., if guests arrive at home and the person must speak in front of 4–5 people, they will be found sitting quietly in a corner, avoiding direct communication
- People with a very short Sun finger tend to **quarrel over small matters**, due to an internal complex/inferiority feeling

### Tilt / Inclination of Sun Finger
Two possible tilt directions: toward **Saturn**, or toward **Mercury**.

#### Sun Tilted Toward Saturn
Sun = king; Saturn = subject/labor class.
- **Confidence level decreases**
- But **hard work increases** (Saturn = karma-oriented)
- Increases **negativity**; this effect is confirmed together with thumb condition and Sun-finger length — a short Sun finger with this tilt shows much lower confidence and pervasive self-doubt about even starting or completing tasks
- Since Saturn also governs **traditions and rules**, this person will be somewhat **afraid of wrongdoing**
- **Risk-taking ability decreases**, from fear of social judgment/defamation
- This fear disappears only if the Sun finger is very long **and** the thumb/willpower and Jupiter finger conditions are also good — such a person may then feel "no one can say anything to me" even regarding wrongdoing
- Positive trait: good **balance between personal and professional life**

#### Sun Tilted Toward Mercury
Sun = confidence; Mercury = communication, education, speech.
- The person's inner confidence/self-strength becomes **expressible through communication** — they can articulate their inner state to others
- Since Mercury is lord of the **3rd house** (representing society, neighbors, the world just outside one's home), this combination brings a drive toward **new ideas/innovations for society**
- Combination of Sun's creativity + Mercury's communication → creativity may express as **design**, **social media presence/fame**, etc.
- Reduced excess "seriousness" — Mercury represents a child (referred to in tradition as the "prince," राजकुमार) — so even a strict/serious person with this combination will show a softer, more playful side (e.g., playing with children after office hours)

> [!TIP] Sub-case — Gap Between Sun and Mercury Fingers
> Two configurations are possible when the Mercury finger tilts toward Sun:
> 1. Mercury finger stays **stuck/close** to the Sun finger while tilting.
> 2. Mercury finger **separates**, creating a visible **gap** at the top (not at the base) between Sun and Mercury fingers.
>
> A visible gap makes the person **bold** — willing to take **major life steps that no one else in their family has taken**. Mercury (the "child") pulling away from all the other fingers reflects a personality that pulls away and takes independent, sometimes rule-breaking, major decisions.

> [!EXAMPLE] Case Study — CA Professional Who Changed Careers
> A woman the teacher had read for was training as (and had become) a **Chartered Accountant**, but later made a complete career pivot: she left her CA career, left two subsequent businesses, and started a third on her own initiative. This matched a Mercury finger clearly separated from the other fingers with a visible gap, reaching outward. She also now **travels alone** to many places, without needing family/sibling support.

## 4️⃣ Audience Q&A

> [!FAQ]- Q1: "What accounts for apparent color differences (e.g., reddish vs. whitish) in the hand photos being studied?"
> **A:** This is generally a **lighting effect** from how/where the photo was taken (e.g., poor overhead lighting), not a genuine skin-tone reading feature — similar to how the same object can appear different colors under different photography conditions.

> [!FAQ]- Q2: "Which line was being referred to when mentioning that a good/bad line outcome depends on 'the line'?"
> **A:** Lines are covered in a **future, dedicated set of lectures** — there are too many lines to explain in passing at this stage. Any effect that depends on "the line" is left unexplained for now until lines (रेखाएं) are taught later.

> [!FAQ]- Q3: "During Demonstration 1, was the marking on the Jupiter finger's second phalanx a 'rectangle' shape?"
> **A:** No — this is simply a **संधि रेखा (Sandhi Rekhā / joint line)**, a normal feature present on this kind of phalanx, not a special marking to be interpreted.

> [!FAQ]- Q4: "During Demonstration 1, did this person's life show more hardship and a lack of comfort despite having money?"
> **A:** Confirmed as an accurate read: the subject has money, but not the corresponding **comfort** in life — visible from the hand's dense, cluttered lines ("traffic jam") despite a strong Venus mount. Hand quality (softness, cleanliness) reflects comfort level separately from wealth indicators.

> [!FAQ]- Q5: "During Demonstration 2, what was the gender and age of the hand shown?"
> **A:** Students guessed male, roughly 50–55 years old. The actual subject was a **75-year-old woman** — a deliberate illustration that a soft, clean hand (high "hand quality") can look much younger than its true age.

> [!SUMMARY] Key Takeaways from This Lecture
> - Saturn finger: judged mainly by **length category** (normal/long/short) and **tilt** (toward Jupiter or Sun); no comparison finger is used, since Saturn is naturally the longest.
> - Sun finger: judged by **length compared to Jupiter/Saturn's first phalanx**, by **crookedness**, and by **tilt** (toward Saturn or Mercury).
> - A **gap** between Sun and Mercury fingers signals boldness and willingness to take major, independent life decisions.
> - Wealth and comfort should never be judged from a single finger alone — always cross-check against overall **hand quality** and (eventually) the **lines**.
> - Detailed line-reading is reserved for future lectures.
