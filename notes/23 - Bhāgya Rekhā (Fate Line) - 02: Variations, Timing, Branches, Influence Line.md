# Hasta Sāmudrika Śāstra – Fate Line (Career Line): Variations, Timing, Branches, Influence Line

> [!NOTE] Scope and terminology
> Lecture continues from the previous class (Moon mount, maṇibandha/wrist, fate line vs life line). The teacher's own terms are kept: **Fate Line** (also called *career line*), **Life Line**, **Mind Line / Brain Line / Headline** (used interchangeably for the same line), **Heart Line**, **Influence Line**, **Ambition Line**, **Healing Line**. Reading is organised by *what the fate line does* and *which line/mount it touches*. Word "**line**" below = *rekhā*.
> Two senses to avoid confusing: "Venus/Moon **mount**" (a location on the palm) vs the "**line from** Venus/Moon" (where a line starts).

## 2. Doubled lines (two life lines, two fate lines)

### 2.1 Two Life Lines
> [!EXAMPLE] Case: Sushant Singh Rajput's hand (shown earlier by the teacher)
> - **Who:** Sushant Singh Rajput (celebrity example, hand sent earlier by teacher). **Hand:** not stated.
> - **Observed:** two life lines.
> - **Student question:** If two life lines, how do we explain that he died by suicide?
> - **Interpretation given:** Suicide is **not** read from two life lines. Two life lines do **not** indicate death or length of life.
> - **Reasoning / Meaning:** Two life lines = **a double life**: two kinds of life, complete **pattern change**, possibly **personality change**; "up-down, up-down" life; possibly living in **two places**. This shows when a line goes outward and joins another.
> - **Outcome:** Teacher also noted the "two lines" idea applies to any line.
> - Before this, the student recalled the teacher had said she would explain two life lines "when I draw the lines"; teacher confirmed it had been mentioned.

### 2.2 Two Fate Lines → two careers / two income sources
- Any line can appear doubled. **Two fate lines = two careers or two income sources** (e.g., fixed career + part-time job; weekend work; after office hours teaching, tuition, coaching).
- Ways a second fate line can appear:
  1. Main fate line straight (as normal) **plus** another.
  2. Another fate line coming **from the Moon mount**.
  3. Another fate line coming **from Venus**.
  4. A fate line appearing **only after half the age** (middle of life).
- The **main fate line = the main career**.

| Second line starts from | Meaning given by teacher |
|---|---|
| **Moon** | Moon-related income: arts (Moon discussion in class on arts), share-market investing, income from interest given, house **rental income**. Many income sources possible. |
| **Venus** (from inside or touching the life line) | **Family work / family business**; family has supported; another income generated through family. |
| **Starts in the middle** | Age is calculated later when age systems are taught. Work was running up to that point; **in the 30s, 35 or 40** another income source was generated. |

## 3. Where the fate line ENDS: the line it stops at

### 3.1 Fate line stops at the Mind Line (Head Line)
> [!WARNING] Fate line ends touching/stopping at the Mind Line; nothing continues, no supportive line comes from elsewhere
> - **Meaning:** Career was good at the start, remained good till **around 40–50**. After that, a **mental problem** arose because of which the **career was left**, or a **wrong decision** affected the career.
> - **Everyday example:** "Someone got angry and quit the job; now no one is ready to give a job; sitting idle."
> - **Main responsibility is the Headline's.** Check its **quality**:
>   - **Wavy** (ups and downs), or very **dark**, or many tiny chain-like lines → **mentally/emotionally disturbed** (heart line also shows emotional disturbance).
>   - **Straight** headline → the more straight, the more **practical** (logical and practical).
> - **Cross-check** at the same age on the **life line**: a dark patch, a cross, or an island → "sudden event" that forced leaving the career.
> - **Then see what happens after** (this determines the rest):
>   | After the stop | Reading |
>   |---|---|
>   | Line continues **straight** | A **break of ~5–7 years**, then you did something in career/generated an income source. |
>   | Line comes in **pieces** (fragments) | Starts, something is lost, work again, something lost… repeated on-off. |
>   | **Broken pieces below, straight above** | Many breaks earlier, then career becomes very good: **career will be built after 40–50**; earlier difficulties and changes. |
>   | Line **good for a while, then not** / **short then good** | Any of these possible; predict according to what appears. |

### 3.2 Fate line stops at the Heart Line
- **Meaning:** a **relationship issue** (marital problem), a love/emotional issue, or a **health issue** (heart line shows health too) that **impacted the career**.
- What comes **after** (the line's condition afterwards) tells how things go: good → fine.

> [!IMPORTANT] Heart Line with many downward branches while the fate line goes straight
> - Many downward branches, **dark, broad** heart line with lines coming out of it → strong **emotional disturbance**.
> - Traditionally said to indicate an **extra-marital affair** (teacher: "this is what is said, but we will understand it by *deśa-kāla*", i.e. time and place).
> - Disturbance can concern **relationships, responsibilities, family, career**, anything.
> - If these branches **cut the fate line** → emotional disturbance **affects the career**. (Teacher's example: people get so worried about children/family that career is affected; "if something like that happened to me I would first cancel your classes. I am a career painter" **[unclear in transcript]**.)
> - If the line afterwards is good → *this patch of that age* disturbs you for that many years; afterwards good.
> - If these lines **grow and cut the life line too** → impact is now beyond career: **the whole home, family, in-laws** disturbed, and **health**, "because every event shows on the life line."

### 3.3 Fate line stops at / ends with an island or cross; square, triangle
| Sign | Meaning given |
|---|---|
| **Cross** on fate line | **Accident/calamity in career**, e.g., suddenly dismissed from the job. |
| **Square** (with the cross) | **Protection**: not "hand-to-mouth"; has **savings**, and could find a job by applying elsewhere. |
| **Island** | Difficulty in career in that time period. |
| **Triangle/square on life line or fate line** | See §4 (asset). |
| Teacher's comment | Because "difficulty" is repeated: it is **deśa-kāla** related; since the class has studied **Jyotiṣa**, the subject is easier. |

## 5. Branches from the fate line

> [!INFO] Branch from the fate line
> A branch can leave from **anywhere** and at **any age**; at the age it leaves, it shows what you do.

| Branch goes toward | Reading |
|---|---|
| **Mercury** (this is *not* the Mercury line; Mercury line to be taught later) | At that age you think of **business**, further **study**, learning new things and implementing them → work gets better. Mercury-related work helpful for career. |
| **Jupiter** | Along with the career, **teaching**, spirituality, **storyteller**, **motivational speaker**; whatever is Jupiter's domain enters the career. |
| **Sun** | **Name, fame** increase at that age (see §6). |

> [!WARNING] Common mistake: trident vs two-branched (dvishākhita) line
> - A line that at the very **end** forks into two with a *bent* shape is **not a trident**: it is **dvishākhita rekhā** (two-branched line), one line's two branches going in two directions.
> - **Trident (triśūl)** has a **V-shape** like a trident, seen **distinctly at the very end** of the line.
> - Example: **Ravi ji's** hand photo with markings (sun line): teacher confirmed it **is a real trident**.
> - The effect of a two-branched line: the influence of **each mount it goes to** shows on the fate line.

> [!NOTE] Ambition line link
> Earlier taught: a line from the **life line toward Jupiter** = **Ambition line** (desire/ambition). A branch to Jupiter works similarly; **the age at which it works** must be checked on career: **~50 and above**.

### 5.1 Many branches at the end (upward) – scattered energy
> [!WARNING]
> - Fate line ending in **many branches going upward**, long or short, possibly reaching other mounts: **not a trident**.
> - Gives **trouble in later career**: **energy scatters in all directions**, cannot focus on one thing.
> - Late-career thinking: "time to leave, do something so job continues, start business, do something else for the children" → this **troubles** and shows later-stage **career trouble**.
> - If these appear **low on the line (early age)**: **career trouble** at that age; need to **work very hard**.
> - If they appear on the **life line**: **multitasker** — but it **spoils health**; "the more work you do, the more health suffers in old age"; so good health needed. Example: **Dave ji** is a multitasker, strain on health.

## 6. Fate line (or any line) toward the Sun
> [!INFO] Any line reaching the Sun mount
> - Whatever line goes to the **Sun mount**, from anywhere, **is the Sun line**. Do not mistake a faint line for a **Mars line**.
> - At that age: **name and fame** increase.

- Fate line turning to **Sun** at **later age**: a **government job** is unlikely so late; but possible: **politics** (people mostly enter politics in old age), **government-related work** (government-linked business, **solar** work, **contract** work).
- Name/fame after 50 often comes **through children** becoming successful; parents get praised with them.
- **Most hands**: Sun line appears **above the Heart Line**. Rare hands show Sun line **from Moon or Venus**: that person has **always had fame**; their **will power is strong**, a **desire** is visible.

## 7. Age calculation (Timing systems) – as taught here
> [!IMPORTANT] Teacher's method
> 1. Total span: general hand → **0 to 75 (or 80)**. Old kāmis (ancients) took **100 years**; now not used. Example: marriage region earlier taken as **50 years** on the lines, but because marriage is now delayed it is taken as **55–56** (recent theory). "Some variation by *deśa-kāla*."
> 2. **Dave ji** (class member) is about **85** (a student said 84; teacher: "fine, 85"). Since 75 was assumed for life-line calculation, for him the span is extended (teacher says "we will go with **91**" **[unclear in transcript]**).
> 3. **Zero** is taken at the **maṇibandha** side, not where the line starts: "even if your line starts from elsewhere, calculation starts from zero here". As taught the previous day: **straighten that line and calculate**.
> 4. Take **half** of the span: use the **paper method on the screen** (fold/place paper) for exactness. **Middle is always 30**; then halve again. Example given "52" and "half of 52" **[unclear in transcript]**.
> 5. For learning, one may also **check by incidents** in one's own hand.

> [!NOTE] Another approach to ages on the fate line

## 8. Fate line absent, starting from Head/Heart Line, or merging with Life Line

### 8.1 No fate line / starts late
- Fate line may be **absent**, or start from the **Head Line** or **Heart Line**, or other places.
- From the **Head Line**: calculate age; earlier maybe **no career**, **career begins after 40–45**.
- **Absent line ≠ no career (not 100%)**: from where a good fate line starts, **focus on career and planning** increases.
- If the line before is very **dark**: the person was **not focused** on career.
- From the **Heart Line** and going upward: career starts **after 50–55**, **self-started** or with **children's support** ("papa, we have done this work; join us").

> [!EXAMPLE] Teacher's other hand (unnamed)
> - **Observed:** good **life line**; dark patch on the fate-line area; later the line is quite good.
> - **Interpretation:** substantial **property, father, home**; **work has not started** because no need and not much focus on career; **later** the person will think of starting something **on his own**.

### 8.2 Family support: line from the Life Line
- **Where** does the line of children's/family support come from? → from the **life line** (teacher asked the class; answered "Life line").
- Possible patterns: a line touches the life line and goes up; or starts from it.
- **Reading tip (looks like "fate line appears, vanishes, reappears"):** read it as the **fate line merged with the life line** and then **ran separately**.
- Meaning for a **career question**: earlier career was **supported by family** (a job through acquaintances, family business, etc.).
- **Age is NOT counted on the life line** (that would give ~20 years); count on the **fate line** → shows **after 40**: you started working **independently**: "leave the family, enough of them, do something of my own."

### 8.3 Wavy / snake-like fate line
- **Ups and downs** in career; career does **not stop**, but **changes**; many changes in the same field.

### 8.4 Wavy line low on the palm
- Early career **troubled**; **many job changes**, **travel** (wavy = travel; part goes toward **Moon**, then toward **Venus**, then Moon).
- After this the line goes straight → **stability** later on the fate line.

> [!EXAMPLE] Harishchandra's friend's hand (age ~22–23, works)
> - **Who:** friend of Harishchandra (age 26); unnamed. **Hand:** not stated.
> - **Observed:** **no clear fate line**; faint at start, then it **merges into the life line** (student's observation; teacher: will look at it enlarged).
> - **Teacher:** no fate line ≠ no career. Since he is doing **family work**, read as **merge** with life line; student confirmed he is doing family work.

## 11. Age on the Heart Line (separate method)
> [!TIP] Heart Line age – the phalanx method (teacher's calculation)
> 1. Heart Line is used to see **emotional disturbance** or **health issues** (heart etc.).
> 2. Divide along the line by the **phalanges** of the fingers: **18 years** for a phalanx area, then a **6-year** gap, then **18**, **6**, **18**, **6**… until the line ends. The line ends at whatever age is reached.
> 3. Varies by hand.
> 4. Example: a **downfall** of the line = **major heartbreak**. Teacher's working: 18 + 6 = **24**, plus half of 18 ("14") **[unclear in transcript]** → event around **38–40**. Then look for the same age on the **life line** and **fate line** (career disturbance).

- For **Sun line**, teacher does not use 50 as fixed; she uses **0 to 75**, and calculates by the **question asked**.

## 12. Influence Line (Marriage/relationship influence)

### 12.1 Marriage question (class interaction)
- Teacher asked **Harishchandra (26)**: he is **unmarried**, no past relationship. Teacher said she was asking because a hand in the class (belonging to a **new member**) had this line.

> [!INFO] Influence line
> A **straight line joining the fate line from the side**. Used to see **relationship (love), partner, role model**, a **female's influence**, career **partnership**, **love marriage**.

| Influence line behaviour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Comes up to the fate line, **does not touch** | No effect on career: influence was brief (e.g., "I want to be like that person," thought for some time then dropped), or love that **never converted into marriage** (line did not continue). |
| **Touches** the fate line | Influence on **career**; someone whose influence runs **along with your life**; **indicator of love marriage / relationship**. Teacher to Harishchandra: if the line is in that age, a **love marriage** is possible. |
| **Cuts** the fate line and goes out | **Disturbed relation**; trouble from role model; **trouble in love affairs**; **not good for females**; disturbed relation, and sometimes **physical abuse**. Age as read on the fate line. |

- Age is read on the **fate line (career)**, when you are thinking of career/study. **Many such lines in one period are bad lines.**

> [!FAQ] Q (student): Can an influence line show an extra-marital affair?
> **A (Teacher):** Check **age**: e.g., married at 25–26, influence line appears after; **many lines disturbing married life**, or the line **sloping downward toward the heart line** (emotional disturbance) → possible; but say it **only once, not every time**; **all combinations** must be seen: **Venus mount badly afflicted**; for a female, **Jupiter**; **Heart Line and Mind Line wavy** (emotionally disturbed).
> - **How to ask:** first ask about **career**: was there someone's influence, a role, a partnership? If the client says no to everything, then something remains; "the line tells; the hand does not lie."
