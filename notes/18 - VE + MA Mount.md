# Śukra Parvata (Venus Mount) and Maṅgala Parvata (Mars Mount) – Lecture Notes

> [!NOTE] Conventions used in these notes
> - Notes follow the lecture in the exact order taught.
> - The teacher uses three states for every mount: **sāmānya** (normal), **atividikasita** (over-developed), **nimna** (low / below normal). For Venus, *nimna* means deficient. For Mars, *nimna maṅgala* also names the **Lower Mars** position (see below) – do not confuse the two uses.

# 1. Śukra Parvata (Venus Mount)

## 1.1 Location

- On the thumb side of the palm.
- It is the most swollen mount and the one given the most space on the hand.

## 1.2 Space (area) given to Venus

> [!INFO] Definition – how Venus's space is judged
> The space a person's hand gives to Venus is not judged by how much the mount bulges. It **depends on how much space the jīvana rekhā (Life Line) gives it**.

- In every case Venus looks bigger than other mounts, but in one hand it may be less and in another more.
- Demonstrated by touching the hand: the Life Line may run close to the thumb, closer still, or very far from it.
- Rule: **the more space Venus gets, the more the results connected with Venus come in life; the less space, the fewer results.**

> [!IMPORTANT] Dominant (destiny) hand vs active hand
> - Venus may have more space in the **destiny hand** but less space in the **active hand**.
> - Meaning: at birth God sent the person with all comforts and facilities, but **how much the person can actually enjoy and use them, and whether he can fulfil his desires, is shown by the active hand's Venus.**

## 1.3 Condition of the mount (rising and sinking)

Two things are examined for Venus: (1) space/area, (2) the rising or sinking of the mount.

| State | Appearance |
|---|---|
| Normal (sāmānya) | Normal condition |
| Over-developed (atividikasita) | Looks like a separate piece of flesh placed on the hand; visible at once |
| Low (nimna) | Below normal, pressed/depressed |

> [!EXAMPLE] Photographs shown
> 1. **Hand 1** – Venus below the normal level: a **depressed Venus** (understood at a glance). Falls in the *nimna* category.
> 2. **Hand 2** – Venus is raised, but **not in good condition**: its **area has become smaller**, and there are **folds/wrinkles** on the mount. These indicate that Venus's condition is not good. Raised, yes – but a poor condition.
> - Teacher asked students to look for such hands among themselves; a Venus in good condition (normal at least) will be recognized.

> [!NOTE] Summary of the three categories
> Venus, like the other mounts, has the same three categories: **normal**, **over-developed**, **below normal (nimna)**.

## 1.4 Qualities of Venus – in order taught

### (a) Desire (icchā)

> [!INFO] Venus gives desire
> Venus gives desire – many wishes. Desire can be physical, mental or emotional.

| State | Result |
|---|---|
| Normal | Desires of any kind remain **controlled**. The person is not mad after his desires and knows how to control them |
| Over-developed | Desire increases; it can become a **madness** – one cannot stay without it even a minute. Any desire that becomes a source of trouble in any situation is shown by an over-developed Venus. Such people are also called **vyabhicārī** in ordinary astrological language |
| Low (nimna) | Two possibilities (see below) |

> [!EXAMPLE] Examples given by the teacher
> - **Potato paratha** – One person likes aloo paratha for breakfast and wishes to get it daily (a desire). With over-developed Venus, even on a one-day journey he will need aloo paratha there too, and be upset if not getting it. 
> - **Desire to learn and study** can also go to madness (mental level).
> - **Instagram reels** – watching reel after reel and wasting time can also be due to an over-developed Venus.
> - **Physical work** – such people do less physical labour.
> - **New phone** – Bought a phone two–three days ago; hears from a friend of a newer phone and wants that too. Buying a phone is a desire anyone can have, including the normal type, but the normal type will use the phone for 3–4–5 years until it runs. The over-developed type thinks "a new one has come – leave this, take the other." Anything new in the market is wanted.

> [!CAUTION] Low (nimna) Venus – two possible conditions
> 1. **Desire is more than needed but is not getting fulfilled.** (Teacher stressed: two separate things – desire is very high, but unfulfilled.)
> 2. **No inclination for anything at all** – no wish to dress up or adorn oneself, no desire for anything. "The phone is running, it will do; what do we need, old age is coming; the children buy things." The inner feelings are not expressed at all; the person has completely finished them off.

### Area vs condition of the mount (two perspectives)

- Imagine a low-condition Venus with a **large area**. Large area means the person can have comforts and facilities ("when the scope/range of Venus increases, you can get facilities").
- But if Venus is a bit pressed, or – as in the hand shown earlier – the **skin of the Venus area is shrunken**, or **a little blackness** is seen: area has been taken, i.e. the person has house, property, everything – but **how much can he use it, how happy can he stay with it**; he cannot enjoy it all.
- The mount should be **fully raised and shining (chamakdār)**.
- Therefore Venus must be seen from **two angles: area-wise, and the condition of the mount.**

### (b) Luxury and enjoyment (bhoga-vilāsa)

> [!INFO] Luxury/enjoyment is also a quality of Venus.

| State | Result |
|---|---|
| Normal | Has all luxuries and handles them well; does not let them dominate the mind; likes new things but does not use them in a wrong direction. E.g., mobile phone becomes a **tool of use**, not of **addiction** |
| Over-developed | Addiction. Many people are seen talking – even in a meeting – and their phone keeps running. That is the over-developed story: it goes to the level of madness; **misuse** is possible |
| Low | Forget luxuries: shortage of money, desires not fulfilled, wants to buy but cannot; this causes **great frustration** |

> [!NOTE] Over-developed with large vs small area
> - Within the over-developed type, area can be large or small. The teacher said that when she later teaches the chapter on **rekhā (lines)** this must be joined to it: **a larger or smaller area changes the prediction.**
> - **Over-developed + large area:** such a person may have received everything by birth but uses it **only for luxury and ease**; addiction is very bad. Like parents who **spoon-feed** a child – that story applies.
> - **Good Venus + very soft hand** (watery hand, or fiery but soft-looking): physical labour is practically **zero**; he will not want to do it, or his surroundings will be such that he need not. He may do mental work, not physical.

> [!WARNING] Low Venus in the horoscope
> In the **patrikā (horoscope)** this may correspond to Venus being **asta** (combust) and **pīḍita** (afflicted): trouble in fulfilling desires.

> [!IMPORTANT] Always look at guru parvata (Jupiter Mount) with Venus
> - If Venus has a deficiency, the mount to look at to overcome it is **Jupiter**. Jupiter gives **satisfaction (santuṣṭi)**.
> - **Venus bad + Jupiter bad:** desires are not fulfilled and there is no contentment – both are useless.
> - **Venus bad + Jupiter at least good:** a feeling of contentment – "I am satisfied with what God gave me; I do not even imagine much more."
> - **Rule: before making a prediction, look carefully at all the surrounding indicators.**

### (c) Comfortable life

| State | Result |
|---|---|
| Normal | Generally a comfortable (āramdāyak) life – neither lacking anything nor having anything excessive |
| Low | Comforts become very few, they finish |

> [!EXAMPLE] The teacher's own classroom as illustration of "comfort"
> - Comfort can be understood in any way. She has set a fairly good atmosphere for teaching: the **fan is running, so she can teach easily; she has a white board, which makes it easy.**
> - If her Venus were good, her way of getting ready would be different; there might be a **microphone** in front of her, to which she would be habituated and without which she could not teach; she would have an **assistant** with her; she would not have to get up in between.
> - If it were low, teaching would be even more difficult. Everyone will understand comfort differently.

### (d) Sensuality, passion (kāmukatā)

| State | Result |
|---|---|
| Over-developed | Called **kāmuka** (sensual). Passion and desire increase greatly; **physical need** greatly increases. In their **gaze and in speech** one sees **double meaning**. This is somewhat bad; they may be **flirty** types – often those who are somewhat rich, have money, have show-off and flirt |
| Low | Nothing at all – they do it on **borrowed** means. Today they borrowed clothes, wore them, and flirted. This is visible because they **lack** the comforts of Venus |

### (e) Artistic nature

| State | Result |
|---|---|
| Normal | Can certainly be an artist – shows in gestures, in nature, in writing, in speech. E.g. cooking well and **decorating** the food; a way of **presenting**; **welcoming a guest** in an artistic way |
| Over-developed | The artistic way increases beyond need; **sensuality may be seen** in it. E.g. a painting preference: one may like nature paintings; another may like paintings with **figures** |
| Low | Because **Mars mount lies near Venus** (nimna Mars, to be read the same day), a low Venus can take the person in a **wrong direction**: thought runs on the same thing day and night; he flirts day and night; watches movies of that type; his actions also become so |

### (f) Relationships and married life

> [!INFO] Venus shows relationships in general, not only marriage.
> - **Good Venus:** married life good; relationships generally good.
> - For marriage, in palmistry **Mars** and **budha (Mercury)** are also looked at.

| State | Result |
|---|---|
| Normal | Relationships generally good; **generally no show-off** |
| Over-developed | Greed; **flattery (chāplūsī)** – "lalloh-chappo", smearing/plastering up each other – is more visible. (Teacher could not recall the exact word; the group supplied *chāplūsī*.) These people are **very spendy** and like to **give and receive gifts for show-off** in relationships; they show off everything |
| Low | Worried: "the way everyone lives, the way everyone gives gifts to each other – why can't I?" **Frustration** comes; **confidence level** also lowers |

### (g) Confidence

> [!NOTE] Venus is a big contributor to confidence
> **sūrya** (Sun) is the main one, but **Venus is also responsible.**

> [!EXAMPLE] Party with a colour theme
> - You go to a party where the theme says wear yellow or green clothes. If you do not wear them **only because you have no money** – "somebody else's wedding, and I buy clothes for myself?" – this happens every time. You get a **complex**; confidence goes down.
> - The person with low Venus keeps arranging: borrowing from here, from there, managing; and then feels **shame** in that gathering.
> - So a low Venus lowers confidence.

> [!IMPORTANT] If Venus is low, what must be good
> - **Jupiter mount** must be good; if not, there will be trouble; **or** the **will power (thumb)** should be good.
> - Whenever you see Venus, **always see will power.**
> - **Tie two–three things to Venus:**
>   1. **Tilt of the Jupiter finger toward Saturn** – can stop a person from doing wrong, and look together with the Venus mount.
>   2. **Will power, i.e. the thumb.**
> - **Combination described:** thumb **low-set**, **pent-up energy** present, Venus **very over-developed**, the **Jupiter finger not tilted toward Saturn**, **Mercury finger moves away from Sun** – such a person **can do wrong** if his **Mars** also shows that condition.
> - Teacher: "We do not read only Venus – we read all the conditions along with Venus."

### (h) Love (prema)

| State | Result |
|---|---|
| Normal | Love is good; ("don't talk about true/false now"); one can say **no show-off** in love; **not suspicious by nature**; gives the other **independence/freedom** – "you do what you like, live as you wish; it is not that I keep you controlled". **No controlling nature** |
| Over-developed | Not love for one: **handling two–three at the same time** may be seen. Sensuality is also there, but it can be **by the power of money**, and **for wrong things** |
| Low | The sensuality reaches the point of **burning** |

### (i) Childbirth

- Always look at Venus for childbirth – in **both male and female**.
- Normal: possibility of childbirth.
- Over-developed: possibility of childbirth also **good** – no problem.
- Low: **difficulty in childbirth**.

### (j) Kindness, speech, looks, health

- Confidence already told. Normal Venus: **kindness (dayā)**; speech **style** can be good; **good looks** are possible.
- The better the Venus mount, the better – because **the Venus area also shows the stomach** and **hormonal [Mars? – unclear in transcript]** (teacher said "hormonal Mars" about hormones). In the later **health** chapter, **especially the stomach is spoken of through Venus**. If the stomach is right, the base of health is right.

- **Illustration:** look at a film actor such as **Amrish Puri** – see a picture of him; these things become clear from there.

# 2. Maṅgala Parvata (Mars Mount)

> [!NOTE] Teacher: "First I am thinking to teach you Mars also today."

## 2.1 The two positions

Mars is at **two places** on the hand:

| Position | Hindi | Location (as demonstrated) | Neighbours |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lower Mars** (nimna maṅgala) | निम्न मंगल | The area near the thumb | Between **Jupiter** and **Venus** |
| **Upper Mars** (ucca maṅgala) | उच्च मंगल | Under the area of **Moon and Mercury**, a little lower | Together with **Mercury** and **candra** (Moon) |

- The teacher explained the upper Mars area by drawing a line to divide the region **Moon–Mercury: "see from the heart line, above the heart line this Mercury… dividing it in the middle with a line"** — this is not fully clear **[unclear in transcript]**.
- "In Hindi: call the lower *nimna*, the upper *ucca*."
- Because of these neighbours, **while predicting Mars, also look at the neighbouring mounts.**

## 2.2 How to read Lower Mars (nimna maṅgala) – checks before predicting

> [!TIP] Reading technique – three things to keep in mind
> 1. **Condition of the surrounding mounts** – especially **Jupiter** mount, and Venus particularly.
> 2. **Colour** – is the area **blackish or bluish**?
> 3. **Signs and symbols** (to be taught later) – hard to identify on this area.
> - Also: **how many lines** form a cluster here. Generally everyone has many lines here.
> - With the **thumb open** vs **closed**: demonstrated with two pictures. When the thumb is closed, the area gathers like a **mass**; some lines are there. **Compare both hands**: one hand's lower Mars may not be as raised as the active hand's; or it may sometimes be raised, sometimes depressed – you will understand yourself on looking.

### Lines rising from Lower Mars – "enemy lines"

> [!INFO] Enemy lines
> Lines rising from here are called **enemy lines**. In astrology (jyotiṣa) what we see from the **sixth house** is seen from **Lower Mars**.

- The more lines that rise from here and **cut any line** – Life Line, Heart Line, Mind Line – the more they **impact that area of life**.
- Example: a line goes from here and crosses the whole line – it has affected, at that age, **life (health), mindset and heart**.
- **Timing:** the **heart** effect comes at a **later age**, but the **mind and health** will be seen in the **early age of 10–15 years**.
- Lines are found rising here in everyone's hand; in some hands very many.
- A hand can look **completely plain** and yet the person is under **great tension.** Then there are two ways of looking:
  1. Clusters in this area (generally in everyone's hand).
  2. **Colour of lines** – some lines are light, some **dark**. Some hands show it more, some less.

## 2.3 Lower vs Upper Mars – the difference

> [!IMPORTANT] Appearance
> - The **upper Mars** can look **raised**: it protrudes slightly **outward**, to the side. The **middle area ("the Rahu area")** may be a little **depressed**. It can also be seen going upward or outward. **[unclear in transcript – "Rahu middle area"]**
> - **Picture shown:** the **lower Mars is raised, but the upper Mars is depressed.**

| | Lower Mars (nimna) | Upper Mars (ucca) |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | **Inner (āntarika)** strength – to *feel* courage | **Outer (bāhya)** – to *show* courage |
| Shows | Inner courage, will power (also seen from the Sun), strategy forming in mind | Ability to display retaliation (pratiśodha), act outwardly |
| Weak means | Person is trembling inside | Cannot show it outside |

> [!EXAMPLE] The power-cut example
> - Light goes off at home. One person **externally** shows he is running quickly to another room and arranging light (assume he is alone at home). 
> - Another **only appears** to have courage, but **inside he is trembling**. If inside he trembles, his **lower Mars is bad; upper Mars is good.**
> - **Upper Mars = being able to show outwardly, to show retaliation.** Many go on **strikes**; soldiers and police – for them **both inner and outer Mars** must be seen, because they need courage inside and outside.
> - One who can **show** can even **come to blows**, fight, pick up a stick and hit. One who can only see inside will only **feel** it.

## 2.4 Upper Mars (ucca / outer) – three states

### Over-developed upper Mars

- Very raised → **lots of anger.** You can say this by seeing the hand.
- Such anger may sometimes be **needed**: in a field such as **border duty**. E.g., **Sunny Deol** films – in a recent film (teacher saw it 3–4 days ago; name not remembered) he is shown uprooting a tree and a hand pump. An ordinary person cannot; his **upper Mars must be very highly developed**.
- Such people may include those who **commit murder, theft**. With **Venus in bad condition**, they could also be a **rapist**.
- **Context (deśa-kāla) of the client must be seen.** If the person is not in such a field, he will **let the anger out at home**, in **breaking things**. Such a person must be **given some activity – "go to the gym" or such work** – otherwise he will keep breaking things and show his retaliation always.

### Normal upper Mars

- Shows everything but **within a limit**: knows where to show anger; has **patience (dhairya)** and **courage (sāhasa)**.
- Understands **responsibility** – Mars gives responsibility. E.g., **soldiers on the border**: many places where no one even speaks to them; alone day and night for months; yet so much patience and they do their duty fully.
- Considered a **very good condition**, and such a man knows how to **control anger** – **provided you look at the thumb too**, because the thumb can itself become a cause of anger. If the thumb is good he controls anger and **keeps people around him safe**.
- **Generosity (udāratā):** Mars is called generous – he protects anyone, **not caring about his own life**, not seeing caste or religion.
- Conclusion: an **over-developed upper Mars is sometimes good, sometimes bad – depends on place/time/situation.** Normal: all the good qualities of Mars come.

### Low upper Mars

- The person becomes a **coward**; cannot slap anyone. (A slap may be wrong, but in places it is good – e.g., a child at home did wrong; you must scold, perhaps slap, snatch the wrong thing from his hand.) Such a person is so fearful that he **does not even have the capacity to speak.**
- On both sides of this Mars lie **Moon and Mercury**. Suppose **Mercury is in very good condition** (good lines, good finger): he is expert at speaking but only **shows** it; he lacks courage inside. For giving **speeches** Mercury is fine, but when it comes to **action** he cannot take action.

> [!EXAMPLE] Teacher's own hand
> - Her **outer Mars is fully depressed.** A baby lizard or cockroach comes into the house: she will not kill it; she asks her **mother-in-law** to kill it – "because her Mars is in a very good condition."

## 2.5 Lower Mars (nimna / inner) – states

- Reads the **enthusiasm (utsāha)**, courage and strength you **feel inside**.
- It is **near Venus**, so **desires** and also **affairs** are discussed from here. Some **lines or signs and symbols** (to be taught later) show **affairs** and the **inner mental state**.

> [!WARNING] Low / troubled lower Mars – signs
> - **Very many lines**, **dark** colour, or **bluish**, or slightly **reddish** → understand there may be **blood-related trouble**; possibly **low hemoglobin**.
> - Such a person can give **reasoning for everything** ("why like this? why like that?"); reasoning on every matter can become a problem.
> - **Confidence** can be low if the condition is not good; **enthusiasm** also lower.
> - Sometimes **difficulty in fulfilling physical needs** – seen from the **married life** perspective.

- **Excessively raised lower Mars:** can be both good and bad – a new condition. Desires increase in the mind, sometimes **unnecessary desires** (because it is near Venus).
- **Fully depressed:** not good – it spoils **health first**, and spoils the **mindset**. Desires become **of the wrong kind**. E.g., before a **competition/exam**, the feeling of fear comes; before the exam you may think "my exam is not going to go well; nothing will happen for me today."
