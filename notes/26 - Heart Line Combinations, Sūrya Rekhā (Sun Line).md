# 1. Opening – Semi-Simian and Simian Line (recap)

- The teacher was unsure whether the **simian / semi-simian line** had been taught in the previous class (students said yes); she said she would **repeat it once**, since it comes in the Heart Line combinations too.

> [!INFO] Simian vs semi-simian
> - **Simian line:** the **Heart Line and Brain Line become one line** (taught earlier).
> - When the two lines run **very close, touching, side by side** so that they **look as if the same line**, but are not one — if **branches** run across and **join** them, it becomes a **full simian line**. If **no branch joins** them, it is called a **semi-simian line**.
> - Note this small difference.

# 2. Remaining Heart Line Combinations

## 2.1 Branches of the Heart Line

> [!IMPORTANT] Direction of a Heart Line branch
> | Branch | Indication |
> |---|---|
> | **Branch from the Heart Line coming down and joining the Brain Line** | **Emotional disturbance** — some emotional turbulence in life. May be linked to **family**, or to one's **own inner feelings**; understand by **deśa-kāla-paristhiti**. Can be **heartbreak**, a heart-related problem, or a **breakup**. (Teacher was unsure if she had taught this before.) |
> | **Any downward branch** (in general) | **Family disturbance, breakup, emotional setback**. |
> | **Upward branches** | Check **which mount** each goes toward. They show **deep, hidden desires** in the mind — the kind of desire follows the mount it goes toward. |

- **Examples of upward-branch desires:**
  - Toward **Mercury** → desire to do **business** (whether circumstances allow and whether the person will do it is a separate choice).
  - The desire could relate to a **relationship**, **studies**, or **children** — "everyone's can be different."
- **Branches at the very end of the Heart Line** (last part): generally called the **extra-marital affair line**. **Caution:** these must be understood by deśa-kāla; they can also show **family disturbance** or an **inclination toward someone**. **Do not tell a client this bluntly.** Instead speak of it as the person's **nature** — e.g., "perhaps your nature is such that you like to live in such a way."
- **Multiple branches at the end of the Heart Line** (separate from the above) → **multiple interests**: in **later life** many interests may develop — travel, learning, cooking, etc.; everyone's hobbies differ.

# 3. Sūrya Rekhā (Sun Line)

## 3.1 Definition and nature

> [!INFO] Sun Line
> - **Any line that goes toward the Sun Mount is the Sun Line.** It can **start from anywhere**; it **ends on the Sun Mount**.
> - The **Sun Mount** is the mount below the **Sun finger (Apollo finger)**. Any line that comes there is called a Sun Line.
> - There can be **one or multiple** Sun Lines; it can be **straight, broken, or wavy** — any kind.

- **What it shows:** the Sun Line speaks of **soul satisfaction (ātma-santoṣa)**.
  - **Example (Teacher):** the career line is straight, the person works well, has a good job — but there is **no Sun Line, or it is very wavy**. Then it is often seen that the person **does not get the satisfaction he should from his work**, nor the **name and fame**; "he does it because everyone does", and a **lack of satisfaction** shows.
- **Most hands:** the Sun Line seen is **above the Heart Line**.

> [!IMPORTANT] Age on the Sun Line
> - The Sun Line has **no age of its own**. Events of the Sun are read on **other lines**; the best age calculation comes from the **Fate Line and Life Line**.
> - But it is said: **a Sun Line seen above the Heart Line** is of **age 50 and above**. After 50 ("when you have passed half your age") **name and fame come**.
> - Fame can come **through your own work**, or **through your children** — e.g., when your child clears an exam and the **parents' name is made bright**.

## 3.2 Where the Sun Line originates ("orientation") and what it means

### From the Moon Mount
- Soul satisfaction is linked to the **Moon**: name and fame come **through the Moon** — so: **a female's help; the support of the public (janatā).** You may be **very famous in your area among the public**; people may know you because of your work.
- **It need not connect to the Fate Line** — not at all necessary.
- **Mahābhāgya yoga (rare):**
  - The teacher compared it with astrology's *mahābhāgya yoga* — "when the 2nd, 6th, 8th, 12th are all empty."
  - **In palmistry:** if the **Life Line, Fate Line, Mercury Line and Sun Line all connect together**, it is called **mahābhāgya yoga** — "everything has mixed together": name and fame, a good career, a business mind — "you will have everything." Very good yoga; seen in **very few hands**.
- **If only from the Moon Mount** (no such connection): can show **family help**, **a particular female's help**, or **fame in the nearby area through creativity** (e.g., a very good **painter**).
- **If a branch leaves this same line toward Mercury** → it is a Mercury line, so the person can take the art in a **commercial** way: earn name and fame through the art and also use it commercially — e.g., **sell it on an online platform, make courses** (as many today teach painting, Sanskrit, etc.).
- Method stated: just see **where your line starts and where it goes**, and which line goes where; from there comes the name and fame.

### From the Venus Mount (very rare)
- The family has people with name and fame — e.g., a **doctor family** known across the whole state for generations; the person is known through that.
- Fame may come through **family, in-laws (sasurāl)** — after marriage you joined them and your name/fame joined — or **a woman's help**.
- Reason: **whatever Venus signifies, fame comes through that.**

### From the Mars side – **pūrva-puṇya rekhā**
- A line going **from Mars toward the Sun**: called **pūrva-puṇya rekhā**.
- People can be **very good researchers**, can **go to the army**; **creativity, success and courage** come together.
- Can also be **good comedians**; (a Sun Line from the **Moon** side can also give comedians). **Very talented** persons.

## 3.3 Quality of the Sun Line

> [!SUCCESS] Good conditions
> - **Straight and clean.**
> - **No enemy line from Mars should come across it.**
> - **Not wavy.** A wavy Sun Line is troublesome; it is also called a **segmented Sun Line** (the teacher said: if you read this name elsewhere do not be confused).
> - **Two or three Sun Lines** are good, provided Mars lines do not cut them and they are not wavy.
>   - Two Sun Lines can be read two ways: **two interests**, or they work as a **supportive line**.

> [!WARNING] Bad conditions
> | Condition | Meaning |
> |---|---|
> | **Sun Line that winds** (goes inward, then outward, again and again) | **Defame and fame, defame and fame** keep alternating; **the more it winds, the more trouble**. |
> | **Horizontal lines cutting a straight Sun Line** (not from Mars) | **General palmistry rule: vertical (upward) lines are good; horizontal / cutting lines are bad.** Each cut gives a **small defame** — "praise, then insult, then praise, then insult"; the person must **bear insult again and again**; life is not smooth. |
> | **Branches coming from below / slanting downward on the Sun Line** | **Very bad** — a **line of downfall (patana)**. The scriptures also say it **obstructs success**, and it is **not good for politics**. |
> | **Enemy lines crossing it** | All called **bad**; none is good. |
> | **Line from Mercury crossing and cutting the Sun Line** | **Defame because of a female or a relationship.** (Teacher: such a long line is seldom found since the marriage line is a small short line; she gave this "as a combination we carry".) |
> | **Line rising from the Heart Line and cutting the Sun Line** | Also trouble through **relationships**: **one's own people / close ones** may harm you ("heart = close ones"). **Care:** the line should not be one coming from behind, i.e., from **Mars**; "from Mars, we read it as from Mars, not from the heart". |
> | **Cross at the end of the Sun Line** | **Not good.** |

> [!FAQ] Q (student): I have **multiple Sun Lines** and a Mercury-side line cuts the Sun Line; it seems to reach there. (Four lines seen.)
> **A (Teacher):** If the **Mercury line cuts the Sun Line**, it is **not good** — it shows trouble because of a **female or a relationship**. *(She then added the Heart Line version above.)*

> [!FAQ] Q (student): Do Mercury lines coming and cutting also show **interrupted studies**?
> **A (Teacher):** **No.** Mercury does not show intermittent studies. **For that look at the Life Line** — if studies were intermittent.

### Sun Line combinations that increase fame
- **Branch from the Sun Line toward Mercury:** **name and fame together with business** — through your work, business, **writing**, **healing**, or anything Mercury-related, your studies. Can also be early: if a line goes there in **childhood**, a child who studies well and gets many prizes starts getting fame early; creative business; or **good communication** that makes people praise you. See the age by the Mercury sign.
- **Branch toward Saturn:** fame from **Saturn-related work**. It may **take time** because Saturn means **hard work**; Saturn work includes work with a lot of **physical labour**.
  - **Example (Teacher):** a person's father had **farming**; after age 50 his child took it up but **gave it a new form**, earned far more, built a house, educated children — as on **YouTube** thumbnails of "farm this much, earn this much." This kind of fame may come.
- **Branch toward Jupiter:** fame from **Jupiter's work** — studies, **guru**, **dharma-karma**, **social service**; or astrology, or the **medical line**, helping others, healing, treatment: name and fame across the whole area. Growth through **wisdom** and being known through it.
- **Positive signs** (triangle, trident, etc.) on the Sun Line are all read as **positive**.
  - **Triangle:** good.
  - **Trident at the end of the Sun Line:** **very good**. If the line goes along and **splits into two branches** — one to **Mercury** and one to **Saturn** → **name and fame in two ways**; you will have to carry **Mercury and Saturn together**.
  - **Distinguish:** a line that merely splits in two on the way is not the trident described; at the end it is.
  - **Trident on the Heart Line** (at its end): **as good a result**; can be found in the hands of good political leaders.
- **Cross at the Sun Line's end:** bad (see above).

# 7. Learning Summary

> [!SUMMARY]
> - **Heart Line branches:** down to the Brain Line = **emotional disturbance**; any downward branch = **family disturbance/breakup/setback**; upward = **hidden desires by mount**; many at the end = **multiple interests** later; branches at the end = **so-called extra-marital line — to be handled as "nature"**.
> - **Sun Line:** any line going to the Sun Mount; shows **soul satisfaction, name, fame**; above the Heart Line = **age 50+**; **its origin** (Moon = female/public/creativity; Venus = family/in-laws; Mars = pūrva-puṇya, researchers, army, comedians) and **its branches** (Mercury, Saturn, Jupiter) show **how** fame comes.
> - **Quality:** straight, clean, no Mars enemy lines, not wavy; **two or three Sun Lines good**; **cuts = defame**; **downward branches = downfall**; **trident/triangle = good**; **cross at end = bad**; **Mercury or Heart Line cutting it = trouble through relationships**.
> - **Hand-reading order shown:** hand type (palm shape, finger length) → marks → fingers and mounts → hand quality → lines → answer the client's question with reasons.
> - **Mole on Venus** = problems in the Venus area (kidney, comforts).
> - **Star on Moon Mount** = suicidal tendency (as recalled in this class).
