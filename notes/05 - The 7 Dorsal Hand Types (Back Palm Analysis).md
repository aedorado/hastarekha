# Back Palm Hand Types (Kiro's/Western Division)

> [!NOTE] Class Context
> Recap: Four hand types were already covered from the **front palm** perspective (Fire, Water, Airy, Earthy — good results come from studying these). Today's topic shifts to the **back palm** classification system, attributed to **Kiro**, also called the **Western division**. Some texts describe this classification with **8 types**, others with **11 types** — but to avoid confusion ("khichdi" in the mind), the teacher has simplified the teaching to a **practical set of 7 types**, which is sufficient to derive good results.

---

## Introduction to Back Palm Reading

[!WARNING] Common Difficulty
When students look at their own hand from the **back (posterior) view**, it is initially harder to interpret at first glance compared to the front palm.

[!IMPORTANT] Role of Nails
In back-palm reading, **nails** play a **major role** — an entire dedicated chapter on nails will be covered later. Nail variation is significant:
- Some nails are **wide and short**
- Some are **long and short**
- Some are **wide, large, and spread out**

These nail-shape variations, combined with **skin type** (soft vs. hard, as discussed previously), can significantly change the reading result even within hands that otherwise look similar.

[!CAUTION] Reading Principle
Speaker emphasized repeatedly: **No single hand-type reading should be taken as a final, standalone conclusion.** Every other factor (lines, mounts, finger proportions, skin, nails) must still be examined together for an accurate result. "Clear हुआ? है ना?" — confirmed understanding was checked with the class before proceeding.

## The Seven Back-Palm Hand Types (Overview)

1. **Elementary hand** (श्रमिक हाथ / प्रारंभिक हाथ)
2. **Spatulate hand** (चमचाकार हाथ)
3. **Square hand** (वर्गाकार हाथ)
4. **Conical hand**
5. **Philosophical / Naughty hand**
6. **Psychic hand**
7. **Mixed hand** (मिश्रित हाथ)

## Type 1 — Elementary Hand (श्रमिक हाथ / प्रारंभिक हाथ — "Labourer / Primary Hand")

### Physical Description

[!INFO] Definition
- Compared to other hand types, this hand appears **thicker/heavier** — a "bhaari-bharkam" hand, as opposed to a thin hand.
- Viewed from the back, the **palm portion looks prominent**, while the **fingers do not look as prominent**. The palm appears to "take up more space."
- The palm shape is **square** (not rectangular) — this can be verified visually in the reference picture.

[!TIP] Reading Technique — What Contributes to the Square Look
The square appearance is influenced by:
- The **Mars mount** area
- A specific part of the palm base
> [!EXAMPLE] Example: In a student's (Preetu's) hand photo, the **thumb** portion appeared to jut outward in a particular way — the teacher requested a second photo specifically to examine this detail, though it did not meet the full elementary-hand condition.

### Skin, Wrist, Thumb, and Nails

[!INFO] Physical Traits
- Skin is **generally hard/coarse (kathor)** — there are two sub-variants: one with **soft** skin, one with truly **hard** skin. The authentic elementary/labourer hand described here has **hard skin** (e.g., like a laborer's hands — rough, without the softness that comes from applying creams, etc., due to circumstances/manual work).
- The hand form is **square** and **stiff (kada/stiff hand)** — as opposed to a flexible hand that bends easily (illustrated with the example of a bangle sliding easily onto some hands and with difficulty onto others — an elementary hand's base structure resists easy movement, being thick and hard).
- The **wrist (kalāī)** is **wider/thicker** than a typical thin wrist.
- The **thumb is thick** (only referring to overall thickness here, not which specific phalange — phalange quality is a separate topic).
- **Nails are generally small.**

[!WARNING] Exception — Not Purely Dependent on Body Weight
A student asked whether this depends on body weight. **Answer:** Yes, it is considered in proportion to the body — all features (color, hand shape, etc.) should always be read relative to the individual's body proportions, not in isolation.

[!CAUTION] Exception — Small Nails Alone Are Not Sufficient
Small nails alone do not confirm an elementary hand — small nails can appear in other hand types too. **Example:** A student (Arvind) has small nails but does not trim/bite them; however, his hand overall was not assessed as an elementary hand by the teacher. **Rule:** Small nails are only significant as elementary-hand indicators when combined with the other conditions (hard skin, thick wrist, square stiff shape, thick thumb).

### Mentality and Character

[!WARNING] Trait — "Lower" Mentality (Not Necessarily Socioeconomic Class)
- The elementary hand is generally associated with a **"lower" category of mentality/thinking**, not necessarily a lower economic class.
- [!CAUTION] Exception: It is **not required** that this hand only appears among the poor or laborers — it can also be found among affluent, good people. It is specifically about a **mindset**, not wealth.
- It is, however, **more commonly seen** among people who do more **physical labor**, and **less common** among prosperous/well-off people.
- [!NOTE] Physical labor level does not depend on hand type alone — the **Mercury line** and **Jupiter mount**, along with other lines, must also be checked. Hand type tells you about the "ground" (soil quality) in which the "seed" (the lines) will grow — whether that ground is thick, stony, well-irrigated, fertile, etc.

[!INFO] Core Mentality Description
- Their thinking can be described as somewhat **"animalistic" (pāshavik)** in a simple sense: work, eat, sleep — with little concern for **tomorrow**, the **future**, or providing for **children/future generations**.
- This mindset is commonly seen among lower economic strata, but is **not exclusive** to laborers — it could be found in someone working at a nice place (e.g., a boutique) who has real skill but has no interest in advancing that skill or using it for growth.
- Even when offered advice or investment opportunities for a better future, such a person shows no interest — living only in the present ("ate, drank, slept today"), which **increases life's struggle**.

[!SUCCESS] Reliable Indicator — Hand Quality and Struggle Level
Speaker emphasized repeatedly: **the quality of the hand (especially hand type) can increase or decrease the level of struggle in life** — even with good lines, a harder/coarser hand type means results will come only after much more effort/hardship.

[!WARNING] Further Traits
- Lower-thinking mentality; described as people with limited aim/vision in life.
- **Lack of self-awareness about their own talent** — they don't recognize that a skill they possess could allow them to advance in the world; no "I can do this too" ambition.
- Low **comfort level** in life; little interest in personal advancement.
- **Low emotional appreciation/attachment**: e.g., if their own child brings a drawing/painting to show them, they will not praise it — a flat "okay, fine, no problem" response, then return to their routine of work/eat/sleep. This lack of appreciation is distinct from mere practicality (even practical people show attachment to the future) — the elementary-hand mentality specifically lacks forward-thinking ambition.
- **Not exclusive to men** — women can also have this thicker/heavier elementary hand type; in such cases, **emotional caring/sensitivity (caring attitude)** may also appear lower than typical.

## Type 2 — Spatulate Hand (चमचाकार हाथ — "Spoon-shaped Hand")

### Physical Description

[!INFO] Definition
- Named "spatulate/spoon-shaped" (चमचाकार) after the shape of a large ladle/spoon (not the kind used to serve dal, but a similarly curved, enlarged spoon shape).
- Visually **similar to the elementary hand**, without much apparent difference at first glance.

[!TIP] Reading Technique — Key Differences from Elementary Hand
| Feature | Elementary Hand | Spatulate Hand |
|---|---|---|
| Wrist | Thick/heavy | Thinner |
| Skin | Very hard | Less hard (softer) |
| Nails | Small | Not as small |

### Character and Abilities

[!SUCCESS] Reliable Indicator — Best Hand Category
The spatulate hand, along with the **Square hand**, is considered one of the **two best categories** of hand.

[!IMPORTANT] Key Distinction from Elementary Hand
- The elementary hand relies on **physical labor** without needing intellectual capacity.
- The spatulate hand person can do **both physical work and mental/intellectual work** — they can work hard with their body *and* with their intellect.
- They **recognize their own skill/talent** and understand how to refine and grow it.

[!EXAMPLE] Illustrative Example
Among laborers, some may possess fine hand-craft skill (intricate/delicate work) — such a worker may show the **spatulate hand**. This person recognizes their skill and actively thinks about how to improve it further, unlike the elementary-hand laborer who has no such awareness.

[!SUCCESS] Reliable Indicators — Growth Mindset
- Continuously learn new things — never satisfied with "I've conquered the world" after learning one skill.
- Know how to **make money from money**.
- Philosophy: **"Learn first, then earn."** They need to learn a skill before they can monetize it.
- [!NOTE] Distinction from Square hand: A **Square hand** person can potentially **earn without needing to learn first** (an innate/born quality); the **spatulate hand** person earns only **after** learning.
- Can appear in **any social class** — not limited to lower class; if found among laborers, they will typically be the most skilled/fine craftsmen among them.

[!INFO] Personality
- **Practical and hardworking**, but highly value their **independence** — cannot be "tied down."
- **Punctual and disciplined** — if their work hours end at 5:00 PM, they will leave right at 5:00 PM.
- [!NOTE] Discipline connects to the **thumb**, which represents **willpower**. Willpower and discipline are closely linked — setting a personal parameter/goal reflects willpower, which in turn builds discipline.
- Somewhat **talkative**.
- **Strong followers of customs/rules**: Example given — a person observing a fixed prayer time (e.g., Friday prayers in Islamic practice) will not extend work by even 10 minutes past that time; they will leave for the obligation, then return and do precise, careful work.
- Good understanding of **profit and loss**; can be considered **knowledgeable across many subjects**.

[!SUMMARY] Learning Summary
This is considered a very good/best hand overall.

## Type 3 — Square Hand (वर्गाकार हाथ)

### Two Ways to Read "Square"

[!TIP] Reading Technique
- **Option A**: Just the **palm** portion alone is square-shaped.
- **Option B**: The **entire hand** (palm + fingers together) forms an overall square "look" when viewed as a whole — even if strict measurement would technically classify it as rectangular, the overall visual impression/"look" of the hand is what determines this category.
- In some cases, the **nails** may also appear **squarish** in shape — but this is **subjective** and not universal (e.g., the teacher noted her own long fingers have very short nails).

[!NOTE] Small Nails — Dual Effect
Short/small nails can make a person more **investigative (khojī)** in nature — this can be **good in some contexts and unfavorable in others**; it can go either way depending on other factors.

### Character — "Business Hand"

[!INFO] Interpretation — "Business Hand" Category
This hand is called a **"business hand"** category — but this does **not** mean these people will only pursue business. It refers instead to a strong **understanding of transactions**: awareness of earnings, expenses, budgeting, and the ability to manage money well.

[!IMPORTANT] Key Distinction from Spatulate Hand
- **Spatulate hand**: learns a skill first, *then* earns money from it.
- **Square hand**: can potentially **earn without needing to learn** a skill first — this ability can be **innate/born-in** ("without learning they can earn").

[!SUCCESS] Reliable Indicators
- **Balanced personality**: possesses both **emotion** and **practicality**.
- Good **long-term planners** — natural business-minded planning ability, understanding their own strengths and weaknesses.
- Can be **strongly determined/resolute (dṛiḍh nishchay)**.

[!TIP] Reading Technique — Determination Depends on Thumb & Finger Inclination
Determination level also depends on the **thumb** and the **inclination/bending of the fingers** (to be studied further).

[!NOTE] Distinguishing Stubbornness from Determination
| Hand Type | Stubbornness (Jiddi) Level | Determination Level |
|---|---|---|
| Elementary hand | Highest | — |
| Spatulate hand | Lower than elementary | — |
| Square hand | Lower than both | Higher determination (dṛiḍh nishchay) than the others |

> [!CAUTION] Exception: Being determined/resolute does **not** mean being stubborn every time — these are related but distinct qualities.

[!SUCCESS] Further Traits
- Tend to follow **traditions**.
- Work for **social good** — through wealth (if prosperous), through new ideas, or through free service (e.g., free teaching) — in whatever way they can contribute.
- Display good **etiquette/manners (tehzeeb)** in how they speak, sit, and present themselves in front of others; know how to manage/handle people gracefully.
- [!CAUTION] Exception: The Square hand and Spatulate hand do **not** show the kind of ego seen occasionally in the **elementary hand** (e.g., feeling "why should I be the one to serve tea" is *not* a trait of these two hand types).
- Slightly **researcher-minded**, especially when combined with short nails (though short nails can affect health slightly, they can enhance this investigative quality).

[!SUMMARY] Learning Summary
Both the Spatulate hand and the Square hand are placed in the **best hand category**.

## Type 5 — Philosophical Hand / "Naughty Hand"

[!INFO] Alternate Names
This type is called both the **"Naughty hand"** and the **"Philosophical hand."**

### Physical Description

[!TIP] Reading Technique — Distinguishing from Conical Hand
- Visually **similar to the Conical hand** (both have thin fingers).
- Key distinguishing feature: the **knuckles/knots (gānthein)** at the finger joints are **raised/prominent** in this type, unlike the smooth, soft appearance of a conical hand (e.g., Preetu's hand, shown as an example of a hand with **no** prominent knuckles).

[!IMPORTANT] Which Fingers' Knuckles Are Prominent Matters
- Not all fingers need to show prominent knuckles — whichever fingers *do* show it will show the associated planetary quality **more strongly**.
- [!EXAMPLE] Example given: if the **Jupiter** and **Saturn** finger knuckles are prominent, the "naughty finger" quality will show up more strongly in those two areas, rather than in the **Sun** or **Mercury** fingers.

### Three Sub-Categories

[!INFO] Sub-Types
1. **Simple naughty finger** — just prominent knuckles, nothing further.
2. **Thin skin with visible veins/nerves** — skin is especially soft/thin, allowing veins to be visible from the back of the hand; this slightly changes the predictions.
3. **Crooked/zigzag shape** — the hand or fingers themselves appear bent/crooked (compared by the teacher to the mythological figure **Ashtavakra**, whose entire body was crooked). Whichever finger is crooked, the "crookedness" impact is centered on that finger's specific significations.

### Character

[!SUCCESS] Reliable Indicators
- Adds a **philosophical mindset** on top of the conical hand's baseline qualities — such people tend to bring **philosophy into everything** they discuss.
- [!EXAMPLE] The teacher gave a personal example: she notices she tends to bring philosophy into her own planetary teachings.
- Represents a **"darshanik" (philosophical) way of looking at things**.

[!NOTE] Occupational Correlation
- This hand type is commonly seen among **actors**, particularly those who do more meaningful, message-driven roles rather than mainstream/casual entertainment. A specific example (a now-deceased actor) was discussed by the class, though the exact identity was uncertain during the conversation. [unclear in transcript]
- Common among **stand-up comedians**, since their creativity is elevated (linked to the Moon's quality/philosophical inclination).
- Can become **motivational speakers** or **actors**.

[!WARNING] Emotional Traits
- Can be **very moody** and **very impulsive**.
- Can also be **quite stubborn**.
- [!TIP] Reading Technique: The **more crooked** the finger and the **softer** the skin, the **more moody** the person tends to be.

[!INFO] Comfort Preferences
- Tend to be **particular/fussy about comfort** — e.g., insisting on a specific plate to eat from.
- [!NOTE] Contrast with **elementary hand** people, who are undemanding and can eat comfortably off a simple leaf-plate without complaint.
- Prefer to keep to their own **limited, familiar space** and dislike being disturbed in it — e.g., insisting on a specific corner of a bed as "their spot."
- [!EXAMPLE] The teacher's own daughter shows a similar (though intensified) version of this trait — though her daughter's hand type is actually the **Psychic hand** (covered next), not this type; the underlying quality is shared but stronger in psychic hand.

### Career Implications

[!TIP] Reading Technique — Career Fit
- The more conical/philosophical the hand, the more likely the person may be suited to **healing professions**, the **medical field**, or **creative fields**.
- **Fingerprint analysis** and modern **DMIT tests** can also reveal a person's true career fit — many people leave established careers around **age 40–45** because that career was never truly their calling, only a compulsion/necessity for income.
- Fingerprint study is itself a **large, separate area of study**.

[!NOTE] Prominent Knuckles = Calmer, More Secretive
- People with more prominent knuckles in this hand type tend to be **calmer** and more **secretive**.
- [!CAUTION] Exception: The plain **conical hand** (without prominent knuckles) is **not** as calm or secretive as the philosophical/naughty hand type.

## Type 6 — Psychic Hand

[!INFO] Definition
An extension of the conical/philosophical hand's qualities, taken **further** — even softer and more delicate.

### Physical Description

[!EXAMPLE] Illustrative Comparison
- Compared to a **royal/princess-type hand**, or a **newborn baby's hand** — extremely soft, almost fragile, giving the impression that one should be careful even touching it (as if the fingers might be harmed).
- This exceptional **softness/delicacy** is the defining physical marker.

### Character

[!WARNING] Trait — Heightened Sensitivity
- The greater the softness, the greater the **sensitivity** — affecting nature, personality, and even **health**.
- Health tendencies: prone to **colds, coughs, congestion**, and **stomach upsets** more easily.
- [!EXAMPLE] Compared to the "**chhui-mui**" (touch-me-not) plant, whose leaves curl inward at the slightest touch — similarly, the smallest lack of praise/appreciation can spoil their mood.

[!IMPORTANT] Conditional Spiritual Potential
- **If knuckles (gānthein) are present** in this hand: the person may reach **very high spiritual/adhyātmik levels** — potentially a highly spiritual individual.
- **If knuckles are absent**: the person tends to prefer staying in their **comfort zone**, avoiding physical exertion, wanting things brought directly to them without needing to move.

### Line Quality Correlation

[!TIP] Reading Technique — Line Color and Progression
- Lines in this hand type tend to appear in a **lighter color**.
- [!NOTE] As one progresses from **Elementary → Spatulate → Square → Conical → Philosophical → Psychic**, line quality generally **improves**.
- [!WARNING] Caution: Even if a person's hand type is soft/refined, if the **line quality does not improve** (e.g., lines remain thick/coarse), the person's life struggle will **not** end just because the hand type looks soft — line thickness and color will be studied in more depth later.

### Preference for Routine

[!WARNING] Dislike of Change
- These individuals often prefer staying **within their comfort level** and can do the **same job for years** without wanting change.
- They benefit from being given a **fixed, particular job/routine** to follow.
- Even a small **change** to their routine can cause a strong reaction ("dimāg saatvein āsmān par pahunch jaana" — becoming extremely upset/agitated).
- They generally **do not want change** of any kind in their lives.

## Type 7 — Mixed Hand (मिश्रित हाथ)

### When to Use This Category

[!TIP] Reading Technique
This category is used when a hand does **not clearly fit** into any of the first six types — neither elementary, spatulate, square, conical, naughty, nor psychic.

[!TIP] Method — Finding the "Major Type"
1. Identify which of the six types the hand **most closely resembles** — this becomes the **major type**.
2. Note any deviations from that major type (e.g., a "major square" hand but with **short nails**, or with fingers that are unusually **long**).
3. **Add the qualities** of the secondary/deviating type onto the major type's baseline reading.

### Character

[!SUCCESS] Reliable Indicators
- Mixed-hand individuals tend to be able to **multitask**, since they carry the combined qualities of two or three hand types — they can be **practical**, **emotional**, and **creative** all at once.
- Career often shows **ups and downs**, reflecting internal indecision between different paths (since multiple hand-type qualities pull in different directions).
- Tend to be **full of ideas**.

[!TIP] Reading Technique — Best Confirmed via Lines
Predictions for mixed-hand individuals are made most reliably through detailed study of the **lines** on the hand.

## Fingertip Shape — A Related but Separate Topic

[!IMPORTANT] Advanced Concept
The teacher noted that **finger shape/fingertip type** is a **separate quality** from overall hand type, and can further modify a reading:
- Some fingers are **squared** at the tip.
- Some fingers are **rounded** at the tip.
- Some fingers show a **"bounce" or bulge** at the top — e.g., a bulbous shape like a "lattu" (spinning top) — as was noted earlier regarding **Gopal ji's small thumb**, which showed this bulbous top shape.

[!NOTE] Teaching Approach
The teacher explained that these individual qualities (hand type, finger shape, nail shape, knuckle prominence, etc.) are being taught **one at a time**, to be gradually **combined** later — once combined, hand-reading becomes much more enjoyable and precise.

## Photography Guidance — Additional Notes

> [!FAQ] Question (Student)
> In the mornings, I sometimes experience water retention, causing slight hand swelling that resolves later in the day — does this affect the photo I should submit?
>
> **Answer:** Yes — do **not** submit a hand photo while it is swollen, since a swollen hand will naturally look different/puffed. Wait until the hand returns to its **normal** state before taking the photo.

> [!FAQ] Question (Student)
> Why do you specifically ask us to remove nail polish before photographing our nails?
>
> **Answer:** Because nails contain **fine vertical and horizontal lines** that are diagnostically important and can only be properly seen **without nail polish** — best viewed in **good light**. These lines appear **on the surface of the nail itself**, not on the surrounding skin. Any nail polish — including a fully transparent/colorless one — should be removed, since even a transparent coating can obscure these fine lines. Photos should be taken in good lighting with polish fully removed for the lines to be visible.
