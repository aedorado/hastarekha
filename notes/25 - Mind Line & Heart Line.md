## 2. Combination: Heart Line and Mind Line touching / joined

> [!INFO] The combination
> Mind Line runs and **touches the Heart Line**, or a **branch from the Heart Line comes and joins the Mind Line**. Teacher: touching outright is rare; generally one sees a line with a branch coming off it.

- Meaning: the person is **caught between heart and mind**.
  - Heart = feelings, emotions.
  - Mind = thoughts, logic, reasoning.
  - Such a person tries to use both without merging them and so is always stuck between the two.
- Applies equally when a branch from the Heart Line joins the Mind Line.

> [!TIP] How to tell which line the branch comes from
> Look at the **flow** of the line: when you look closely, its direction shows whether it comes from the Heart Line or goes up from the Mind Line.

- Teacher noted several students may have this; she had seen it in one named student's hand the previous day (name **[unclear in transcript – "Amradha/Anuradha ji"]**).

## 3. The Heart Line – introduction

### 3.1 What the Heart Line is read for
1. **The heart as an organ** – heart health matters.
   - A negative sign on the Heart Line (cut, cross, island) → **heart problems or BP problems**, at some age.
2. **Emotions and sensitivity** – everything relating to relationships, work, and feelings toward others. Feelings can be good or bad: jealousy, love, etc. All kinds of feeling are read on the Heart Line.
3. **Relationship issues** (see 3.4).

> [!IMPORTANT] Balance of Heart Line and Mind Line
> - Both must be healthy. Strong Mind Line but bad Heart Line → wrong. Disturbed Mind Line but good Heart Line → also wrong.
> - **Balance of the two is essential.**

### 3.2 Quality of the line

| Feature | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Line **not too thick/dark** relative to skin colour | Ideal for balance |
| Very **dark/black** line on a **fair** skin | Not "good category". Puts **pressure** on the person (mental or emotional); indicates blood issues |
| Dark line on dark skin | Can be dark naturally (teacher: "my skin is somewhat dark, so lines can be dark") |
| **Yellow** colour on the line or in places along the Heart Line | **Blood-related** trouble |
| **Blue** colour on the line | Tell the person to get a **check-up** straight away; can show **blood infection** (teacher compared this to blue seen on the Venus mount) |
| **Zigzag** (up-down) line | Bad condition |
| **Broken, broken, broken** line | Bad condition (e.g. line goes, breaks, resumes, breaks again) – not a good Heart Line |

> [!WARNING] Breaks and islands
> - Each break or **gap** in the Heart Line, or any **island**, indicates **relationship issues** and **break-ups**.
> - Divorce is read in the marriage chapter: when divorce is discussed there, the Heart Line is consulted. All relationship issues must be checked on the Heart Line.

### 3.3 Mercury line (budha rekhā) and health – to be taught later
> [!NOTE] Teacher said the Mercury Line will be studied ahead; this is a preview in relation to the Heart Line.
- Budha rekhā starting **above the Heart Line** → generally indicates **business**.
- Budha rekhā starting **from the Heart Line / from here** (gesture) → does not mean no business indication, but it points to **health problems**; health problems are then more prominent. Where it starts above the Heart Line, the lean is toward business and there is less need to worry about health. **[Exact start positions were shown by gesture and are unclear in transcript.]**
- Many hands have **no** Mercury Line. **Absence of the line does not mean the person won't fall ill.** If the line **is** present, the **probability of illness is higher**.
- A Mercury Line makes the person: **business-minded**; awakens a **desire to study and read**; can make them a **healer**; but also **brings illness** along with it.

### 3.4 Poor Heart Line with relationship breakdowns
- Many break-ups / relationship issues ("made today, broken tomorrow", as with young people nowadays) → in such a case the **Mind Line must be very strong**.
- If the Mind Line has also gone toward the **Moon** and the **Moon mount is bad** → trouble.
- **Mars lines / enemy lines** from the lower Mars cutting the Heart Line or the Mind Line → disturbance. The whole **age patch** in which this occurs (e.g. teenage) shows a **disturbed mindset**; may go toward **depression**; **suicidal tendencies** are also possible – depends on the exact condition.

### 3.5 Mounts and fingers spanned by the Heart Line
- The Heart Line covers the area beneath four fingers/mounts – the teacher listed Mercury, Sun, Saturn, Jupiter – as if forming their **boundary**. **[Transcript is garbled here; listing as given.]**

## 4. Two basic types of Heart Line: straight vs curved

### 4.1 Straight Heart Line
> [!INFO] Meaning of "straight"
> Straight like a pen or a stick. Straight line = **stubborn**, firm on own point, **practical**; does what he has decided; may be **extrovert** (word in transcript **[unclear]**); "runs his own mind".

- A **slight bend** brings a touch of **emotion**: practicality is there, but the person thinks of emotions first. They do not remain "practical, practical" all the time.

> [!EXAMPLE] Teacher's mother-in-law (straight line)
> - **Who:** Teacher's mother-in-law (teacher will show the hand).
> - **Observed:** perfectly straight Heart Line.
> - **Interpretation / behaviour:** When teacher does tarot or anything similar, she says such things reveal nothing ("Not everyone's future is the same"; "don't be a fatalist; trust *karma*"); says the same of any pandit on TV. When asked to go out to feel better, she says "What is mind? The mind can settle at home too."
> - **Reasoning:** practicality of the straight line.
> - **Note:** Apply *deśa-kāla-paristhiti* (place, time, circumstance): a child or an elderly person will differ.

### 4.2 Where the straight line ends – effects
1. **Stops before the middle of Saturn, or at the middle of Saturn** (śani parvata):
   - Rough behaviour; little attachment.
   - Saturn gives dullness (*nīrasatā*), negativity, cold behaviour; **no great warmth in relationships**; the person cannot truly feel others' words, an inward-living person; emotional touch lacking.
2. **Reaches Saturn, or lies between Saturn and Jupiter, or goes up to/just past Jupiter:**
   - Slight change: becomes **calculative**; imagination can be good.
   - **At Saturn:** speaks only if there is some purpose; shows no interest in what others are saying unless it concerns him ("when it's about my matter, I'll listen"). Can be somewhat **selfish** (*matlabī*).
   - **Reaching Jupiter:** in a group/team discussion he gives his **views**, but in a practical (*vyavahārik*) way with no emotion.
     > [!EXAMPLE] Investment in a family business
     > Talk is about investing money in a business with family members. Person whose line is **curved upward** at its end may think, "It doesn't matter if my profit is a little less, they need it more." The **straight-line-to-Jupiter** person says: "Business should be business; keep work and relationships separate."
   - Because it is going toward **Jupiter (guru)** he will not be very hard on others; some **wisdom** also comes; guru-like qualities; **straightforward**.
3. **Straight line crosses all the way, right to the end, beyond Jupiter:**
   - Such a person has **no compassion or mercy**; a different kind of person.
   - May become **highly spiritual** *if* the Jupiter mount is good, or a **cross** appears on Jupiter, or a **fish sign** appears on the **Ketu parvata**.
   - In relationships may go into a **"no physical zone"** – no concept of intimacy.
   - Reading this line also concerns **married life**.

> [!EXAMPLE] Married-life illustration (straight lines)
> A man takes his wife on the back of a motorcycle chatting along (creating a romantic atmosphere) vs. a man who says "I don't want such display outside." The straight, practical person will **not show love or romance to the outside world**.
> **Rule:** the **straighter** the line, the **more practical** the person.

### 4.3 Special case: very short line stopping at Mercury (Budha)
- Mind Line / Heart Line this short are rarely seen.
- Described as **"alpāyu" (short life) [word in transcript: "alpāyu wālā yogī" – unclear]**, **sickly** (*rogī*), and **selfish** – "you cannot trust such a person".

## 6. Curved Heart Line

### 6.1 Basics
- Most hands (the class's hands) have **slightly curved** rather than straight lines.
- The **degree of curve** matters (sudden curve vs very light curve).
- A line with only a light bend is **not "so straightforward"** → **balanced personality**: emotions and practicality both, carried together.

### 6.2 Ending at Jupiter or between Jupiter and [Saturn]
- Reaches **Jupiter**, or lies between Jupiter and "Guru" (teacher's words; likely Jupiter–Saturn **[unclear in transcript]**), i.e. between the two phalanges of the finger → **balanced personality**.
- This formation is also called the **Scorpio / scorpion tail** (already learned in the Mind Line).
- Such people are **emotional but somewhat dominating**; they run things their way. Teacher: "Don't deny it, whoever has it." May end anywhere in this stretch.
- Emotion rules a bit more than practicality, but they are dominating.

### 6.3 When emotion rules more – role of the Mind Line
- If the **Mind Line rises up and meets the Heart Line**, the thoughts go toward the heart → person is **more emotional** than someone whose Mind Line is **perfectly straight toward Mars**.
- If, in this emotional condition, a **line from the heart joins the Mind Line** → can be **cheated and made a fool of** because of emotions.

> [!TIP] When others can fool you more
> If the **Mercury finger is short** (limited power to think and understand; one accepts what the other says) **and the thumb** (will power) is also weak → easier to be fooled. Teacher: "keep combining combinations."

### 6.4 Twist: the Heart Line flowing into the middle of Jupiter
- A line that runs and then goes into the **middle of Jupiter**: if someone asks "Will my lover be true?", teacher says you can answer: "No **[teacher's words are contradictory – says "absolutely not… your lover will be absolutely true" – unclear in transcript]**."
- **Condition:** there must be no downward **branches** from the line, because those may indicate **extramarital affairs**.
- Teacher: such branches are in many students' hands, but "we're not the same extramarital"; sometimes there are **health conditions** and many other reasons.

### 6.5 Curve crossing Jupiter (or a branch crossing Jupiter)
- The curve crosses Jupiter, or **a branch** branches off and crosses Jupiter.
- People become **extremists** – they want to do things apart from society; they fix an **aim** (*uddeśya*) from childhood and **will fulfil it**; highly **ambitious**; may even go to a "life-or-death" extent to fulfil it.
- **Do not say this to anyone** unless other things are checked, especially the **thumb quality**.

> [!CAUTION] Checks before concluding
> - **Jupiter finger tilted** (toward Saturn) → "broke the world's regulation" (as stated; meaning **[unclear in transcript]**).
> - **Sun finger slightly down** → may not be a strong personality.
> - **Mars pressed down** → do not say it.
> - **Mars mount bulging / protruding outward** → more **anger**, so the extremist feeling can turn **negative**; check Mars when it goes negative.
> - If **Jupiter and the Jupiter finger are good and the thumb condition is good** → it will **not** go negative; it will go toward the positive side.

### 6.6 Curve toward Saturn
- Curved Heart Line goes toward Saturn; may end anywhere in the stretch between two points shown by gesture, or a branch goes toward it **[exact span unclear in transcript]**.
- In love, such persons use **intellect (buddhi)** – this means **physical love**.
- **Emotional love** is present when the line is **more toward Jupiter (guru)**: the more it goes toward Jupiter, the more **spiritual and emotional** the love; you understand the other's feelings and give them **independence**.
- The more it comes to **Saturn**, the more "like **Saturn in the fifth house**": **only physical love**, no feeling of love.
- **Branch to Saturn + main line toward Jupiter:** you think of physical love but also love from the heart; feelings are both negative and positive; **plays both roles**; such persons can be **somewhat romantic**.

### 6.7 Curve ending toward the Sun
- First condition: such people can be **unwell**. A **short Heart Line** going toward the Sun → indication of **illness**. (Teacher said "the Heart Line is going toward buddhi" **[unclear in transcript]**.)
- May have a bit of **anger**; not very **lovable/romantic**; somewhat **self-centred**; possibly **less expressive**.
- Being less expressive is also a **Saturn** feature. For lines going toward Saturn, also check whether the person **hits or abuses** the partner.

### 6.8 Heart Line drooping downward
- The more the Heart Line **bends downward**, the more **negativity** comes in – shown whichever mount it goes toward.
- Shows an attitude of **despair (nirāśā)** toward everything: relationships, career, personal growth.

## 9. Quick-reference summary

> [!SUMMARY] Heart Line – key learning points
> 1. Heart Line and Mind Line must be balanced; a joined/touching pair → caught between heart and mind.
> 2. Quality: not too dark for the skin; yellow → blood problems; blue → check-up/infection; zigzag/broken/gaps/islands → relationship issues and breakups; Mars/enemy lines cutting → disturbance, depression, suicidal tendency possible.
> 3. **Straight** = stubborn, practical; **curved** = emotional + practical, balanced.
> 4. Straight line endpoints: before/at middle of Saturn → cold, rough; at Saturn → calculative, selfish; to Jupiter → practical, wise, straightforward; beyond Jupiter → no compassion, possible spirituality or no-intimacy.
> 5. Curved: to Jupiter/Jupiter–Saturn → balanced, scorpion tail, dominating; crossing Jupiter → extremist, ambitious (verify thumb, Mars, finger); toward Saturn → physical love; toward Jupiter → spiritual/emotional love; toward Sun → ill, self-centred, less expressive.
> 6. Line from Heart Line down to Mind Line → easily cheated; add short Mercury finger + weak thumb → more so.
> 7. Drooping Heart Line → negativity and despair in everything.
