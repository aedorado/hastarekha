# 2. Signs & Symbols – General Rules

> [!IMPORTANT] Rules for reading signs and symbols
> 1. **Where to look:** signs and symbols are seen **on the palm, not on the fingers or the thumb**. They will not be clearly visible there and there is no need to look there. They are seen on the **mounts** and on the **lines (rekhā)**.
> 2. **No prediction from a sign alone.** A sign/symbol by itself gives no prediction. To predict, the **age on the lines must be calculated** — each line has its own age scale (to be taught) — to find *at what time* the event happened.
> 3. **Clarity and size:** consider how clearly and how big the sign appears. Some signs are very faint; they may even fade by the time the event happens. **Such faint minor signs are not taken.**
> 4. **Not everyone has every sign.** Do not hunt for them. When the lines are studied and the relevant age is reached, note it if it is visible; if not, fine. There are many signs, not all show in every hand, and some are too fine to see. **Do not search forcefully** 
> 5. **How signs act:** a **positive sign** on a mount/line **increases** that mount's/line's positive qualities; a **negative sign** **reduces** the quality of the mount/line.
> 8. **Mole (Til) anywhere on the palm** — in the old practice, closing the fist and seeing Til come out was said to mean money, "or whatever they used to say". **Teacher: that is not so.** Til on the palm counts as a **negative sign**.

> [!FAQ] Q: Will the Til give a health issue or a wealth issue too?
> **A (Teacher):** It gives a **health issue**, first of all. It has **nothing to do with wealth**. For wealth, one indication was taught on the nails: the **white dot** (the "three-month" concept taught earlier, which came true for Jīvesh ji at that time).

# 3. The Signs (in the order taught)

## 3.1 Spot / Dot

- **Size:** very small (so small it may not even be visible), or larger; can also be bigger and of other shapes.
- **Colour matters:**

| Colour | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **White** | **Not a permanent mark.** White spots on the hand can be due to **digestion** or **sleep trouble** (when sleep is not complete they commonly show on the hands). Can be left out of consideration. |
| **Black, blue, red** (any of these) | **Considered.** |

- A **dot** (a coloured spot) is a **negative sign**; it generally indicates **ill health** (bad health condition). **Which age** it is at and **which mount** it is on decides the related health issues.

![Dot vs. Circle - Dot is Negative, Circle is Positive](/lecture-notes-images/19/01-dot-vs-circle.png)


> [!INFO] Dot vs Circle
> - A **dot** is filled/solid. If it is entirely filled in, it could be a **mole (til), or a deep mark**, which are treated as a dot → **negative**.
> - A **circle** is **empty inside** (not filled). A circle is a **positive sign**.
> - Teacher: **leave the circle out of the "dot" rule** — the dot rule is negative, the circle is positive.

> [!SUCCESS] Circle on the Sun Mount
> A circle on the **Sun Mount** is considered **very good**. It is a **rare sign** — seen very rarely, but a very good sign.

> [!FAQ] Q: The circle you described is positive — is it formed by lines, or is it a spot-like circle?
> **A (Teacher):** How can a circle be formed by lines? — We will show it on one hand shortly; it will become clear. *(Deferred to the hand demonstration; see §5. In the demonstration the teacher said a certain mark was **not** "that circle" — see §6.3.)*

## 3.2 Cross

- Shape: two lines crossing, a distinct mark.

> [!WARNING] What is NOT a cross
> - Lines merely **crossing each other on their paths** do not count as a cross — e.g., the **Fate Line (bhāgya rekhā)** crossing the **Heart Line**, or the **Mind Line** crossing another line, or an **enemy line** crossing a line making a crossing shape.
> - A true cross appears **separately** on the hand, **very clearly**; students will see it easily when taught further.

- A cross is a **negative sign** in the hand. **The only place where a cross is positive is the Jupiter Mount.**
- **Cross on Jupiter Mount:** good — good for **married life** and good for **education**.
- *Most students should have this cross on Jupiter*
- A cross can show **disease, some kind of trouble, or an accident**.

## 3.3 Star (nakṣatra cihna)

![Cross & Star](/lecture-notes-images/19/02-cross-and-star.png)

- A **cross filled in further** — more crossing lines (cross, cross, cross…) — is called a **nakṣatra cihna (Star)**.
- The more crossings, the **more negative** it becomes.
Indications:
  - an **accident**;
  - **love affairs**, specifically in **teenage**, if something of that sort happened;
  - **health is bad**, for sure.
- **Cross/star on Mars Mount, especially Lower Mars** (the part near the thumb) — seen there most often.

## 3.4 Island

![Island & Square Formation](/lecture-notes-images/19/03-island-and-square-formations.png)

- **Where it appears:** on the **lines** (**not on mounts**).
- **Size:** may be small or large.
- **Shapes described:**
  - A line (e.g., the Life Line) running along, with a **small bulge/space in the middle** — the empty space with a slight swelling is an island.
  - A **large** island of the same type.
  - A small island formed "on top of" a running line; a large one formed on top of it.
  - One line running straight and **another line coming** and forming an island with it.
- **Meaning:**
  - The **distance (length) over which the island extends** → when timing is calculated, it causes trouble in life **for that many years**.
  - Trouble relates to **the line it is on**: could be **health** or **career**.
  - Overall, it shows the **struggle of life** — the teacher linked it to the **6th, 8th and 12th bhāva** of the horoscope, which show life's struggle.
- Islands are generally **very clear and easy to find**: wherever there is a problem in someone's life, an island will be seen.

> [!NOTE] The "habituation" observation about large islands
> Sometimes the island is very large. If a life pattern has run for **10–15 years** the person becomes **used to it**, and when something becomes a habit it **no longer feels like trouble**.
> - Analogy (Teacher): if my voicr  were bad and my voice stayed the same for years, I'd say "this is normal".
> - Consequence for reading: when you ask a client about the trouble indicated at that place, some clients may say **"this is normal"** — meaning they have become used to living in that island.

> [!FAQ] Q: If the island is formed on the Life Line on the **inside** rather than outside, is it still an island?
> **A (Teacher):** Yes, **from whichever side, it is an island.**
>
> **Exception (Teacher):** At the **start of the Life Line near the thumb**, small **bunches/clusters (gucche)** are seen. These are **not islands**. If it is one, it is an island; but if it is **in a series**, it is **not** an island — because generally everyone's hand shows these there.

> [!FAQ] Q (Anuradha ji, student): "I have many islands" — on her **Heart Line, Mind Line, Fate Line**, at different time slots; she feels the teacher's remark about getting used to living with trouble applies to her: the islands "have become like that".
> **A (Teacher):** Yes, they become like that — if very many come every few days, one becomes used to it. 

> [!FAQ] Q: Does the island form on lines, or on mounts too?
> **A (Teacher):** On **lines**. You will see it only on lines.

## 3.5 Square

- Must be an **individual square**. If it appears only by lines joining/crossing each other, **do not count it**.
- Can be **anywhere**: on the **outer** side or the **inner** side of a line (e.g., Life Line); on an island or **inside an island**.
- Called the **sign of protection**.
  - Meaning: trouble is going on (e.g., an island on the line), but a square on it means **you will find a solution** and **get out of the trouble**.
- Also called a **property sign** — **a sign of property purchase**. (There are **two** signs of property purchase; this is one of them.)
- **Square on Jupiter Mount:** can make a **good teacher**. This does **not** mean a job as a teacher; it shows a **capacity to teach/guide others**, even if one teaches at home, neighbours, children — anyone.
- Reference: **Aravind ji's hand** showed a very clear, big property sign. (Notes: Session 18)

# 5. Jīvana Rekhā (Life Line)

## 5.1 Rules before reading any line

> [!IMPORTANT] Quality of a line – rules to fix **before reading any line**
> | Line condition | Verdict |
> |---|---|
> | **Very light** (many soft hands have very faint lines) | **Not good** |
> | **Very dark / blackish, very thick, flat and spread** ("takes space", looks as if filled in) | **Not good** — called a **bleeding line** |
> | **Broken** (goes, breaks, goes again, breaks again…) | **Not good** — called a **breaking line** |
> | **Normal line** | The line should be normal. |
>
> - Too thick, too black, too light and broken lines — **results are not good.**
> - **Even if the quality of the hand is excellent**, if the line is bad, **the result is not good**.
> - **A negative sign on the line**, however good the line's quality, gives **some negative result in that time period**. It may be related to anything, depending on **deśa–kāla–paristhiti (place, time and circumstance)**.

## 5.2 Names of the Life Line

- The Life Line is **jīvana rekhā**. Other names: **prāṇa rekhā, āyu rekhā, pitṛ rekhā, gotra rekhā, śakti rekhā** …

## 5.3 What the Life Line is NOT / what it shows

> [!WARNING] Common mistake: "Life Line = only age"
> - **Remove the notion** that the Life Line is merely a line for calculating age. Age may be calculated from it, **but she will not say 100%**; **it alone does not determine longevity (āyu)**.
> - Students often get frightened: "my Life Line is very short, so my life will be short" — **this is not so at all**.

- **The absence of a Life Line, or more than one Life Line** (there can be two) — and a very short one — **will not affect longevity**. It **will affect immunity** — shows the **immune system**.
- A **short Life Line** *can* indicate:
  - **major transformation** (a major change in life) **at the time period where the line stops**;
  - **problems in childbirth**;
  - **problems in health**.
- Along with it, the **Brain Line** and **Heart Line** must also be checked (heart and brain are needed for staying alive) — "we will see all these conditions."

> [!INFO] What the Life Line shows
> It shows **major events of life** — the **flow and direction of life**, a **road map**. It gives a *dāyarā* (sphere/range) and a direction of life; all the results are within that direction.
> - **Analogy (horoscope):** in astrology, for a given lagna we see which **daśā** comes, then the next, then the next — a flow. We look at the **mahādaśā**, and **by its influence the antardaśās give all the results**. If the mahādaśā says "you have to win at Ludo", you will win at Ludo, **not at chess**.
> - So the Life Line prepares the **road map**.

## 5.4 Origin and the early "chains" (childhood)

- **Origin:** from near the **thumb**. The starting point can be higher or lower — up to the **Jupiter Mount** — depending on the hand.
- **At the start** many **chains/clusters** are seen. Understand them as **childhood**: the first ~**10–15 years** (*"10–15 years of childhood is struggle-time for all"* and, later, **"from the start up to at most 15 years; 10 or 15 years"**).
  - What happens at this age: **studies, friends (winning and losing), health problems**. Each of these becomes the main focus, and a child will feel problems in exactly these areas.
- **If a hand shows** many clusters **and** dots **and** signs **and** many lines going from **Lower Mars** cutting across (these are called **enemy lines**), then: *"you extended a hand to do any work, then trouble came"* — and it is judged by what the child's life holds at that age (studies, health, friends).
- The **Life Line also speaks about the father**: whatever area has an **island, trouble, smoothness, a thin or light line** — it tells about the **father** just as it tells about the person.

> [!IMPORTANT] Dominant (destiny) hand vs active hand — clusters at the start
> - You may **not** see the clusters/chains at the start in your **destiny hand**, yet see them in your **active hand**.
> - Understanding: **at birth** your immunity and vitality were very good, but **you were not looked after fully** — food, upbringing not taken care of. So due to **causes you created yourself/environment** your health had problems. "What God gave was right."

# 6. Life Line – Venus Space and Expansion

## 6.1 How much space Venus is given (reading the starting position)

- Previously the Venus Mount's states were taught — **raised, low (nimna), normal**. More important now: **how much space Venus gets**.

![Venus Spacing As Per Life Line Starting Points & Variations](/lecture-notes-images/19/05-venus-spacing-life-line-start-points.png)

- Above photograph: **3–4 lines** — one starts **right next to the thumb**, another starts **a little further away**, the next even further; the lines' distance goes on increasing.
- **Method:** first check **where the Life Line starts** and **where it goes**. *"How much space did my Venus get?"*

> [!INFO] Meaning of Venus's space
> - Space decides the **comfort of life**.
> - A Venus mount may be good, raised, active and clean, but with a **small range**. A small range means that **even with comforts you cannot enjoy them**. Example: the house has everything, the room has an AC, a car, money — but day and night there is the same running around as in a normal person's life — so what is the use? You cannot enjoy life as others of your age can.
> - **The wider the Venus range, the more it shows the person's kindness (dayālutā)**: nature becomes **humble**; and **big thinking** — *"I want to do something further; I won't live like a frog in a well like everybody else; I want to go out and see the outside world."*
> - It **also indicates travel**: as the range grows, **smoothness in life** grows, mentality changes, you enjoy life and want to do work outside your circle.

## 6.2 Life Line reaching the Moon Mount / a branch going there

![Life Line To Moon Mount](/lecture-notes-images/19/06-life-line-to-moon-mount.png)

> [!INFO] Life Line reaching the Moon Mount
> - If the **Life Line itself** goes to the Moon Mount, **or a branch from the Life Line** goes there, it shows:
>   - **foreign settlement** (a home of your own abroad — "we are settled there"), **or**
>   - **a great deal of travel**. *(Two conditions.)*
> - **Branch from the Life Line:** the Life Line remains the **third** line, but a branch leaves it towards the Moon Mount. **At the age at which the branch leaves**, **expansion starts** in life.
> - "Expansion" can mean: **shifting** from the birthplace/where you always lived to elsewhere; or **extending your business** — and now, since going abroad is not required (work can be **online**), adapt by **deśa-kāla-paristhiti**: one may be at home doing an online business, but **travel when needed**; the residence could also be abroad.
>
> **Reading method – which line is darker?** Between the **main inner line** and the **outer line (branch)**, see which is **darker**. **The darker line plays the major event in life.**
> - If the **inner line is slightly darker**: the **inclination is more towards the motherland** — **you may roam the world but peace will be at your own home.**
> - If the **outer line is slightly darker** and the **inner line goes down but fades** (not visible): expansion may be **away from the homeland** — you may go out of your homeland, your circle, "people like you".
> - **This is one example; many variations will come.**

# 7. Life Line – Forks, Setbacks, Branches

## 7.1 Fork (small and large) at the end or start

![Small Fork At End of Life Line](/lecture-notes-images/19/09-life-line-small-fork-at-end.png)
![Larker Fork At End of Life Line](/lecture-notes-images/19/10-life-line-larger-fork-at-end.png)

- A line running to the end where a **small fork** forms. Called a **fork line**; can appear **at the end or at the start**.
- **Small fork** (outward-going line): shows **travel, family trips** — because **there is little expansion**; the size varies, can be very small. **The smaller the fork, the more it indicates family trips** — going out of the comfort zone for a short time.
  - At the very end: like grown-up children saying "let's go out", **or** a child who settled abroad/elsewhere and the parents **visit** them. Whether their mind settles there depends on other conditions: if **Venus's range is small**, the mind may not settle there and they come back home.
- **Larger fork:** a bit larger — shows **two directions** in life: **life splits into two parts after this age**.
  - Two homes — you look after both; work spread at two places (e.g., one settled place and a second home with work elsewhere).
  - Can be about **work, residence, or children**.
  - This is generally **later age: after 50**; at that time we want things to settle, and the **steps follow the family, especially the children**.
- **General rule (simple formula):** **any line going outward from the Life Line shows expansion**; and **at the age where it joins the Life Line**, speak of expansion.

> [!FAQ] Q : If the fork at the end of the Life Line closes like an island, is it still a fork?
> **A:** If it forms like an island it is **considered an island**. **Only if it is open is it a fork.**

## 7.2 Sudden inward turn / break – "drastic change"

![Larger Fork At End of Life Line](/lecture-notes-images/19/11-life-line-sudden-curve.png)

- Sometimes an island is not clearly visible. Two conditions:
  1. The Life Line runs, then **suddenly turns inward**.
  2. The Life Line runs, turns inward suddenly, and **a line continues from there but is not joined**.
- Both mean a **sudden drastic change**, not good: it can lead to **depression**; it may be **financial loss**, **loss of a dear one**, or **major health conditions**. *(The teacher said she would show a hand.)*
- Reading: going straight inward = at that age, a **major setback** in life. **How life is afterwards** is shown by **how the remaining Life Line runs**:
  - If the remaining line is **broken/broken-broken** — "not very good", you will manage life.
  - If the later life is **smooth and expands outward** — what happened happened; **afterwards life becomes better and better.**
- Distinguishing: with this condition, life continues, a major setback came, **but you did not lose courage** — you carry on despite it. *"Understood the difference between the two?"* — we must see **how to give the result**.
- A line that comes **inward** generally also shows **financial loss (dhana hāni)** — as in the hand where a line turned inward and was strongly a financial-loss indicator; a hand to be shown "also had financial loss". 

## 7.3 Many lines at the very end of the Life Line

![Life Line Various Patterns](/lecture-notes-images/19/12-life-line-various-patterns.png)

- A **cluster** of lines emerging at the very end (3–4 or more). The age is around **60–70** (to be calculated per hand).
- Shows **multitasking**: even at that age there is **no peace**; running about, perhaps responsibilities.
- Shows **low vitality** — **energy is divided in all four directions** (doing this, that, that).
- **Not good for health**, but you **remain active**.
- If a **mole (til)** also comes there, **health will be said to be bad at that time**.

## 7.4 Branches going inward vs outward from the Life Line

> [!WARNING] Do not confuse with vertical lines
> In the area between the thumb and the Venus Mount, **vertical lines** are seen that may **cross the Life Line or touch it**. **These are not the branches meant here.** Only lines **emerging from the Life Line itself** are meant.

| Direction of branch | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Inward** (toward Venus) | **Responsibility** — an **extra burden or responsibility chosen by your own will**. Shows **financial loss** too (see below). |
| **Outward** (away) | **Expansion** — travel, small trips, **pleasure**. Can also show that **you yourself are dependent on someone**. |

- **Inward-branch meaning (responsibility):** e.g., taking on your mother-in-law's care; taking all of children's work until they grow; a **house loan**; supporting your sisters from part of your salary (a **liability** you can't leave even if you wanted). **Chosen willingly**, whether happily or however — you carry it along. The responsibility is a **burden to you**; some people don't *feel* the responsibility as a burden, but in the hands of those who do, these lines will be seen.

## 7.5 Four conditions of the Life Line's course

1. **Straight, smooth line** — life runs as it runs; wherever an **island or star** comes in between, trouble there.
2. **Line runs, a major break arrives (e.g. at age 30), then the line starts again** — until that age, life was smooth; then a major shock. The nature of the shock must be checked on the **Heart Line** and **Brain Line** (e.g., divorce-like situation, breakup, career setback after thirty).
   - If the line is **not visible in the middle** but a **square** is seen there, or the line is very faint ("almost nothing"): there was trouble, **but you got a way out** — **"square means someone protected you / helped you."**
   - **How life continues** depends on the line afterwards: **good later line → good later life; bad later line → trouble may not leave you for life.** (If the later line is bad but the person stays good, the person can come out of it — as stated.)
3. **Overlapping line / preparatory line:** one line runs straight and another runs alongside it **with no break** — they **overlap**.
   - **Analogy:** I am teaching palmistry now, but I am preparing what I will bring **next year** — one work is on, and **before it ends the preparation of the next life has begun**.
   - **Example:** Someone bought and set up a house elsewhere, rents out the present place, **will move in 4 years**, set up the business there, children will study there. In the in-between time **you have not disturbed anyone**, but you **prepared**. Hence it is called a **preparatory line**; growth/expansion comes from the preparation.
4. **Direction decides it:** if the overlapping line is on the **inner side** → "you are going back to your roots" — may be a return to the **old way** of working (people returning to the old era, their roots). If the line is on the **outer side** → you move to the **new era, new cities, good-growth areas**. So **"when will life become better than now"** depends on whether the preparation line goes outward or inward.

# 9. Learning Summary

> [!SUMMARY]
> - **Signs are read on mounts and lines only; a sign alone never predicts — it needs the age on the line.** Faint signs are ignored.
> - **Positive:** circle (empty inside; especially on the Sun Mount), cross on the Jupiter Mount, square (protection, property purchase; on Jupiter = teaching capacity).
> - **Negative:** dot (coloured, filled; white is ignored), cross elsewhere, star (more negative; Lower Mars → accident, teenage love affair, health), island (struggle for as many years as its length; on the lines only), til on the palm (health).
> - **Life Line:** line quality first (not too light/too dark-bleeding/broken). It is a **road map**, **not an age-meter**; short or absent line does not shorten life but affects **immunity**.
> - **Venus's space** = comfort and breadth of thinking; **line going outward = expansion**, inward = responsibility/financial loss.
> - **Moon Mount / Sun area / Saturn reach** of the line → settlement abroad, name and fame, or work-related travel only.
> - **Fork:** open = fork (small = family trips; large = life splits in two, later age); closed = island.
> - **Supportive line (far from the Life Line)** = support; **parasite line (fine and very near)** = a termite-like troublemaker.
